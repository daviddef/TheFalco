# Forchia birth registers, 1883–1915 — read end to end (26 volumes, 1,021 images), 26 October 2026

Antenati, Archivio di Stato di Benevento, *Stato civile italiano*, Forchia, *Nati*: 1883–1896, 1898–1904, 1910 and 1912–1915 (the Antenati listing has no volume for 1897, 1905–1909 or 1911, and nothing after 1915). Five delegated readers looked at every image; the Falco, Annecchino and Carfora acts are in `private/reads/forchia/` (not in the repository). Re-opened by the archive's own hand: the three Falco margins below.

**The result is a nil, with its reasoning:** *no child named Falco was born at Forchia in any of these 26 volumes, and no Falco is a father, mother, declarant, midwife or witness in any act.* The runs are unbroken (act numbers 1..N in each) and each annual index agrees with the acts. The word «Falco» appears only as the place name *Masseria Falco* (an address in 1884, 1889, 1891, 1892, 1894, 1914). **So the children of Carmine Antonio Falco and Filomena Annecchino were not born at Forchia in these years** — either they were born before 1883 (at Arienzo), after 1915 (Forchia's births 1916 onward are not on Antenati), or elsewhere.

Three marriages of FALCO spouses appear in the margins of Forchia birth acts, all of people born 1896–1901 and all married in the 1920s:

No kinship is claimed. A Falco man and two Falco women marrying Forchia people in 1922–25 is the first sign this archive has of a Falco presence at Forchia before Carmine Antonio's.

**Annecchino and Carfora** are in every volume (children, mothers, witnesses); Annecchino Raffaele was the town's civil officer in 1886–87. The births of Annecchino Antonio (6 Sep 1883, of Carmine and Maria Giovanna Crisci) and Giacinta (18 Sep 1883, of Carmine and Maria Carfora), Agata (1884, of Carmine and Maria Carfora), Giacomo (1887 and 1901, of Carmine and Maria Carfora) are in the readers' files.
