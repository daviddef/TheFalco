## Falco appearances (all at 3300 px)
1. **Act 10 (img8L), 28 Jan 1855, born 28 Jan 16h: ANDREANA FALCO**. Father Luigi FALCO, 32, contadino, "figlio di Vincenzo", strada Capodiconca; mother Carolina Cimmino; father declares ("da lui dichiarante"), cannot write; witnesses Pasquale Montesarchio (muratore, Terra Murata), Pietro Grazia (Porta di sopra). Parish column: Andreana Falco. No margin seen. CONFIRMED.
2. **Act 69 (img37R), 16 Aug 1855, born 15 Aug 2h: COSTANZA FALCO**. Father Angelo FALCO, 28, "industriante", mother Maria Cimmino, 32, strada Santa Lucia; declarant Rosa Morgillo, levatrice (50); witnesses Giovanni Diglio, Angelo Majone (Camellara). No margin. CONFIRMED.
3. **Act 78 (img42L), 16 Sep 1855, born 16 Sep 7h: ANGELO FALCO**. Father Giuseppe FALCO, 29, colono, "figlio di Vincenzo", strada Camellara; mother Rosa Limbardi (read Limbardi/Zimbardi), 25; father declares; witnesses Antonio Diglio, Angelo Majone (Camellara). No margin. CONFIRMED. (Luigi and Giuseppe both 'son of Vincenzo' -- brothers; ages 32 / 29.)
4. **Act 109 (img57R), 12 Dec 1855, born 12 Dec: Domenico MAURO**, son of Antonio Mauro 30 fruttajolo (strada Croce) and **Sara FALCO** 26, "sua moglie legittima"; father declares; margin: Domenico Mauro m. 6 Dec 1887 (n.32) Angela di Lucia. FALCO MOTHER, CONFIRMED.
5. **Act 111 (img58R), 15 Dec 1855, born 14 Dec: LUCIA FALCO**. Father Crescenzo FALCO, 35, contadino, strada Porta di sopra (own house); mother Maria Morgillo; midwife Rosa Balletta; witnesses Pasquale Montesarchio, Pasquale Limbardo (Terra Murata). Margin: Falco Lucia m. Clemente Porrino 30 Sep 1888 (n.21). CONFIRMED.
6. **Act 82 (img44L), 28 Sep 1855: WITNESS Crescenzo FALCO**, colono, strada Sant'Andrea (other witness Vincenzo Orologio, pettinatore). Child Maria Giovanna de Ciero, son/dau of Alessandro de Ciero 30 ferraro and Elena Croisi 31, strada Croce. CONFIRMED.
7. Margins only: act 42 (img24L) Nicoletta Nuzzo m. Giuseppe FALCO 13 Apr 1876 (Caserta 5 Jun 1884); act 67 (img36R) Maria Clementina Gaetana Crispo m.
