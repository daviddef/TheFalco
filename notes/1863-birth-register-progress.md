## What is proven
Every image that carries acts was rendered (full opening, ~2,300 px wide, both pages) and every act was read; the act numbers form an unbroken run (see counts). The act-by-act roster is in 1863.tsv. Every Falco/near-Falco was re-rendered at 3000-3400 px (the Antenati limit per leaf is ~3200-3400) and transcribed. In these Kingdom-of-Italy forms each page holds ONE complete act with both parents named on the same page and the declarant and two witnesses all named on the same page, so one full-page read covers child, father, mother, declarant, midwife and witnesses (no two-band problem as in the 1830s).

## Limitations
- Ages, given names and many surnames in the TSV were read at survey resolution (~1,100 px per page) and are approximate ('?' marks the doubtful ones); only the Falco acts and the near-misses were re-read at 3,000-3,400 px. The TSV is a roster for finding surnames, not a transcription to cite.
- A Falco WITNESS can be missed more easily than a parent; witnesses were read on every act, and Falco witnesses were found (so the pass was reading them), but no independent second reading was made.
- Marginal notes later than the act (marriages, sentences) were recorded where legible; most are dated after 1880.
