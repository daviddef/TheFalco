## Why this matters more than a child's death act usually would

**THE FATHER DECLARED IT HIMSELF.** Carminantonio Falco walked into the Casa Comunale of Forchia at
half past eight on the morning of 2 August 1937 and reported the death of his nine-month-old son.

1. **IT IS THE FIRST DOCUMENT THIS ARCHIVE HAS EVER FOUND THAT NAMES FILOMENA ANNECCHINO.** Until
   today her existence rested entirely on the family tree — the open-questions page still says her
   birth record «was not found» and that the descent is *inferred*. **Here she is, in a civil act, in
   her own household: «ANNECCHINO FILOMENA, casalinga, domiciliata in Forchia».**
2. **CARMINANTONIO IS THIRTY-SIX in August 1937 → born about 1901.** The archive has his birth as
   **11 January 1901 at Arienzo**. **Independently corroborated for the first time.**
3. **A CHILD THE ARCHIVE HAD ONLY FROM THE TREE IS NOW DOCUMENTED.** **ANIELLO FALCO**, born at
   Forchia about **November 1936**, died there **2 August 1937**, aged nine months.
4. **AN ADDRESS.** The household lived at **Contrada CASE SPARSE, number 68, Forchia** — the first
   Italian address the archive has for this generation.
5. **A TRADE.** Carminantonio was a **contadino**, like every Falco before him back to Matteo.

## Two leads that are NOT claimed

- **MARIA ANNUNZIATA FALCO**, born Forchia, **died Forchia 31 July 1938** (`an_ud2010050`). The index
  gives her father as **«ANTONIO Falco»** and her mother as **«FILOMENA ANNECCHINO»**. That is either
  **another child of this household**, with the father's name written short, or **a different couple
  of similar name**. **The act itself has not been read.** This archive has been caught before
  treating a name plus a date as an identification.
- **RAFFAELE FALCO — marriage at Forchia, 28 October 1937** (`an_ud2008887`, volume `an_ua2008823`),
  **born at ARPAIA**. Carminantonio had a brother Gerardo born at Arpaia in 1913, and the family's
  drift ran Arienzo → Arpaia → Forchia. **Whose son this Raffaele is has not been established.**

## AND THE DOOR IT OPENS

This was found through **Antenati's NAME index**, `/search-nominative/?cognome=X&localita=Y&s_page=N`
— which returns the person, **their parents**, the act type and the date, and links to the volume.
For **Forchia** it holds **237 Annecchino, 261 Carfora and 31 Falco** records, spanning **1861 to
1942**, including **55 Annecchino death acts and 10 marriages**.

**This archive had never used it on the Benevento *Stato civile italiano* series, because it did not
know that series was on Antenati at all.** It is, and it is indexed by name.
