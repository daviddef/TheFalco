# Arienzo civil death register 1831 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14333` (Arienzo, Morti 1831), 62 images. Read in full.
**Acts:** 13 January – 31 December 1831. Image 61 is the closing certification of the Procuratore del Re.
**Duplicate bindings:** 12≡11, 17≡16, 24≡23, 32≡31, 39≡38, 43≡42, 47≡46, 56≡55.

**Administrative note.** These acts give Arienzo's district as **Nola**; the 1832 acts onward give
**Caserta**. The district appears to have been redrawn between the two volumes. Observed in the
registers, not yet verified against an administrative source.

## The one Falco death — and the record that changes the archive

### Anna Falco, thirteen months — image 19 (right), page 67, act no. 33, 4 May 1831

> …avanti di noi **Andrea Sorice, secondo eletto**… sono comparsi **Andrea Crisci**, di anni
> cinquantotto, contadino, domiciliato ivi, **strada Camellara**, *vicino della defunta*, e
> **VINCENZO FALCO**, di anni **trentotto**, contadino, domiciliato ivi, strada Camellara,
> ***parente della defunta***, i quali han dichiarato, che nel giorno quattro del mese di Maggio,
> anno corrente, alle ore tredici, è morta nella sua propria casa **ANNA FALCO, di mesi tredici**,
> nata in Arienzo, **figlia di MICHELANGELO FALCO**, contadino, domiciliato ivi, **e di
> MARIANTONIA VIGLIOTTA**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/anna-falco-death-1831.jpg`. Full discussion in
`notes/1831-vincenzo-parente.md`.

**The clerk distinguished the two declarants: one *vicino*, a neighbour; the other *parente*, a
kinsman.** Vincenzo Falco, thirty-eight in 1831, is generation three of this line — born 1793, son of
Pasquale and Chiara Rivetti. So a sworn civil act records that **the direct line and Michelangelo
Falco's household are related.** It does not say how. *Parente* covers brother, cousin, uncle, nephew.

Also new: **Mariantonia Vigliotta**, Michelangelo's wife. And Michelangelo and Mariantonia used the
name **Anna** twice — this child, dead at thirteen months in 1831, and another Anna dead at eight days
in March 1834.

## Two more Falco, alive, as declarants
- **Vincenzo Falco**, 38, *contadino*, strada Camellara (act 33, above).
- **Giuseppe Falco**, 46, *colono*, strada Camellara — declaring the death of Alessandra Crisci,
  six months, on 20 July (image 30, left). Born about 1785. Whether he is the Giuseppe Falco who
  married Antonia di Guida — a son of Matteo, and therefore Pasquale's brother — is **not
  established**; the age is compatible and nothing more.

That makes **six Falco householders** now attested on strada Camellara across 1831–36: Pasquale,
Vincenzo, Michele, Michelangelo, Francesco and Giuseppe.

## The street, in one year
Strada Camellara appears again and again as the address of the declarants: Crisci (Andrea, Clemente,
Pasquale, Simone, Alessandra), Lettieri (Carmine, Carmina), Martone (Lazzaro, Marta di Cesare, Teresa
aged 100), Rivetti (Paolo), Carfora, Melillo, Cimmino, Cioffi. It is a working-class quarter and the
same few families keep burying each other's children.

## Also of note
- **Benedetto Itri, 70, a *mendico* — a beggar — born at Arpaia**, died in the hospital at Arienzo,
  27 March. The earliest sighting of Arpaia in these registers.
- **Teresa Martone, aged one hundred**, widow of Nicola Crisci, died 16 September, reported by her son.
- A dense parallel gentry: Don Vincenzo Vetrone (*canonico*), Don Giovanni Migliore (*sacerdote*),
  Donna Michele Cioffi and Donna Costanza de Luptiis (both *monache*, both in their twenties),
  Donna Mariantonia de Luptiis, and Donna Maria Giuseppa Scoppa, a *gentildonna* born at Naples.
