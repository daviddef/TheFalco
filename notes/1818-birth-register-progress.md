# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1818 — sweep in progress

**Volume:** `an_ua14415` (Arienzo, Nati 1818), **85 images**.
**Why this volume:** it should hold the civil birth act of **Raffaele Falco, generation 4** — known
so far only from a parish baptism — and of **Maria Rosa Falco**, daughter of the Michele Falco of
strada Camellara, who died aged sixteen in May 1834.

## The volume map (worked out, and reusable)
Antenati's Arienzo birth registers run **one ark per year from `an_ua14406` = 1809**, incrementing by
one. So **year Y = an_ua(14406 + Y − 1809)**: 1818 = 14415, 1830 = 14427, 1832 = 14429,
1834 = 14431, 1835 = 14432. Thirty-four volumes, 1809–1842.

## Why birth acts are worth more than death acts here
A Neapolitan birth act names **the father with his age, trade and street, and the mother by name** —
so each one dates the father's own birth to within a year or two. That is exactly what is needed to
place the strada Camellara Falco:

| Household | What a birth act would settle | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Michelangelo Falco & Mariantonia Vigliotta | his age → his birth year | 1830 (Anna, b. c. Apr 1830) |
| Francesco Falco & Raffaela Falco | his age → his birth year | 1832 (Carmina, b. c. Jun 1832) |
| Michele Falco | his age → his birth year | 1818 (Maria Rosa, b. c. 1818) |
| Vincenzo Falco & Andreana Crisci | **Raffaele, generation 4** | 1818 |

## Crop calibration for birth acts
Different layout from the death registers. The opening — *«è comparso [FATHER], di anni [AGE], di
professione [TRADE], domiciliato … strada [STREET]»* — sits higher on the page and the two pages of a
spread are offset:
- **left act** `pct:22,16,30,11`
- **right act** `pct:50,16,32,11`
- render width **1350**, six pages (twelve acts) per strip.

## The volume's structure — two copies
This volume holds **two copies of the 1818 births**. Images 2–13 are one copy (folios 113–120);
images 17 onward are the second, which starts again at folio 126 and runs the whole year to about
act 95 near image 70, with blank forms and a ministerial insert of 10 January 1818 scattered through.
The acts read at images 17–28 duplicate those at 2–7 exactly, which is how the duplication was
spotted. **Read the second copy** — it is the complete one.

## FOUND: Raffaele Falco, generation 4 — image 41 (right), act 47, folio 149

> Numero d'ordine **quarantasette**, Foglio 149. L'anno mille ottocento **diciotto**, a' dì **dieci**
> del mese di **Maggio**, ad ore **venti**, avanti di noi **Salvatore Scarpati, secondo eletto** ed
> ufiziale dello Stato Civile del Comune di **Cerqua d'Arienzo**, Provincia di Terra di Lavoro — è
> comparso **VINCENZO FALCO**, di anni **ventisette**, di professione **contadino**, domiciliato in
> detto comune, **strada Camellara**, ed ha dichiarato che il giorno sopraindicato ad ore **quindici**
> è nato nella sua propria casa, da lui dichiarante e da **ANDREANA CRISCI sua moglie legittima,
> d'anni venti**, un maschio che ci ha presentato, **a cui si è dato il nome di RAFFAELE**.
> La presentazione si è fatta alla presenza di **Domenico Crisci**…

Plate: `site/public/plates/raffaele-falco-birth-1818.jpg`

**What it adds to a generation known only as "1818" from a parish baptism:**
- **Born 10 May 1818 at three in the afternoon**, in his father's own house.
- **On strada Camellara** — the family street, now confirmed at a birth as well as at deaths.
- **Vincenzo was 27**, so born about 1791. His marriage act of December 1814 called him 21 (b. 1793)
  and his 1831 declaration 38 (b. 1793). The ages wobble by two years; the 1814 and 1831 statements
  agree with each other and are preferred.
- **Andreana Crisci was 20**, so born about 1798 — she had married in December 1814 at about sixteen.
  This is the first record to give her an age at all.

## FOUND: Giuseppe Falco of strada Camellara — image 46 (right), act 57, foglio 154

> …è comparso **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **trentasei**, di professione **contadino**, domiciliato in
> questo comune, **strada Camellara**, ed ha dichiarato che il giorno sopraindicato ad ore quindici è
> nata nella sua propria casa, da lui dichiarante, e da **ANTONIA DI GUIDA sua moglie legittima,
> d'anni trenta**, una femina che ci ha presentata, **a cui si è dato il nome di MADDALENA**.
> — 7 June 1818

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-birth-1818.jpg`

**This is the most structurally important find of the volume.** The wife is the identification:
the parish marriage record names *«Josephum Falco filium Matthei, e Franciscae Crisci»* marrying
**Antonia di Guida**. So this Giuseppe is **a documented son of Matteo Falco and Francesca Crisci —
Pasquale's own brother — and he is living on strada Camellara.**

Four weeks earlier, on the same street, his nephew Raffaele was born to his brother's son Vincenzo.

**What strada Camellara now is.** Not merely "the Falco street" but demonstrably **Matteo Falco's
family's street**, across two generations and several siblings:

| Who | Relation to the line | On the street |
|---|---|---|
| **Giuseppe Falco** & Antonia di Guida | **son of Matteo** — Pasquale's brother | June 1818 |
| **Rosa Falco** & Carlantonio Morgillo | **daughter of Matteo** — Pasquale's sister | died there 1830 |
| **Pasquale Falco** & Chiara Rivetti | **son of Matteo** — the line | died there 1834 |
| **Vincenzo Falco** & Andreana Crisci | Pasquale's son | May 1818, and throughout |
| Michele, Michelangelo, Francesco Falco | **not established** | 1831–36 |

Three of Matteo's children are now placed on one short street. That does not prove Michele,
Michelangelo and Francesco are his too — but it makes the 1831 act, in which Vincenzo calls
Michelangelo's daughter kin, much easier to read.

Also new: **Antonia di Guida was 30** in June 1818, so born about 1788; and Giuseppe at 36 was born
about 1782, which sits neatly between Matteo's known children Joachim (1779) and Rosa (1786).

who died aged sixteen in May 1834 and so was born about 1818 — her act would give Michele's age and

**Maria Rosa Falco has NOT been found.** She was sixteen when she died in May 1834, so born about
1817–18; she is not in the 1818 acts read so far, which makes **1817 (`an_ua14414`)** the next volume
