# MOIANO, OPENED — 525 records, and two clean negatives

**14 September 2026.** Moiano has **557 registers** on Antenati and this archive had **never examined
it**, though its own notes called it «the second stronghold of both Zampiello and Annecchino». It is
**name-indexed**, so it gives parents.

## What is there

| surname | records | span | births / deaths / marriages | households |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ANNECCHINO** | **215** | 1861–1942 | 158 / 48 / 12 | **15** |
| **FALCO** | **310** | 1861–1942 | 205 / 98 / 13 | **15** |

## Negative 1 — the Moiano ANNECCHINO are not the Forchia Annecchino

Their fifteen households marry **Cuozzo, Saccone, Morzillo, Di Stasi, Cieri, Mauriello, Perna**.
**Not one Carfora. Not one Crisci** — the two surnames that define the Forchia Annecchino.

Moiano's own **Filomena Annecchino** married a **Ruggiero** and bore children from **1879** — a
generation too early, and the wrong family.

**So the hypothesis this archive raised against itself — that Filomena Annecchino was not born at
Forchia at all — does not hold here.** Carmine Annecchino × Maria Carfora of Forchia remain the best
fit for her parents, and her missing birth act is still unexplained.

## Negative 2 — the Moiano FALCO are a separate stock

Fifteen households: **Filippo × Francesca Massaro · Gennaro × Antonia Cesare · Carmine × Mariantonia
Amoriello · Francesco × Annunciata Amoriello · Pietro × Angela Buonanno · Giambattista × Mariagrazia
Morgillo · Bernardo × Giovanna D'Angelo · Gennaro × Filomena Cesare · Pasquale × Rosa Ciambrello ·
Vincenzo × Agostina Izzo · Pasquale × Antonia Diodato** and others.

**Against the line's own surnames — Arricale, Maione, Morgillo, Crisci, Annecchino, Zampiello — there
is exactly ONE overlap**: *Giambattista Falco × Mariagrazia Morgillo*, and **Morgillo is ubiquitous in
the Valle Caudina**. That is not a connection; it is the local surname pool.

**No kinship to this line is claimed for any Moiano Falco.** The 310 records are published in the
register so that the next Falco found there can be checked against them rather than guessed at.

## And a holdings fact worth more than either

**Arienzo's separate DEATH-INDEX volumes run 1809–1849 and stop.** There are **39** of them (missing
**1836** and **1848**), mapped for the first time: **1829 = `an_ua14291`, +1 a year to 1849 =
`an_ua14309`.**

**After 1849 there are no index volumes at all** — so from 1850 the year's *tavola alfabetica*, where
it exists, is **bound into the register itself**. **1859 has one at the front; 1860 does not**, and
ends at act 102 with no table anywhere. **That is why the post-1858 sweep has to be done volume by
volume, and why 1859 took one page while 1860 will take sixty.**
