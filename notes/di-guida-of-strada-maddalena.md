# The di Guida of strada Maddalena — generation three's wife, proven

**14 September 2026.** Antonia di Guida, wife of Giuseppe Falco, now has a birth date, two parents,
nine siblings and a house. Found in the same pass that read Maddalena Falco's baptism; see
[maddalena-falco-1818.md](maddalena-falco-1818.md) for the chain that led here.

## Antonia — READ FROM THE IMAGE

Ark `3Q9M-CSD3-13K9-C`, Arienzo parish register, Sant'Andrea Apostolo. Margin «**Antonia di Guida**».

> «Anno D̄ni Millesimo Septingentesimo **octogesimo tertio** 178[3], die vero **trigesimaprima
> 31 Octobris**, Adm R̄dus D̄nus Can. D. **Joseph Migliore** Substitutus baptizavit infantem **natam**
> ex **Josepho di Guida, et Magdalena Rivetta** conjugibus hujus Parochiae, quae nominata est
> **ANTONIA**. Matrina fuit **Prudentia Papa**.» — Antonius Archipr. Romano

So she married at **eighteen** (4 Aug 1802) and was **thirty-four**, not «*d'anni trenta*», when
Maddalena was born in June 1818. The archive's «born about 1788» was five years late and rested on a
clerk's guess — the same clerks who gave Giuseppe Falco as 32, 37, 36, 43, 42, 42 and 40 across nine
years.

## What proves it

**ANGELO ANDREA.** Baptised 29 Nov 1791 to Giuseppe di Guida and Magdalena Rivetta. Dead at Arienzo on
**21 October 1817**, husband of Teresa Vigliotta, his civil act reading «*figlio di **Giuseppe di
Guida** e **Michedalena Rivetti***». Two independent series, one household, the same son — and he was
about twenty-five, which the act (giving no age) does not contradict.

That also disposes of «Michedalena». The archive had read it as a Campanian blend of Michela and
Maddalena on the strength of the scribe's line-break «e Miche-/dalena». **The parish wrote *Magdalena*
for forty years.** It was the civil clerk's, not the family's.

## The control — stated because it is real

Arienzo holds a **second** di Guida–Rivetta couple: **Filippo di Guida × Margherita Rivetta**, whose
daughter **Vittoria** was baptised **15 February 1779** (`3Q9M-CSD3-138Q-4` — the same page as
Gioacchino Falco's baptism). Two di Guida men married two Rivetta women in one parish. **The forenames
separate them.** The caution this archive carried before the baptisms were found was not misplaced,
and it is not being retrospectively tidied away.

## The godmother followed the bride

Matrina at the di Guida baptisms: **Prudenzia Papa** through 1787, then **Fortunata Villano** from
1789. Fortunata Villano is the matrina at **Giovanna Falco's baptism in 1805** and **Michele Angelo
Antonio Falco's in 1808** — Antonia's own children, in her husband's house. Godparent choice is one of
the few things in these registers that tracks real association rather than administrative convenience.

## Method

A full-text sweep of `M9J1-7MY` (`q.anyPlace=Arienzo`, `q.text=Guida`) returned **1,349 results,
1,298 distinct pages**. Then a regex across the machine transcripts for any *di Guida* standing within
170 characters of a Rivetta-shaped surname — `Rivet|Riveta|Rivera|Pivera|Riuet`, because the engine
cannot hold that word steady. **Ten acts fell out of 1,298 pages.** One image read fixed the year the
machine could not render.

**The deliberately loose regex is the point.** Searching for «Rivetta» alone would have missed
«Riverra», «Riveta» and «Riuetta», which is how these acts stayed unfound. See the corrections page on
PIVERA.
