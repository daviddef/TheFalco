# GENERATION FOUR'S MARRIAGE — 5 March 1844, Sant'Agnese, Arienzo

**Film 007786833, image 1884**, parish marriage register of **Sant'Agnese, Terra d'Arienzo**.
**Read from the image, 13 September 2026.** Margin: «**Raphael Falco cum Angela Rosa Morgillo**».

> «**Anno Domini 1844, die vero 5 Mensis Martii**, servatis omnibus de jure servandis, coram me
> infrascripto **Parocho S. AGNETIS TERRAE ARGENTII**, et coram testibus **[E]usebio Lettieri filio
> Clementis**, **Angelo di Marzo filio Francisci**, … matrimonium contraxerunt **in faciem Ecclesiae
> per verba de praesenti**: **RAPHAEL FALCO filius VINCENTII et ANDREANAE CRISCI**, *Archipresbyteralis
> Ecclesiae S. ANDREAE Apostoli*, et **mea Parochiana ANGELA ROSA MORGILLO filia HORATII et VINCENTIAE
> PESCE**. In quorum fidem — **Josaphat Ruggiero Parochus**.»

## What it gives

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | **5 March 1844** — the archive had no date at all |
| **Parish** | **Sant'Agnese, Arienzo**. The groom belongs to the archpriest's church of **Sant'Andrea Apostolo**; the bride is the celebrant's own parishioner — «**mea Parochiana**» |
| **Groom's parents** | **VINCENZO FALCO and ANDREANA CRISCI** — generation three, confirmed in the parish's own hand |
| **Bride's parents** | **ORAZIO MORGILLO and VINCENZA PESCE** — confirmed, and the pair were first found only days ago in Angela Rosa's 1818 San Felice birth act |
| **Witnesses** | **[E]usebio Lettieri son of Clemente**, **Angelo di Marzo son of Francesco** |
| **Celebrant** | **Josaphat Ruggiero, Parochus** |

**It also closes a question the archive opened, closed wrongly, and reopened in a single day.** The
index leaf was published as Arienzo's, then "corrected" to San Felice a Cancello's on the strength of
FamilySearch's film label, then reopened when the register that follows it turned out to say
Sant'Agnese. **The act settles it: ARIENZO.** The first answer was right. See `/corrections`.

## And a second Falco marriage on the facing page

> «Anno Domini **1844**, die vero **1° Julii** … in Matrimonium copulavi … non dum uxoratos
> **DOMINICUM FALCO filium JOSEPHI et GELSOMINAE VIGLIOTTA**, *Parochialis Ecclesiae S. Mariae ad
> Nives*, et **MARIAM ANTONIAM FRASCA filiam JOSEPHI et LUCIAE PASSARIELLO** … praesentibus testibus
> **Paschale Cioffi filio Dominici** et **Raphaele P[ezz]a filio Lucae**.»

**DOMENICO FALCO is a son of the OTHER Giuseppe Falco** — the one of **strada Costa**, married to
**GELSOMINA VIGLIOTTA**, whom the archive separated from the founder's son by street and wife. His
parish is **Santa Maria ad Nives**, a third Arienzo parish. Married **1 July 1844**, four months after
Raffaele.

> **Three parishes in Arienzo are now in play**: the collegiate **Sant'Andrea Apostolo**,
> **Sant'Agnese**, and **Santa Maria ad Nives**. Which one a family belongs to is evidence, and this
> archive has not been using it.

## Method note

The volume is reached by **walking the film**: the viewer's filmstrip carries an image-number → ark
map in the DOM, so any image can be composited from its DeepZoom tiles. The index leaf naming
Raffaele is **image 1873**; the register begins at **1880**; the act is at **1884**. See
`familysearch-image-reading` in memory.
