## Why it matters

**1. Matteo Falco and Francesca Crisci are confirmed a second time, from a second child.**
Pasquale's own death act of 1834 names them. This names them again, four years earlier, on the death
of a *different* child. Two civil acts, two siblings, the same parents. The head of this line is now
about as firmly attested as a peasant couple of the 1760s can be.

**2. Rosa Falco is Pasquale's sister**, and she can be matched to the parish register: Matteo and
Francesca baptised a daughter **Rosa on 5 January 1786**. That gives her forty-four in October 1830
against the forty-two stated here — the usual slop. The identification is strong but rests on the
match; the act itself does not give her a birth date.

**3. The Falco and the Morgillo married twice, two generations apart.** Rosa Falco married
**Carlantonio Morgillo** in about 1810. Two generations later **Raffaele Falco**, generation four of
this line, married **Angela Rosa Morgillo**. And the Morgillo were on the same street: Carlantonio's
son **Raffaele Morgillo** died on strada Camellara in May 1832 aged twenty-two; **Salvatore Morgillo**,
eighty-seven, a *falegname*, died there in November 1830; **Anna Morgillo**, eighty-two, widow of
Francesco Crisci, died there that same month. The family Raffaele Falco married into were his
great-aunt's people and his neighbours.

**4. It puts a daughter of Matteo Falco on strada Camellara** — the first of that generation placed
on the street by name.

## The street in 1830
Falco (Rosa), Morgillo (Salvatore, Anna, and Carlantonio's household), Crisci (Giuseppe *calzolajo*,
Maria, Pellegrino Giuseppe, Marcello), Perrucci, Torrino (Clemente, Mariantonia), Lettieri (Aniello,
Vincenzo), Martone (Agostino, Nicoletta), Carfora, Manna, delle Cave, Giglio (Pasquale, 90), Maione
(Costanza).

## Also of note
- **Giuliano Alati, 77, a Capuchin lay brother born at Sora**, died in the Convento dei Cappuccini.
- **Andrea Maria Angelo Ruggiero**, forty-six days old, a *gentiluomo* **born at Arpaia**, son of
  Don Lorenzo Ruggiero.
- A death at the **Ospedale degli Incurabili in Naples** transcribed back into the Arienzo register —
  so this series can catch Arienzo people who died away from home.
- **Aniello Attarelli buried three children in three weeks** in January and February: Tomaso, ten;
  Angelo, five; Carmine, three.
- **Luigi Rubino**, five months, died in the *fermata di Gendarmeria* — the gendarmerie post.
