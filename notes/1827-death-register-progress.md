# Arienzo civil death register 1827 — sweep complete

> **CORRECTION (published, not swapped).** Where this file reads **«Cossi»** for the household
> of **Francesco Cossi and Maria Falco of strada Camellara**, and for the **Cesare** and
> **Marcello** who declare with Giuseppe Falco on that street, the register actually reads
> **CIOFFI**. The 1825 volume distinguishes the two families in one hand: *Cioffi* with a
> dotted «i» after the C, *Cossi* — of strada Cossi — without. See
> `site/public/plates/cioffi-not-cossi-1825.jpg`. The text below is left as it was read.

**Volume:** `an_ua14329` (Arienzo, Morti 1827), 42 images. **Read in full, act by act.**
**Acts:** 68, running 1 January – 25 December 1827, plus one death recorded on a loose sheet and a
letter from the Royal Magistrate bound in behind it.
**Officers:** **SAVERIO CIOFFARI, sindaco**, to atto 34 in May; from **atto 35 (20 May)** onward
**AGOSTINO COSSI, *secondo eletto***.
**Duplicate bindings:** 27≡26, 35≡34. **Blank:** 20L, 21R, 22L, 41R.

## Two Falco

### Giuseppe Falco stands for the same house a fourth time — atto 30, 1 May

> …sono comparsi **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **quaranta**, di professione **COLONO**, regnicolo,
> domiciliato in strada **Camellara**, *vicino del Defunto*, e **Cesare Cossi**, di anni trenta, di
> professione contadino, domiciliato ivi strada Camellara, ***parente del Defunto***… è morto
> **Giuseppe Cossi, di un anno**, figlio di **Francesco Cossi**.

Francesco Cossi and the third Maria Falco buried Antonia in June 1823, Giuseppe in March 1824,
Giuseppa in April 1825 — and now this Giuseppe, aged one. **Giuseppe Falco of strada Camellara
declared all four.** The register calls him *vicino* every time and never *parente*, so it states no
kinship; four turns for one household is still the strongest hint the archive has that this Maria
Falco is his kin.

**But the age has stopped behaving.** He is thirty-seven in October 1817, forty in April 1825,
**forty-six twice in 1826**, and forty again here — and his trade slides from *massaro di campagna*
to *massaro* to *colono*. Either the clerks guessed, or there are two Giuseppe Falco on that street.
The archive does not yet know which, and says so.

### Matteo Falco, twenty-eight, *possidente* — atto 52, 12 October

> …sono comparsi Raffaele Palladino, di anni quarantadue, di professione Sartore … **e MATTEO
> FALCO**, di anni **ventotto**, di professione **POSSIDENTE**, regnicolo, domiciliato in strada
> **Porta di Sopra**, vicino del Defunto.

Plate: `site/public/plates/matteo-falco-possidente-1827.jpg`

**A propertied Falco, and a household this archive did not have.** Born about 1799. He does not live
on Camellara but on strada Porta di Sopra, and he is not a *contadino* or a *massaro* but a
*possidente* — the only Falco in twenty annual registers described that way.

The reading was checked at full resolution because of a known trap: the 1817 volume holds a
**«Giuseppe FALIO», 38**, whose surname ends «-io», not «-co», and which this archive has already
ruled out. This one ends **«-co»**, in the same hand and the same shape as the verified
«Giuseppe Falco» seven months earlier in the same volume. It is a Falco.

**He bears the founding ancestor's name.** Matteo Falco of generation one was dead by 1822. A
grandson named for him, born about 1799, would be exactly this man's age — but nothing in the act
names his father, and a propertied Falco sits oddly beside the labouring Camellara households. He
is recorded as an **unjoined strand**, like the other Camellara households before 1831.

## A murder in the register

Bound in behind atto 34, on a loose sheet, is a letter on the paper of the **GIUDICATO REGIO**:

> **Signore.** Ieri fu trovato **infossato nella Selva di D. Raffaele Acquaviello** un cadavere …
> **difformato nel volto, con la mancanza delle mura del cranio sinistro, ed altre offese**, che è
> stato riconosciuto per quello della nominata **MARIANTONIA D'ADDIO fu Felice, e moglie di
> PELLEGRINO MORGILLO**, colà interrata da circa venti in ventiquattro giorni … notizie che potranno
> esser di guida alla formazione del corrispondente atto di Morte, quale subito che avrà formato me
> ne spedirà copia **per allegarla ad un processo criminale alla quale sono occupato.**

Plate: `site/public/plates/daddio-murder-letter-1827.jpg`

The sindaco then wrote the death act itself on a second loose sheet — *«essendo inattuabile la sua
redazione al modello riportato in stampa ne' Registri corrispondenti»* — and sewed both into the
volume: **Mariantonia d'Addio, thirty-one, a *filatrice* of strada Capodichina, daughter of the late
Felice d'Addio and Gelsomina Laudato, wife of Pellegrino Morgillo.** Two words are not certain: the
wood's owner (Acquaviello / Acquaviva) and one verb describing the body.

This is the only violent death in twenty annual registers read so far, and the only time the criminal
court's own paper appears in a civil register. **Pellegrino Morgillo** is a name to carry forward.

## Suor Maria Teresa Cossi, one hundred and seven

> …è morta nel **Monistero di Ave Gratia Plena, SUOR MARIA TERESA COSSI, di anni CENTOSETTE e mesi
> quattro**, nata il **venti gennajo mille settecento venti**, di professione **Monaca** … figlia di
> **Carmine Cossi** defunto. — atto 35, 20 May

**The arithmetic is the register's own, and it checks**: January 1720 to May 1827 is 107 years and
4 months. She was born under Charles VI, lived through the whole Bourbon kingdom, the Parthenopean
Republic, Napoleon's brother and Murat, and died under Francis I.

## The houses that emptied

- **The Porrino of strada Cossi.** Claudia Vigliotta, 82, on 6 February; her husband **Nicola
  Porrino, 72**, nine days later; then **Giuseppa Porrino, 56** on 11 April and **Isabella Porrino,
  73** on 17 April, both daughters of the late Alessandro Porrino, six days apart.
- **The Carfora of strada Signorindio.** **Mariantonia, 5** (30 April), **Angela, 8** (3 May), and
  their father **Matteo Carfora, 49** (11 May) — three of one house in eleven days.
- **The Troisi.** **Don Biagio Troisi, 26, *farmacista*** on 27 February; **Don Giuseppe Troisi, 16**
  on 24 March. **Don Raffaele Troisi**, their father, had buried his wife Donna Agnese Vigliotta in
  February 1826. In May he appears again — declaring a neighbour's death.
- **The Liparulo**, from 1826, and the **Amigale**: Domenico Amigale, 45, in January, declared by his
  brother Raffaele; Antonio Amigale, twenty-four days old, in June, son of the same Raffaele.

## The Morgillo, and one age that will not fit

1827 adds six more Morgillo to the five buried in 1826:

- **SAVERIA MORGILLO, 48**, wife of **Carlantonio Porrino**, ***figlia di ANGELO MORGILLO*** —
  atto 64, 29 November. She is the mother of the Raffaele Porrino buried in September 1826.
- **Pellegrino Morgillo**, husband of the murdered Mariantonia d'Addio.
- **Antonio Morgillo, 30, *calzolajo*, strada Santa Lucia** (atto 42).
- **Clemente Morgillo, 30, contadino, strada Cossi** (atto 51).
- **Francesco Morgillo, 40, contadino, strada Cossi** (atto 63).
- **Gaetano Morgillo, 50** (1826, atto 75), still to be placed.

**The age does not work.** Saveria is forty-eight here, so born about 1779. The **Angelo Morgillo**
buried on 4 October 1826 was given as fifty-eight, born about 1768 — eleven years old at her birth.
Either there are two Angelo Morgillo, or one of the two ages is wrong. Both readings stand as read.

## The di Guida, with trades at last

- **LUIGI DI GUIDA, 27, *agrimensore*, of strada Monticello** — declaring atto 34 as ***parente del
  defunto*** for Matteo Carfora.
- **GENNARO DI GUIDA, 32, contadino, of strada Camellara** — declaring atto 49.

Antonia di Guida, wife of Giuseppe Falco, still has no named parents. These are the first di Guida in
the civil series with an age, a trade and a street, and the first on the family's own road.

## The reading this volume will not settle

The clerk of 1827 writes the surname of the strada Cossi family in a way that reads **CIOFFI** —
a clear «i» after the C, then the double long-s. He writes the sindaco's own name **Cioffari** with
the same opening. If the archive has been reading *Cioffi* as *Cossi* since 1823, that touches the
household of **Francesco Cossi and the third Maria Falco** directly. Flagged, not silently changed;
it needs a side-by-side against the 1825 volume.
