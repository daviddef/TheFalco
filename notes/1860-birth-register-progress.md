# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1860 — read end to end (`an_ua14457`)

> **Delegated read, 18 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand.**
> A delegated reader read every act of this volume at contact-sheet resolution (about 1,100 px a half
> page) and wrote a roster of it; **every Falco act, in any role, was then re-opened at about 2,400 px and
> read from the image again**, and a random sample of the other acts (acts 74–75 and 110–111) was compared with
> the manuscript — **no name in the sample disagreed**. The roster is therefore a *finding aid*: ages,
> trades and many non-Falco surnames in it are approximate and are not to be cited. What the
> reader's text below calls "confirmed" is what was re-opened; where it says "survey" the reading was not.
> The households this produced are in `households.json` (row evidence names the act and the image);
> the rows for the volume are in `searched.json` and on `/searched`.

## Volume facts
- Portal label: Arienzo > 1860, 64 images.
- **Year proved from the manuscript**: image 2R is the printed title «Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento sessanta … Il presente volume contiene fogli cento(?) …» (Distretto di Caserta, Comune di Arienzo; the President's delegation to a judge for the leaf-numbering follows); the cover (image 1R) is pencilled «1860 … Arienzo» and carries an archivist's stamp «18 Maggio 1861», «66» and the label 2282; every act reads «L'anno mille ottocento sessanta». Act 1 is 2 January 1860; act 122 is 31 December 1860 (born the same day).
- **Layout**: image 1 cover (patterned endpaper), image 2 title page; acts begin at **3R**. Each page carries one complete act (parents, declarant, witnesses on the same page; parish baptism notice and later marriage notes at the right). 3R=1, 4L=2, 4R=3 … 64L=122 (L = even, R = odd). **64R = blank printed form 123 struck through with a diagonal cross and the marginal note «END».** One band (0.10–0.74, both halves) reads the whole act.
- **N = 122 acts**, numbers 1..122 unbroken and unduplicated (script check); the TSV also carries the blank form 123 as a marker row.
- **Tavola: none** (front 1–2 and back 63–64 inspected; the volume ends with the END form). No index count to check.
- Registrars: Stefano Scarpati, sindaco (Jan–Jun), then Nicola Martone, sindaco (from act 76, 10 August). Midwives: Rosa Balletta, Rosa Morgillo (fu Marco), Marianna Bernardo, Rosa Crisci.
- Legibility: all 122 acts read. Ten acts «poor» in the TSV (forename/age/trade or a surname only approximate): 6, 7, 10, 14, 19, 24, 31, 53, 62, 72. No tear or stain hides a name. Images 13–64 were read at about 1,100 px per half page after the layout check; images 3–12 were read in a first pass at the same size.

## Falco appearances: ONE act as a parent, ONE in a margin

### Act 15 (image 10R) — CONFIRMED — Falco MOTHER
Read at ~3,400 px. «… è comparsa **Rosa Morgillo** figlia del fu Marco, di anni cinquanta(?), levatrice, domiciliata strada Santa Lucia, quale ci ha presentato un bambino … è nato da **Pasqua Falco** di anni trentasei(?) domiciliata strada **Camellara**, e da **Angelo Mojone** marito della stessa di anni cinquanta, contadino, domiciliato colla moglie, nel giorno ventidue del suddetto mese alle ore diciotto, nella casa de' suddetti conjugi.» Name **Domenico**; parish (Sant'Andrea Apostolo): baptised «Domenico Mojone». Witnesses Gaetano Fierro (sellajo) and Angelantonio Fierro (sartore), strada Santa Lucia. No margin note. Registered 23 January 1860. **Same couple as 1858 act 30 (Francesco)**: Angelo Mojone × Pasqua Falco, strada Camellara (her age 33 in 1858, 36 here; his 40 and 50 — the clerks guess).

### Act 57 (image 31R) — Falco only in the margin
Child Giuseppa Maria Eugenia, b. 14 May 1860, registered 17 May, to **Don Alessandro Gezue** (legale) and **Donna Pasqualina** (of Arienzo?), Santa Lucia; presented by the father's midwife Rosa Balletta. Margin read at ~3,400 px: «Addì 11 Luglio 1883. N.o 25. **Giuseppa Gezuè sposò in Arienzo Giacomo Di Falco**. Caserta 29 maggio 89.» The Falco is the GROOM of 1883 (written «Di Falco»); he is not a party to the birth act; his parents are not named.

## What is proven
- All 122 acts (1..122) viewed on contact sheets (both halves of images 3–64) and transcribed to `1860.tsv`; cover, title page and the END form viewed.
- Falco as a parent: **act 15 only**. Falco in a margin: **act 57 only**. No Falco as child, father, declarant, midwife or witness in any other act of 1860, to the strength of survey-scale reading of every parent/declarant/witness line (the ten «poor» acts have approximate surnames, none Falco-shaped). There is NO act of 1860 in which Angelo, Luigi, Michele, Giuseppe or Andrea Falco is named as a father.
- Not proven: ages/trades in the poor rows; the later-marriage margin notes in the other acts were read at survey scale.
