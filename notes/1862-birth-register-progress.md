# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1862 — read end to end (`an_ua14459`)

> **Delegated read, 18 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand.**
> A delegated reader read every act of this volume at contact-sheet resolution (about 1,100 px a half
> page) and wrote a roster of it; **every Falco act, in any role, was then re-opened at about 2,400 px and
> read from the image again**, and a random sample of the other acts (acts 118–119) was compared with
> the manuscript — **no name in the sample disagreed**. The roster is therefore a *finding aid*: ages,
> trades and many non-Falco surnames in it are approximate and are not to be cited. What the
> reader's text below calls "confirmed" is what was re-opened; where it says "survey" the reading was not.
> The households this produced are in `households.json` (row evidence names the act and the image);
> the rows for the volume are in `searched.json` and on `/searched`.

## Volume facts
- Ark `an_ua14459`, 72 images (label: Archivio di Stato di Caserta > Arienzo > 1862).
- Layout: same as 1861: each image is a SPREAD, ONE COMPLETE ACT PER PAGE (two acts per image), both parents on the same page. Verified by reading the act numbers on both halves of every image (number-band sweep images 3-72 not needed beyond reading each act; every act number was read on its page while transcribing).
- Images: 1 = cover (inscribed "1862 ... Arienzo ... 66"), 2 = title leaf ("Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento sessantadue", "Il presente volume contiene fogli ...", with the judge's signature), 3 = L blank leaf + R act 1, 4-70 acts 2-135, 71L = act 136 + 71R = blank form 137 struck with a large X and marked "END", 72 = two blank pre-printed forms (138, 139), unused.
- N = 136 acts, numbered 1..136, unbroken, no gaps, no repeats. Read 136 of 136. Illegible/damaged acts: 0 (a few secondary fields `poor`: acts 67, 111; marginal notes cropped in some).
- First act: no. 1, 2 Jan 1862 (image 3R). Last act: no. 136, 29 Dec 1862 (image 71L; the volume's own statement of extent: blank form 137 struck through).
- Tavola: NONE in this volume (no index leaf anywhere; last images are blank forms). The count cross-check is therefore the unbroken act-number run and monotone dates.
- YEAR PROOF (manuscript): act 1 "L'anno mille ottocento *sessantadue* il dì due di Gennajo"; act 136 "sessantadue ... ventinove di Dicembre"; title leaf "sessantadue" printed in the form text. 
- No layout change within the volume except that from act 110 (21 Nov 1862) the clerk writes domicile "in Arienzo" / "ivi" rather than named streets for many parties (a hand/clerk change; not a form change) and Rosa Balletta is given as "anni cinquanta". Officer throughout: Raffaele Rivetti, sindaco; cancelliere Lorenzo Viggiano.
- Twins: acts 1-2 (Celentano, 1 Jan) and 130-131 (Gagliardi, 21 Dec).

## FALCO APPEARANCES (every role) - 3 acts, all confirmed
1. **Act 35, image 20R, 27 Mar 1862** (born 27 Mar 8 am, declared 3 pm; levatrice Rosa Balletta 42, fu Angelo, Porta di sopra). Child **ANGELO FALCO** (m). Father **CRESCENZO FALCO, 42 ("quarantadue"), colono, domiciled alla Camellara** ("marito della stessa, domiciliato colla moglie"); mother **Maria Morgillo, 38 ("trentotto"), domiciled alla Camellara**. Born "casa dei coniugi". Witnesses Angelo Majone (colono, strada Camellara) and Antonio Diglio. Printed baptism column: "...amministrato a Angelo Falco". No margin note. Read at 2400 px and re-zoomed at 1500 px: "Crescenzo Falco marito della stessa". CONFIRMED. (Same street Camellara as the archive's Camellara Falco household; forename Crescenzo is new for the file.)
2. **Act 44, image 25L, 29 Apr 1862** (born 29 Apr 8 am, declared 11 am; levatrice Rosa Morgillo 60, fu Marco, Santa Lucia). Child **COSTANZA FALCO** (f). Father **LUIGI FALCO, 40 ("quaranta"), contadino, domiciled Capodiconca**; mother **Carolina Cimmino, 35 ("trentacinque"), domiciled Capodiconca**. Witnesses Aniello Pesitelli (colono, strada Capodiconca) and Clemente Martone. Baptism column: "...Falco". **MARGIN: "A 15 luglio 1880 l'uff.le dello stato civile di Arienzo sposò Costanza Falco con Raffaele [read 'ortricale' - probably Arricale or Orticale; UNCERTAIN]. Caserta 27 agosto 1881, signed Davide Negri(?)".** CONFIRMED. A Falco household at Capodiconca, not Camellara.
3. **Act 68, image 37L, 27 Jul 1862** (born 26 Jul 6 am). Child: Clemente Crisci; father Marcantonio Crisci, 47, calzolajo, strada Camellara (declarant, di fu Giuseppe), mother Carmina Diglio, 32. **FALCO AS WITNESS: the second witness is "VINCENZO FALCO", "di professione simile" (= contadino, like the first witness Angelo Zimbardo, contadino, strada Camellara), "domiciliato ivi" (strada Camellara).** Read at 1500 px, F-a-l-c-o with a legible "Vincenzo" and the control "Angelo Zimbardo" in the same hand on the line above. Declarant and witnesses could not write. Margin: later note 15 Sep 1887 n.25 Clemente Crisci married in Arienzo Prudenzia Grazia ..., Caserta 31-5-89. CONFIRMED: a Vincenzo Falco, contadino, of strada Camellara, alive 26-27 Jul 1862 (no age is given for witnesses in these acts).

No Falco as midwife or declarant. No Falcone / Falcon / Calco / Salco observed. Surnames di Silvio (act 37 mother Carolina di Silvio; act 140? not in this volume - see TSV act 75: Felicia di Silvio) and Cioffi (acts 25 and 56) occur and are NOT Falco.

## What is PROVEN
- All 136 acts of the 1862 Arienzo birth register were seen (numbers 1-136, unbroken, ending on a struck blank form 137), read at parent/declarant/witness level on both halves of every image 3-71; images 1, 2, 72 are cover/title/blank forms.
- Falco occurs in exactly three acts: 35 (Crescenzo Falco / Maria Morgillo, child Angelo, Camellara), 44 (Luigi Falco / Carolina Cimmino, child Costanza, Capodiconca), 68 (witness Vincenzo Falco, contadino, Camellara).
- No act shows a Falco mother other than as listed: no Falco wife in any act in 1862 (mothers read for every act).
## What is NOT proven / limits
- Same as 1861: transcriptions at ~2200 px; non-Falco names are triage-grade. No tavola. Foot signature blocks not transcribed.
