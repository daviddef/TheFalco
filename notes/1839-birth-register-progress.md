Closing certificate (image 135R): «L'Anno mille ottocento trentanove, il dì trentuno del mese di Decembre, noi Filippo Troisi Sindaco ed ufficiale dello Stato Civile del Comune di Corpo d'Arienzo ... abbiamo chiuso, e firmato il presente registro di nascita ... di fogli scritti num. - First act: no. 1, 1 Jan 1839 (Gaetano Sansone, Carmela). Last act: no. 132, 31 Dec 1839 (Domenico Carfora, Giovanna). N = 132 acts. Run unbroken 1..132: YES (checked mechanically on the TSV: no gap, no duplicate), and 132 matches the number written in the closing certificate.
- Layout: the one-act-per-two-facing-halves form described in the brief. Act N = right page of image N+1 + left page of image N+2 (act 1 = 2R+3L, act 132 = 133R+134L). Right page: printed «ATTO DI NASCITA» with «INDICAZIONE» (baptism) column; left page of next image: mother, father, child's name, witnesses, margin column. Both halves of every image read at 3000 px (whole left half; top 58% of the right half). Margin notes are in the right-hand printed column of the LEFT page.
- Front matter: image 1 = title page on the right, a 1849 ministerial/Procuratore notice (mirrored show-through) on the left; image 2L = show-through of the title page (not a separate leaf); act 1 begins at 2R.
- Back matter: image 134R-135L = BLANK printed «Tavola alfabetica annuale de' Nati» form (not filled); image 135R = the closing certificate of 31 Dec 1839 with seal; image 136L-136R = a HAND-WRITTEN «Tavola annuale alfabetica di nati per l'anno Mille ottocento trentanove» — INCOMPLETE: it carries only 22 entries, in the A–C stretch (Altarelli, Angiovino, Arnone, Arrigale, Affinito, Crisci, Cimmino, Cillo, Celentano), ending at act 74 and leaving the sheet; 137L is an empty ruled page with only the column heads; 137R = another copy of the 1849 ministerial notice (stamped «END»). So the F page of the tavola does not exist; the tavola was not used as a substitute for the acts.
  The tavola entry for act 19 reads «Crisci Maria Maddalena / Crisci Aniello, e Falco Giovanna / 10 Febbrajo 1839», which independently confirms the mother Falco.
- Duplicate frames: none. Blank forms at the end: 134R-135L (blank tavola form).
- Damage: none material. Pale/faint ink in some acts; fair legibility of trades and some surnames (marked `?` in the TSV). Ages copied as written. Twins at acts 104/105 (Lettieri) and 122/123 (Zimbardo, 11 Dec).
