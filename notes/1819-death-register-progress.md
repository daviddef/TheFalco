# Arienzo civil death register 1819 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14321` (Arienzo, Morti 1819), 37 images. **Read in full.**
**Acts:** roughly 60, running 9 January – 20 December 1819. Image 37 is blank.
**Officers:** **Andrea Sorice, Sindaco**, with **Salvatore Scarpati, secondo eletto** deputising.
**Crop:** left `8,19,46,27`, right `50,19,46,27`, width ~950 — the 1818 band, unchanged.
**Duplicate bindings:** 11≡10, 22≡21.

## Three Falco entries, and two of them are a single day

### Domenico and Rosaria Falco — image 8, both 15 March 1819

> **DOMENICO FALCO** *figlio di* **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, *e* **GELSOMINA VIGLIOTTA** — died at
> **twelve o'clock**.
> **ROSARIA FALCO** *figlia di* **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, *e* **GELSOMINA VIGLIOTTA** — died at
> **twenty-three hours**.

Plates: `site/public/plates/domenico-falco-death-1819.jpg`, `rosaria-falco-death-1819.jpg`

**Two children of one house on one day, eleven hours apart.** Domenico was born on 25 November 1816
and was two years and four months old. **No birth act has been found for Rosaria** — she is not in
the Arienzo birth registers of 1816, 1817 or 1818, so she was born before them.

This is the household of the **strada Costa** Giuseppe Falco, the one who married **Gelsomina
Vigliotta** — not the strada Camellara Giuseppe who married Antonia di Guida. The two men have
never been easy to keep apart, and this act settles the method for doing it: **go by the wife, not
the street.** The declarants here are of strada Camellara, so the street alone would mislead.

### Angela Falco, named a second time — image 5, 12 February 1819

> **ANGELANDREA RIVETTI** *figlia del fu* **CRESCENZO RIVETTI**, *ed* **ANGELA FALCO**

Plate: `site/public/plates/angelandrea-rivetti-death-1819.jpg`

Crescenzo Rivetti died on 1 December 1816, in the act that first told this archive a Falco had
married a Rivetti — declared by **Gioacchino Falco** and **Giuseppe Falco** as *«parenti del
defunto»*. **This is their daughter**, dead at two years and two months, and the only other record
anywhere that names Angela Falco. She is written without *fu*, so she was still alive.

## The gentry Rivetti are now unmistakable

### Suor Maria Maddalena Rivetti — image 10, 30 March 1819

> **SUOR MARIA MADDALENA RIVETTI** *figlia delli fu* **DON GIUSEPPE RIVETTI** *e* **GIOVANNA BEGA**

A **nun**. Her father is a *Don* Giuseppe Rivetti already dead — and therefore **not** the Don
Giuseppe Rivetti, *sacerdote*, aged forty-one, who was declaring deaths in October 1818. A priest
would have no legitimate daughter in any case. Two men of one name in one gentry house.

The Rivetti of standing at Arienzo are now four: **Donna Francesca Rivetti** (1816), the apothecary
**Don Giovanbattista Rivetti** (1818), the priest **Don Giuseppe Rivetti** (1818), and this **Don
Giuseppe Rivetti the elder** with a daughter in religion.

**And the contadini Rivetti are just as numerous** — Raffaele, Pasquale, Paolo, Marcello, Francesco,
Salvatore, Anna, Maria, Girolama, Manilia, Gelsomina, across four streets. **The surname reaches
from the bottom of the town to the top**, which is exactly why the forenames «Nicola and Anna»
cannot place Chiara Rivetti in the gentry house. That reading is on the open-questions page.

**A warning about Rivetti names.** They reuse them relentlessly. There are two Paolo Rivetti (one
died March 1818, one aged 45 in May 1819), two Marcello (one died September 1818, one aged 55 in
June 1819), and a Pasquale Rivetti aged 24 in 1819 where Crescenzo's father Pasquale was dead by
1816. Ages wobble too: Raffaele Rivetti is 22, 28, 26 and 28 across twenty months.

## Giuseppe di Guida, and the strada Maddalena cluster

### Image 29, 6 September 1819

> …sono comparsi **GIUSEPPE QUOTOLO**, di anni **sessantasette**, *massaro di campagna*, **strada
> Maddalena**, e di **GIUSEPPE DI GUIDA**, di anni **sessantotto**, *massaro di campagna*, strada
> Maddalena … **ANGELO QUOTOLO** *figlio di* **FRANCESCO QUOTOLO**, *e* **VITTORIA DI GUIDA**.

Two things worth having. **Giuseppe Quotolo is sixty-seven** here and was sixty-five in July 1817,
when he declared **Maria Falco's** death — a rare case of an age that behaves. And **Giuseppe di
Guida, sixty-eight**, born about 1751, is a living head of the family that Giuseppe Falco of strada
Camellara married into. A Giuseppe di Guida is named in October 1817 as the father of Angelo Andrea
di Guida by Michedalena Rivetti; a man of this age could father both him and **Antonia di Guida**.
**Nothing here names Antonia, so it is a lead and not a link.**

The strada Maddalena cluster is now clear: Quotolo and di Guida, both *massari di campagna*,
intermarrying — and Maria Falco married a Francesco Quotolo of that street, while Carlantonio
Morgillo, Rosa Falco's husband, lived on it too.

## Carlantonio Morgillo, a fifth time

Image 32, 20 October: **«Carlantonio Morgillo, di anni trentacinque, contadino»**. The run is now
30 (Feb 1816), 36 (Oct 1816), 33 (Sept 1817), 33 (Aug 1818), 35 (Oct 1819). The last three hold
together around a birth of **1784–85**; the two 1816 readings remain the outliers.

## Also noted

- **Donn'Agnese Vigliotta**, wife of Don Giuseppe Troisi (image 18) — a gentry Vigliotta, the
  surname two Falco married.
- **Donna Nicoletta Morgillo**, daughter of **Don Vincenzo Morgillo** and **Donn'Orsola Carriero**,
  wife of Don Bernardino di Lucia (image 6) — a **second** gentry Morgillo house beside Don Lelio's.
- **Gennaro Morgillo**, 32, *contadino* of **strada Camellara** (image 34) — a third Morgillo
  household placed on the family's street.
- **Domenico Balletta**, Giuseppe Falco's Camellara neighbour, appears again at forty-six.
- **Angelo Crisci**, 52, alive in February 1819 — though a *«fu Angelo Crisci»* fathered Faustina
  Crisci in 1817. Two men, or a widow's second marriage; not resolved.
