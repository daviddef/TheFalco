# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1817 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14414` (Arienzo, Nati 1817), 48 images, one copy only.
Acts January – December; blank forms from image 45; closing certification at 47.
As in 1818, every act is pre-printed **"sedici"** and corrected by hand to **"diciassette"**.

## Result: no Falco at all
**Not one Falco appears in the 1817 births** — not as a father, not as a declarant.

## What that means for Maria Rosa Falco
She was **sixteen** when she died in May 1834, so born about 1817–18. **She is in neither the 1817
nor the 1818 birth register.** Three readings, and this archive does not choose between them:
1. her stated age was loose — sixteen could stretch to a birth in **1816** (`an_ua14413`) or earlier;
2. she was **born outside Arienzo**;
3. the act exists and was missed, though both volumes were read act by act.

**Michele Falco of strada Camellara is therefore still undated**, and the question of whether he is
Matteo's son remains open. The next volume to try is **1816, `an_ua14413`**.

## Two by-products worth more than the null

### There really are two Arcangelo Crisci
This archive has carried an open question: Arcangelo Crisci appears both as **Andreana Crisci's
father** and as the **husband of Anna Falco**, who died at twenty-two in 1820 — one man twice
married, or two men? The 1817 register settles that there were **two men of the name alive at
Arienzo at once**:

| image | Arcangelo Crisci | age | implies born |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32 (left) | contadino | **52** | about 1765 |
| 40 (right) | contadino | **30** | about 1787 |

The elder fits Andreana's father — she was twenty in May 1818, so born about 1798, when he would
have been thirty-three. The younger fits a man marrying Anna Falco (born 1795) around 1817.
**Two men, not one.** The 1829 sighting of an Arcangelo Crisci fathering a child on strada Camellara
sits comfortably with the younger.

### Carlantonio Morgillo dated
**Carlantonio Morgillo, 33, *bracciale*, strada Maddalena**, declaring a birth in September 1817 —
so born about **1784**. He is the man who married **Rosa Falco**, Pasquale's sister, who died on
strada Camellara in 1830. In 1817 his household is on strada Maddalena, so the Morgillo moved to
Camellara later, or kept more than one address.
