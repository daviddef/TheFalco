# Arienzo civil death register 1828 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14330` (Arienzo, Morti 1828), 48 images. **Read in full, act by act.**
**Acts:** 70, running 16 January – 22 December 1828, plus one death transcribed in from Aversa.
**Officers:** **AGOSTINO COSSI, *secondo eletto***, almost throughout; **SAVERIO CIOFFARI, *sindaco***,
signs atto 55.
**Duplicate bindings, unusually many:** 9≡8, 18≡17, 21≡20, 27≡26, 30≡29, 33≡32, 37≡36, 43≡42, 44≡42.
**The form:** from 1828 the printed act names **both** parents of the deceased as a matter of course,
with each parent's trade and address.

## The word the archive has been waiting six years for

### Atto 54, 24 October 1828

> …sono comparsi **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **CINQUANTASEI**, di professione **POSSIDENTE**,
> regnicolo, domiciliato in strada **CAMELLARA**, ***PARENTE DELLA DEFUNTA***, e **CESARE COSSI**,
> di anni trentuno, di professione contadino, regnicolo, domiciliato in strada Camellara,
> ***parente della defunta***, i quali han dichiarato, che nel giorno ventitré del mese di Ottobre,
> anno corrente, alle ore ventidue, è morta nella sua propria casa **MARIANTONIA COSSI, di mesi
> sette** … **figlia di FRANCESCO COSSI**, di professione contadino, **e di MARIA FALCO**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-parente-1828.jpg`

**The register finally states the kinship.** Since 1823 this archive has watched **Giuseppe Falco of
strada Camellara** declare the deaths of the children of **Francesco Cossi and the third Maria
Falco** — Antonia in June 1823, Giuseppe in March 1824, Giuseppa in April 1825, Giuseppe in May 1827,
and now Mariantonia. **Five children in six years.** Every previous act called him *vicino*, a
neighbour. This one calls him ***parente***, a kinsman — and writes the word twice, once for him and
once for Cesare Cossi.

The archive has carried the question as **not established** for two years. It can now be stated:
**Maria Falco, wife of Francesco Cossi, is kin to Giuseppe Falco of strada Camellara.** The degree is
still unstated; the fact is not.

### And the same act deepens a problem

Giuseppe Falco is **fifty-six** here, and a **possidente**. Eighteen months earlier, in the same
street, in the same register series, he is **forty** and a **colono**. The full run now reads:

| date | age | trade |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 1817 | 37 | massaro di campagna |
| Apr 1825 | 40 | massaro |
| Aug 1826 | 46 | massaro |
| Oct 1826 | 46 | massaro |
| May 1827 | 40 | colono |
| **Oct 1828** | **56** | **possidente** |

Thirty-seven to fifty-six is nineteen years in eleven. **Either the clerks were guessing, or there
are two Giuseppe Falco on strada Camellara** — one a labouring *massaro/colono* born about 1780, one
a *possidente* born about 1772. The archive does not know which, and will not choose. Note that a
second propertied Falco — **Matteo Falco, 28, *possidente*** — turned up in 1827 on strada Porta di
Sopra.

## The Morgillo, now in households

1828 does for the Morgillo what 1831 did for the Falco: it starts joining them.

**On Monticello de' Cappuccini:**
- **ANGELO MORGILLO, 70, *colono*, of strada Cappuccini** (atto 56). **This is a second Angelo
  Morgillo** — not the Angelo, 58, of strada Nuova di San Gaetano buried in October 1826. That
  resolves the age conflict raised by Saveria Morgillo's act of 1827: *her* father is plainly this
  one, born about 1758.
- **NICOLA MORGILLO, 34**, × **Maria Majone** — their eight-year-old Raffaele dies in May (atto 14).
- **VINCENZO MORGILLO, 29–32**, × **Felicetta Rivetti** — their one-year-old Grazia dies in October
  (atto 56), and **Nicola declares it as *zio della defunta***. So **Nicola and Vincenzo are
  brothers**, and Angelo, 70, is very likely their father.
- The two brothers also declare the death of the Capuchin tertiary in December.

**On strada Nuova di San Gaetano:**
- **BIAGIO MORGILLO** × **Vincenza Vece** — their 26-month-old Clementina dies in March (atto 11),
  declared by **ANTONIO MORGILLO, 27** and **SIMONE MORGILLO, 24**, both ***parenti***.
- **DOMENICO MORGILLO, 25** declares in September (atto 44), also *parente*.
- That act buries **Vittoria Morgillo, 30**, *«figlia di GIUSEPPE MORGILLO defunto e di GELSOMINA
  RIVETTI»* — **a household this archive already holds**.

**Of the older generation:**
- **ANNA MORGILLO, 85** (b. ~1743), widow of Sabato Cossi, *«figlia di NICOLA MORGILLO defunto e di
  ROSOLINA BRUNO»* (atto 42).
- **ROSA D'ADDIO, 70, widow of BIAGIO MORGILLO** (atto 16) — **a second d'Addio woman married to a
  Morgillo man**, beside the Mariantonia d'Addio, wife of Pellegrino Morgillo, who was murdered in
  1827.
- **Cesare Morgillo**, husband of the Andreana di Luzo buried in December (atto 63).

## Two conflicts recorded, not smoothed

**Ovidia Morgillo's husband.** In June 1826 she is *«vedova di RAFFAELE Diglio»*. In July 1828 her son
**Francesco Diglio, 38**, dies *«figlio di PASQUALE Diglio defunto e di OVIDIA MORGILLO defunta»*.
One of the two is wrong. (Francesco is also 44 in 1826 and 38 in 1828.)

**Marcello Cossi, or Marcello Crisci?** Atto 51 buries a three-year-old *«figlio di MARCELLO COSSI e
di MARIA ROSA MAURO»* on strada Camellara. This archive holds a household **«Marcello Crisci & Maria
Rosa Mauro»**, built from a 1823 act. The wife's name is identical in both. **It is the same couple,
and one of the two surname readings is wrong** — the same Cossi/Crisci/Cioffi ambiguity flagged in
the 1827 notes. This now has a household attached to it and must be settled.

## The officer buries his own

**Donn'Agostino Cossi**, the *secondo eletto* who signs almost every act in this volume, buried
**Vincenzo, fifteen days old**, on 7 December 1827 and **Alfonso, twelve days old**, on 29 October
1828 (atto 55). His wife is **Donna Gelsomina d'Addio**. He signed both acts himself.

## Institutions, trades and the rest

- **ALESSANDRO GAMMELLA, 76, *terziario cappuccino***, died in the **Convento de' Cappuccini**
  (atto 64) — the **fourth** Capuchin death across these volumes.
- **Don CARLANTONIO BRUNO, 70, *Dottor Fisico*** (atto 36) — a second physician, after the
  Don Alessandro Sequa of 1827.
- **Don GIUSEPPE GESPO, 58, *proprietario*** (atto 68); **Vincenza Sepistella, 84, *possidente***
  (atto 15); **Don Nicola Cossi, 36, *amanuense***; **Don Nicola Giglio, 25, *sacerdote***, declaring
  as *parente* at atto 1.
- **Gennaro Cossi** died on **16 May 1826 in the ospedale di Sant'Agostino at AVERSA**; his death was
  transcribed into the Arienzo register two years later.
- Trades new to the series: **saponaro**, **fondachiere**, **ministeriale**, **corriere**,
  **serviente**, **fabbricatore**, **pettinatore**, **panettiere**.
- **GIUSEPPE RIVETTI, 50, contadino, strada Camellara** (atto 51) — a Rivetti on the family's street.
- **GENNARO DI GUIDA, 30, contadino, strada Camellara** (atto 28), with **Aniello Diglio, 50**, who
  declared Clemente Falco's death in 1825.
