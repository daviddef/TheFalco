# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1842 — read end to end by a delegated reader (`an_ua14439`)

> **25 October 2026.** The roster below is a finding aid: only the Falco acts the households file cites as READ FROM THE IMAGE were re-opened by the archive's own hand at ~2,800 px. Everything else is triage-grade.

**Ark:** an_ua14439. **Images:** 121.

## Year proof (from the manuscript)
- Image 1R, printed title page (pencil '236'): «Registro degli atti dello stato civile. Degli atti di Nascita a norma del libro 1.° tit.° 2.° della parte prima del Codice per lo Regno delle Due Sicilie. Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento quarantadue [handwritten 'quarantadue']. Il presente volume contiene fogli [number left blank]». Provincia di Terra di Lavoro, Distretto di Caserta, Circondario di Arienzo, Comune di Borgo d'Arienzo (as written).
- Act 2 (images 3R/4L): «N. d'ordine due. L'anno mille ottocento quarantadue il dì due del mese di Gennajo ad ore sedici ...».
- Act 114 (image 117R): «L'anno mille ottocento quarantadue il dì ventotto del mese di Dicembre».
- Closing certificate (image 119L, page 352): «L'anno mille ottocento quarantadue il dì trentuno del mese di Dicembre, noi Raffaele de' Musso Andrea? ... ufficiale dello Stato Civile del comune di Borgo d'Arienzo ... abbiamo chiuso e firmato il presente registro degli atti di nascita, di fogli scritti num. Duecento ventotto [?], ... contenenti atti num. cento quattor[dici] (end of the word lost at the line break)». The last act ends on printed page 228, which agrees with 'fogli scritti 228'.

## Run
- First act: no. 1, 1 Jan 1842 (images 2-3; the child was born 31 Dec 1841 and registered 1 Jan). Last act: no. 114, 28 Dec 1842 (head 117R page 227, tail 118L page 228).
- **114 acts, numbers 1..114 unbroken** (checked against the TSV: no gap, no repeat). Matches the officer's closing certificate ('cento quattor...' = 114). After act 114 the leaves are the printed blank tavola form (118R), the closing certificate (119L), a blank (119R), the tavola page (120L), a blank (121L) and the printed ministerial notice (121R).
- Act order near the end is not strictly chronological: act 110 = 16 Dec, 111 = 27 Dec, 112 = 28 Dec, 113 = 27 Dec, 114 = 28 Dec. The numbers run on unbroken; the dates were re-read at 3000 px and the same day-words are repeated in the header, the Indicazione and the birth line of each act (a clerk entering two days' acts out of sequence). An earlier pass read 110 as 9 Dec and 111/112 as 29 Dec; the re-read corrects these (TSV rows replaced).
- Officer: Raffaele de' Musso Andrea? ; second signature of the clerk 'Felice (Felio) Morgillo, Cancelliere' under every act (the clerk, not a Falco).

## Layout
Act N = right page of image k (head, declarant, Indicazione of baptism) + left page of image k+1 (mother, father / 'da lui dichiarante', hour of birth, child's name, witnesses, margin column). Constant through the volume. Printed form edition changes at acts 31, 79 and 105 (type face and wording of the form; layout unchanged). Printed page numbers on the outer top corner (R odd, L even) with pencil folio numbers beside them.

## Duplicate frames (same spread filmed twice, not extra acts)
Image 68 (= 67, pages 130/131) and image 85 (= 84, pages 162/163). Rows 'dup' in the TSV.

## Tavola
At the BACK and incomplete in the images. Image 118R is a printed blank tavola form (page 351). Image 120L is a handwritten 'Tavola alfabetica annuale de' Nati per l'anno mille ottocento quarantadue 1842' - ONE page only, listing initials A..C (Arrigale, Arnone, Bernardo, Crisci, Carfora, Cimmino, Celentano, Cerio, Cappuccio ...; act numbers up to 94), 22 lines. 120R carries only the mirrored show-through of the verso (illegible even flipped and contrast-stretched); 121L is blank. No F page exists in the images, so the tavola could NOT be used to cross-check Falco. Every Falco below is from the acts themselves.

## Damage / other
Right-hand pages of many images are shadowed by the binding and the paper-string tie; day-words and hours in December are scrawled (re-read at 3000 px for acts 110-114). Ages and hours of many non-Falco acts are fair, not certain; copy as written. Acts 101 and 105 are poor (child's surname doubtful); neither is a Falco candidate (no F-initial surname, no -lco).

## Near-misses cleared
- Act 9 (10R left, diagonal later note): marriage 1903/1905 of Fiorentino Arnone with Antonuccia Zolli?/Folli? - not Falco (letters counted, no -lco).
- Act 14 margin spouse Ferriello; act 24 margin Lettieri; act 42 margin 1913 Carfora - none Falco.
- Acts 110-114 (December, re-read at 3000 px): Marchese, Strazzolino?, Zangara?, Dorrino, Liparulo, Giglio, d'Ago, Angiolino?, Mennone?, Zazzaro? - no Falco in any role. Act 112's witness Clemente Giglio has a trade word that is not a surname.
- Act 101 'Zimbardo?/Lombardo?' (poor): not Falco.

## Limitations
- Rows for acts 1-109 were written in the first pass; non-Falco names, ages and hours are fair, not certain (no verbatim transcription required).
- Acts 110-114 were re-read this session; the December day-words are scrawled (see above).
- Tavola unusable beyond one A-C page (above).
- Act 4 mother's given name and surname ('Raffaela? Falco') and act 46's declarant age 'ventotto?' are doubtful readings; the surname Falco is clear in all four acts.
- Closing certificate: the final digits ('cento quattor[dici]') are cut at the line break; 114 is supported by the unbroken act numbers.
