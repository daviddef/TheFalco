# The passports of Carminantonio Falco and Filomena Annecchino

**Photographed October 2026 by the family — 52 images**, 1024 px, phone photographs. Many are duplicates;
there are two covers each, the identity pages, a run of Australian visas and landing stamps, the Australian
alien-registration cards, and one image of the Arpaia marriage extract (published separately as a plate).
**The originals stay with the family. Only the identity pages and the 1972 landing spread are published, as
crops, and the passport numbers are deliberately left off every plate, note and household row.**

## The Australian pages, as read

**Filomena** — visitor visa *99*, one person, single journey, a stay of two months, issued **20 July 1972** at
the Australian Embassy, Rome; landing stamp **«Permitted to enter Australia — 24 Jul 1972 — Sydney Airport»**;
Brisbane permits her to remain until **24 January 1973** (stamp of 5 October 1972) and until **24 May 1973**
(stamp of 16 February 1973). Then a class **125A** visa issued Rome **20 August 1976** (multiple entry, three
months, to 19 August 1977), and another issued Rome **18 July 1978** (single entry, six months, to 17 July
1979, *employment prohibited*) with a landing stamp of **26 August 1978**, and a Brisbane stamp of **4 May 1979**.

**Carminantonio** — the same two 125A visas (20 August 1976; 18 July 1978) and the same landing of
**26 August 1978**. *The two landed together.* His 1976 passport cannot show 1972.

**Both** carry the Australian alien-registration card with its departure boxes **blank**: no departure from
Australia was ever written on either.

## What this changes

1. **Filomena's birth date and place now rest on an Italian official record** made in her lifetime, not only on
   a Brisbane grave. Her **birth act is still unfound**; a passport records what the holder declared.
2. **Carminantonio was born at Arienzo** — the family tree said «Caserta», the province.
3. **A 24 July 1972 landing is Filomena's.** The spine had attributed one to Carminantonio on a forename and a
   date (NAA barcode 12047001, p. 1605, image not pulled). Doubtful, not disproved — **worklist row 103.**
4. Filomena was **in Italy in July 1972**, so the 1968 arrival was not her only one.
