# Arienzo civil death register 1822 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14324` (Arienzo, Morti 1822), 44 images. **Read in full.**
**Acts:** 82, running 4 January – 31 December 1822. Image 43 (right) is a blank form.
**Officer:** **Vincenzo Morgillo, Sindaco**, throughout. *Distretto di Nola*.
**Form:** the 1820 form, and by now it gives birthplace as «**Arienzo Corpo**» rather than plain
Arienzo — a small change that distinguishes the town proper from its hamlets.
**Crop:** left `0.06,0.17,0.50,0.34`, right `0.48,0.17,0.50,0.34`, one full-page fetch per leaf.

## ANGELA FALCO — atto 65, 17 October 1822

> …sono comparsi **NICOLA MAGGIONE**, di anni **ventidue**, *Contadino*, domiciliato ivi **strada
> Capodiuomo**, *vicino della defunta*, ed **ALESSANDRO MARTONE**, di anni **ventidue**,
> *Contadino*, domiciliato ivi, strada Capodiuomo, *vicino della defunta* … **è morta nella sua
> propria casa ANGELA FALCO di anni CINQUANTUNO, VEDOVA DI CRESCENZO RIVETTI, nata in Arienzo, di
> professione Contadina, domiciliata ivi strada Capodiuomo, FIGLIA DI MATTEO FALCO Defunto, di
> professione Contadino, E DI FRANCESCA CRISCI Defunta.**

Plate: `site/public/plates/angela-falco-death-1822.jpg`

**A daughter of the couple at the head of this pedigree** — and so a **sister of Pasquale Falco**.
Born about **1771**.

**Matteo Falco and Francesca Crisci are now named from the death acts of three separate children:**

| child | act | date | age |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Angela Falco** | Morti 1822, atto 65 | **17 Oct 1822** | 51 |
| Rosa Falco | Morti 1830, act 53 | 16 Oct 1830 | 42 |
| Pasquale Falco | Morti 1834, act 69 | 1 Oct 1834 | 74 |

**Angela's is the earliest of the three**, and it is the first time the archive has had the founding
couple attested before 1830.

### It also solves an act found earlier in this research

When Angela's husband **Crescenzo Rivetti** died on 1 December 1816, the declarants were
**GIOACCHINO FALCO**, thirty-six, and **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, thirty-two, both *massari di campagna* of
strada Camellara, and the register calls them ***«parenti del defunto»*** — kin of the dead man.
Nothing then explained the relationship. **Now it does: they were his wife's brothers.**

That makes **Gioacchino Falco a strong candidate for a fifth child of Matteo and Francesca** —
born about 1780, on the family's own street. **It is an inference, not a record**: no act yet names
Gioacchino's parents. It is stated as such on the direct-line page.

### One correction to my own reading

At the low-resolution pass I read the husband as *«vedova di **Gaspare** Rivetti»*. The enlargement
shows **Crescenzo** — the same man whose 1816 death act this archive already holds. Angela outlived
him by nearly six years and had moved from strada Camellara to **strada Capodiuomo**.

## The rest of the year

Eighty-two acts and no other Falco. What the volume does show is how thick the family's street
still is: the Camellara declarants of 1822 are the **Giglio** (Cesare, Carmine, Bartolomeo,
Francesco, Alfonzo, Luigi), the **Cossi** (Giuseppe, Nicola, Andrea), **Giuseppe** and **Domenico
Martone**, **Giovanni Saccavino**, **Virgilio Saccavino**, **Alessandro Lettieri**, **Arcangelo
Esposito**, **Sabbatino Carfora** and **Nicola Maggio**.

**Other threads worth keeping:**

- **The di Guida and Quotolo of strada Maddalena, again.** On 30 December **Giuseppe di Guida**,
  about sixty, declares the death of an eleven-month-old **Margarita Quotolo** as her ***avo*** —
  her grandfather. So a di Guida daughter had married a Quotolo, which the 1819 act (Francesco
  Quotolo × Vittoria di Guida) already implied. **Pietro Quotolo**, thirty-two, declares beside him.
  A **Gennaro di Guida**, thirty-seven, appears in January.
- **More Rivetti generations.** **Girolama Rivetti**, eighty, «figlia di **Carmine Rivetti**», died
  3 October; **Carlo Rivetti**, seventy, and **Vincenzo Rivetti**, forty-three, declare from strada
  Monticello — the street where Carmine Rivetti died in 1821.
- **Three priests and a gentry roll-call.** Don Giuseppe Verdicchio, *sacerdote*, fifty-two;
  Donofrio Morgillo, seventy-six, *sacerdote di Sant'Andrea Apostolo*; Don Antonio Martone; Don
  Angelo d'Onofrio; Don Vincenzo Vetrone. Plus **Don Luigi Guglielmone Morgillo**, aged two, son of
  **Don Felice Morgillo** — a further gentry Morgillo house.
- **Signora Faustina Cimmino**, widow of **Signor Stefano Scarpati** — the mother of Don Salvatore
  Scarpati, the notary who died in February 1820.
- **A beggar boy from Puglia.** Atto 44: **Nicola Fasciotelo**, fourteen, *mendicante*, born at
  Alessi in the Province of Bari, dead in the Ospedale di Ave Grazia Plena. The hospital takes six
  of the year's dead, and the same two *calzolai* of San Felice — Matteo and Domenico Migliore —
  declare every one of them.
- **A death on the road.** Vincenzo di Luca, seventy-four, a mason of Santa Maria a Vico, died
  «sulla strada» at Montanovecchio in this comune.
