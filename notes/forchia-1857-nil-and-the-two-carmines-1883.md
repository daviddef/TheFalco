# Forchia: no Annecchino birth in 1857, and two Carmine Annecchino in September 1883 — 17 Oct 2026

## 1. The 1857 birth act does not exist at Forchia (a nil, with its reasoning)
Question: Carmine Annecchino's birth act, from the niche date 26·1·1857.
- Volume resolved from the manuscripts, not arithmetic: Forchia Nati 1857 = `an_ua29086` (Caserta, Stato civile della
  Restaurazione, 26 images); 1854–1856 = `an_ua29083–29085`; 1858–1860 = `an_ua29087–29089`.
- 1857 holds **41 acts; the first is dated 3 February** (Clemente Rivetti). A date-line sweep of every act shows strictly
  increasing dates to 31 December, so no January act is out of sequence. Its annual table starts at Buffolino and has **no
  Annecchino** (41 entries = 41 acts, so it is not a half-page nil).
- The tables of 1854–1856 and 1858–1860 were read at their first rows only: an Annecchino would sort first or second, so that
  suffices for those years. **Not read: 1853 and earlier; the neighbouring towns; Forchia's parish baptisms.**
- Meaning: the man was not registered at Forchia in 1854–1860, or the stone's date is not his birth, or he is not the Carfora
  Carmine. The Forchia name index has no marriage of a Carmine Annecchino (Forchia's marriages are indexed from 1931 only; the
  marriage registers 1866–1930 are NOT on Antenati) and no 1943 death (Antenati's Forchia deaths stop at 1865 and resume
  1931–1942). **What settles it is a marriage act (age, parents) or the 1943 death act — both via FamilySearch's Benevento civil
  registration, collection 2475030, or the Archivio di Stato di Benevento.**

## 2. TWO men named Carmine Annecchino fathered children at Forchia in September 1883
Forchia, *Nati* 1883 = `an_ud2011514` (Benevento, Stato civile italiano, 41 images; the `an_ud2011518` ark is the same volume).
- **Act 35 (image 26, lower half): registered 6 Sep 1883** — Carmine Annecchino, **di anni trenta**, contadino; wife **Maria
  Giovanna Crisci**; son **Antonio**, born 11.15 that morning, via Mis[…] no. 5 (street not certain).
- **Act 37 (image 28): registered 21 Sep 1883** — Carmine Annecchino, **di anni ventisette**, contadino; wife **Maria Carfora**;
  daughter **Giacinta**, born 3.30 pm on the 18th, in a masseria (name not certain).
- The old note («the Crisci Carmine may be the same man remarried») is answered: **no**. Ages 30 and 27 give births c.1853 and
  c.1856; the Carfora one is the niche man's household, but 27 in 1883 is a year short of «1857» (ages are guessed — not an objection).
- The tree's «Carmine Annecchino Annecchino» × «Giovanna Crisci (Annecchino)», child Maria Filomena b. 7 Jun 1878, is probably the
  Crisci Carmine. **Not joined**: «Giovanna» is not «Maria Giovanna», nothing reads the 1878 act's mother. The 1878 and 1880
  index births to a father of the name are attributed to neither man.

## 3. How this volume is laid out (so the next read is quick)
- One **portrait page per image**, number and child's name in the LEFT margin box, text to its right; **two acts per page**, an
  act running over onto the next page (act 35 starts at the bottom of image 26 and ends at the top of 27).
- Act numbers run to 45; Parte I ends about image 30 and a **supplement** starts at image 31 (acts 40–45, Nov–Dec). The annual
  table (*indice annuale*) is the LAST image (41) — read it first; it gives act numbers by surname.
