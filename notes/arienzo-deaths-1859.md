# ARIENZO DEATHS 1859 — read from the year's own INDEX, and no Vincenzo Falco

**14 September 2026.** The archive's named next step for **generation three** was the Arienzo civil
death registers **after 1858**, never read. Two things were established before a single act was opened.

## The series stops at 1865

Antenati holds **57 Arienzo death volumes** and they run **1809 to 1865**. **There are no post-1865
Arienzo civil deaths on Antenati at all** — so this route covers **seven years only**, and anything
later must come from FamilySearch's Caserta collections or from extracts quoted inside processetti.
(The 1883 death of Pasquale Falco surfaced exactly that way, inside a marriage dossier.)

## The 1859 volume opens with a TAVOLA ALFABETICA

`an_ua14361`, 68 images — and the **first leaves are an alphabetical index of the year's dead**, with
columns for **act number, name, patria, profession, THE PARENTS' NAMES, and the date**.

**That is one page per year instead of sixty-eight**, and it names parents, which the acts themselves
would take a full reading to yield.

## The complete F section for 1859

| act | who | profession | parents | died |

| **19** | **FALCO MARIA** | contadina | **Michele** and **d'Ambrogio Giovanna** | **4 March** |

| **60** | **FALCO RAFFAELE** | contadino | **Michele** and **d'Ambrogio Maria Giovanna** | **17 August** |

**No Vincenzo Falco. 1859 is eliminated.**

## And three children for two households the archive already had

Both Micheles are already on `/which-falco`, and both gain children:

- **MICHELE FALCO × MARIA GIOVANNA D'AMBROSIO** — the archive knew only «their daughter Antonia was
  born 19 November 1850». **Now a daughter MARIA (d. 4 March 1859) and a son RAFFAELE (d. 17 August
  1859).**
- **MICHELE FALCO × ANTONIA VIGLIOTTA** — the archive knew only «their daughter Chiara died 6 July
  1846, aged seven». **Now a daughter CARMELA (d. 4 August 1859).**

**Neither household is claimed as kin to the line.** They are recorded so that the next Falco found at
Arienzo can be checked against them.

## Method note — the tavola moves

**1859 has its index at the FRONT. 1860 does not** — that volume runs straight into the acts and was
still running at image 58 of 60. The archive has already recorded a «tavola bound at the front» for
1850. **Check both ends of every volume before concluding there is no index**, and do not assume the
binder was consistent from one year to the next.
