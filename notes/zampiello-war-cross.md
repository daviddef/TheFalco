# Angelo Zampiello and the Croce al Merito di Guerra

**The claim, as the family carried it:** that Angelo Zampiello served in the Second Italo-Abyssinian
War in 1935 and was decorated — with the family adding that it was for saving many lives. The site
carried this as *"still uncorroborated."*

**It was corroborated all along**, by a document in the family's own scans that this archive had
filed as "a second portrait."

## The certificate

> **MINISTERO DELL'AFRICA ITALIANA** · Numero d'Ordine 18745
>
> *Il Ministro per l'Africa Italiana*
>
> *Visto il R. Decreto 2 luglio 1936-XIV, n. 1712, e successive modificazioni, che estendono
> l'applicazione delle norme riguardanti la concessione della croce al merito di guerra al personale
> che dal 3 ottobre 1935-XIII abbia preso parte ad operazioni militari in Africa Orientale;*
>
> *Con suo Decreto in data 14 Settembre 1938 ha conferito la*
>
> **CROCE AL MERITO DI GUERRA**
>
> *all'operaio* **ZAMPIELLO ANGELO** *di Tommaso*
>
> *Roma, addì 24 Settembre 1938 — Anno XVI* · *Il Ministro* [signed]

## In English
The Ministry of Italian Africa, citing the royal decree of 2 July 1936 which extended the War Merit
Cross to personnel who had taken part in military operations in East Africa **from 3 October 1935** —
the day Italian forces crossed into Ethiopia — conferred the Cross by decree of 14 September 1938 on
**the workman Angelo Zampiello, son of Tommaso**. Rome, 24 September 1938.

## What it establishes, and what it does not

**Established**
- He was in **Italian East Africa** and took part in operations from the 1935 campaign.
- He was **decorated**: the Croce al Merito di Guerra, conferred 14 September 1938.
- His father was **Tommaso Zampiello** — a generation this archive did not have.
- He was there as an ***operaio*** — a **workman**, not a soldier. Italy sent tens of thousands of
  labourers to East Africa to build the roads the campaign ran on, and the 1936 decree exists
  precisely to extend a military award to them.

**Not established**
- The family's account that the decoration was **for saving many lives**. The Croce al Merito di
  Guerra is a war *merit* cross, given for service in a theatre of operations. An act of gallantry
  would ordinarily carry a *Medaglia al Valor Militare* instead. The bravery story may be true and
  separately awarded, or it may be the family's gloss on this certificate. **Nothing here proves it.**

## Where to look next
The **foglio matricolare** for his class at the Archivio di Stato di Benevento would give his unit,
his dates in Africa and any other awards. Note that a man born 31 August 1895 belongs to the class of
1895 and would have been called up for the **First World War** at twenty; that service is entirely
untraced, and is the larger gap.
