# Maria Arricale, d. 18 September 1901 (Arienzo Morti 1901 Parte I act 68; MC55-RM9 image 117)

Read from the FamilySearch image (collection 2043630), 15 November 2026, level 12 with level-13 zooms z895, z896.

Eighteen months old, of Raffaele, trainante, and Costanza Falco, fruttivendola, at Corso Caudino 88. Placed in `luigi-falco-carolina-cimmino` as the girl born 22 March 1900 (Nascite 1900 act 22): the parents' names, the street where the couple's son died in 1904, and an age that is exact.

**It settles a doubt about the Maria Arricale of 15 December 1908** (Morti 1908 act 89, six years old): she cannot be the same child, who was already dead in 1901. The 1908 child was born about 1902, a second daughter given the dead sister's name.

Not placed: the second declarant, Pasquale Falco, 32, trainante. The act does not say how he was related; another Pasquale Falco, trainante, 37, witnessed a birth earlier in 1901 (Nascite act 30). Leads only.
