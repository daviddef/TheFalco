# ANTONIA ARRICALE, born 9 April 1832 — and she is NOT Giovanna Arricale's sister

**Found 23 September 2026. The candidate was raised by the Arienzo marriage register for 1859 and killed by the Arienzo processetti for the same year, one image apart.**

## The lead

**Arienzo, Matrimoni 1859, act 10** (`an_ua14263` img 12r): **ANTONIA ARRICALE**, twenty-seven, of Arienzo, marries **ANGELO MAGLIATO**, twenty-seven, of **San Felice**, «*figlia di **FRANCESCO ARRICALE**, di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi, e di **ANGELA PIGNATELLI**, domiciliata ivi*».

**Work-list row 9 is looking for the siblings of GIOVANNA ARRICALE** — generation five's wife, born **22 December 1848** to **FRANCESCO ARRICALE and PASQUA FALCO** of **strada Capodiconca**. *Same surname. Same street. Same trade. A father of the same forename.* It looked like a sister.

## The document that settles it

**`an_ua14206` — Arienzo PROCESSETTI 1859, 350 images.** `tools/isitread.py` found **no mention of this volume anywhere in the archive**: no coverage row, no work-list row, no note. Genuinely untouched.

**Bundle 10 runs images 123–133** and holds the groom's birth act (San Felice), three *estratti di morte*, two *estratti di notificazione*, the parish certificate, the parents' consent — and, at **image 127**, a certified copy of **Arienzo, Nati 1832, atto 37**:

> «*ATTO DI NASCITA. Num.º d'ordine **TRENTASETTE**. L'anno mille ottocento **TRENTADUE**, il dì **NOVE** del mese di **APRILE**, ad ore **QUATTORDICI**, avanti di noi **FRANCESCO D'AMBROGIO Sindaco** ed uffiziale dello Stato Civile del Comune di **CORPO D'ARIENZO**, distretto di **CASERTA** … è comparsa **CATARINA RUGGIERO, di anni SESSANTOTTO, di professione LEVATRICE**, domiciliata **ivi strada PORTA DI SOPRA**, quale ci à presentato una **FEMINA** … ed à dichiarato che **essa è nata da ANGELA PIGNATELLI, di anni TRENTA** … domiciliata **ivi strada CAPODICONCA**, **e da FRANCESCO ARRICALE, MARITO DELLA STESSA, di anni TRENTA, di professione CONTADINO**, domiciliato con detta sua moglie*»

and the *Indicazione* column names the child outright:

> «*il Sagramento del battesimo è stato amministrato **AD ANTONIA ARRICALE** nel giorno **nove Aprile** corrente anno*»

## TWO MEN OF ONE NAME ON ONE STREET, TWENTY-TWO YEARS APART

| | **Antonia's father** | **Giovanna's father** |
|---|---|---|
| act | Nati **1832**, atto 37 | Nati **1848**, atto 114 |
| stated age | «*di anni **TRENTA***» → b. c.**1802** | «*di anni **VENTIQUATTRO***» → b. c.**1824** |
| wife | **Angela Pignatelli**, 30 | **Pasqua Falco**, 25 |
| street | **CAPODICONCA** | **CAPODICONCA** |
| trade | contadino | contadino |

**A man born in 1824 was eight years old in 1832.** *These are two different men, and the stated ages are doing the work of a document here rather than a guess — this archive has shown the Arienzo clerks wander by up to five years, and twenty-two is far outside that.* → [[ages-are-guessed]]

**ANTONIA ARRICALE IS NOT GIOVANNA ARRICALE'S SISTER.**

**SHE MAY WELL BE HER AUNT.** The elder Francesco, born about 1802, is exactly the right age to be the father of the younger, born about 1824. **That is a candidate and it is not claimed.** What would settle it is **the younger Francesco Arricale's own birth act, about 1824**, which would name his parents. → [[wrong-negatives]]

## Two things worth keeping besides

**BOTH PARENTS WERE ALIVE ON 3 APRIL 1859, BY A DOCUMENT AND NOT BY SILENCE.** Image 128 of the bundle is the consent: «*si sono presentati **FRANCESCO ARRICALE, ed ANGELA [PIGNATELLI]** … contadini di detto comune, [i quali] prestano il loro pieno **consenso** … [alla] figlia **ANTONIA ARRICALE***». **They appeared in person.** That is worth more than any «no fu» inference → [[quondam-not-evidence]].

**BOTH ARRICALE GIRLS WERE DECLARED BY A MIDWIFE, NOT BY THEIR FATHER** — **Catarina Ruggiero**, sixty-eight, *levatrice* of strada Porta di Sopra, in 1832; **Rosa Morgillo**, forty-eight, *levatrice* of strada San Filippo Neri, in 1848. *A midwife-declared act still names both parents in the «è nata da … e da …» clause, so it is not a case of the hidden father* → [[birth-act-bands]].

## THE METHOD NOTE, AND IT IS THE CHEAP PART

**Row 9 proposes sweeping birth *tavole* across fifteen years.** This took **one image**.

**A marriage dossier holds the bride's birth act already**, quoted in full with both parents and their ages. So **for any person who married at Arienzo between 1809 and 1865, the processetti answer the parentage question in one image instead of a volume** — `an_ua14149` (1809) … `an_ua14211` (1865), **bundle *n* = marriage act *n***, about thirteen images per bundle, so bundle *n* begins near image **13n − 11**. Bundle 10 of 1859 began at 123; the formula predicted 119. → [[processetti-method]]
