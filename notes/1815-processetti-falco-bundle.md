## THE BAPTISM — image 128, read at native resolution (3562 px)

> Testor Ego infrascriptus Parochus Ecclesiae Parochialis **S. FELICIS MARTYRIS** hujus Terrae
> Argentii, **Diocesis S. AGATHAE GOTHORUM**, in Provincia Terrae Laboris, qualiter perquisito
> **libro VII baptizatorum** dictae Parochiae inveni **fol. 148** sequentem adnotationem: —
> **Die vigesima septima m[ensis] Februarii millesimo septingentesimo nonagesimo septimo 1797** —
> Argentii, Admodum Rev. D. **Didacus Primicerius Petrone** de meo infrascripti Parochi licentia
> sacro fonte expiavit **infantem natum ex legitimis Conjugibus SEBASTIANO FALCO et COLUMBA
> MONTEFUSCO** dictae Parochiae, cui…

**LUIGI FALCO was baptised on 27 February 1797** — exactly the birth his stated age of eighteen in
1815 implies.

**And it names the parish and the diocese.** The San Felice Falco belonged to the parish of
**SAN FELICE MARTIRE**, in the **DIOCESE OF SANT'AGATA DE' GOTI** — a jurisdiction this archive has
not worked in. Their books are cited here as *liber VII baptizatorum*, folio 148.

## The banns — images 132–140

The sindaco of the «**Comune de' CASALI UNITI d'Arienzo**» certifies that, on notice from the
sindaco of the Comune Corpo dated **4 March 1815**, he published the banns on two consecutive
Sundays under article 63 of the Civil Code, for

> «…il Matrimonio che vogliono contrarre **LUIGI FALCO, nativo, e domiciliato in questa detta
> Comune de' Casali Uniti, strada detta la CROCE [di San Felice]**…»

**A third name for the same town.** Its own clerks call it the *Comune de' sei Casali d'Arienzo*,
the *Comune de' Casali Uniti d'Arienzo*, and it is catalogued on Antenati as *San Felice (oggi San
Felice a Cancello)*. Any one of the three will hide it from a search for the others.

## What the bundle does NOT contain, and why

**No certificate naming Sebastiano Falco's parents.** That was the reason for opening it, and the
absence is structural rather than accidental: **both of Luigi's parents were alive and consented in
person**, so the Code required no death certificates on his side. Compare bundle 13 of 1814, where
the bride's parents and grandfather were all dead and their extracts reach back to 1789.

> **The route to Sebastiano Falco's parents is therefore NOT this bundle.** It is a death act for
> Sebastiano or Colomba in San Felice's own registers — now reachable, and now with **27
> «Morti, indice» volumes** to search instead of the registers themselves.
