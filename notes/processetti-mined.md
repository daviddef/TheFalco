# THE PROCESSETTI, MINED — 1,431 pages, and exactly four Arienzo Falco acts

**14 September 2026.** Collection **2043630** — the *allegati* to the Caserta marriage registers,
1866–1929 — holds **1,503 Falco pages** for Arienzo. **1,431 were harvested** and their machine
transcripts searched for the extracts bound into the dossiers.

## What the corpus actually yields

**Most Falco mentions in this collection are not this family and not even this town.** A Falco is
frequently the **registrar**: *Giuseppe de Falco, sindaco of **Caserta*** signs birth extracts through
the 1860s; *Falco Raffaele, consigliere comunale of **Caivano***, signs death acts of 1903, 1904 and
1907; *Falco Giacomo, assessore of **Sessa Aurunca***, signs in 1900. **Reading a name without reading
its role would have produced a dozen false ancestors.**

Sorted by comune, the **death** extracts naming a Falco come from **Castel Campagnano, Sessa Aurunca,
Caivano, Caserta, Aversa** — and **only two from Arienzo**.

| | Arienzo Falco acts found in the whole corpus |
|---|---|
| **Deaths** | **1883 act 60 — Pasquale Falco** · **1909 act 70 — Clementina Falco** |
| **Births** | **1873 act 39 — Raffaele Falco** · **1877 act 20 — Pasqualina Falco** |

**Four acts.** That is the true yield, and it is worth stating plainly so the collection is not
re-mined in hope.

## The find: a household documented twice, six years apart

**Birth, 1 February 1877** (`3QS7-897N-1D2H`):

> «*è comparso **PASQUALE FALCO, di anni TRENTOTTO, contadino**, domiciliato in questo Comune, il quale
> mi ha dichiarato che … del dì primo del corrente mese, **nella casa posta in VIA CAMELLARA**, da
> **MARIA AMALIA GUIDA sua moglie, contadina, seco lui convivente, è nato un bambino di sesso
> femminile** … e le dà il nome di **PASQUALINA***.» Witnesses **Biondo Silvestro**, 58, and
> **Domenico Ruotolo**, 42.

**Death, 26 September 1883** (`3QS7-897N-1DLW`):

> «*nella casa posta in **VIA CAMELLARA al numero CINQUE**, **è morto PASQUALE FALCO, di anni
> QUARANTAQUATTRO, agricoltore** … **del fu FRANCESCO** … **e della fu RAFFAELA FALCO** … **marito di
> MARIA AMALIA GUIDA***.»

**The same man: thirty-eight in 1877, forty-four in 1883, born about 1839.** So three generations of a
collateral Falco household stand on the family's own street:

> **FRANCESCO FALCO × RAFFAELA FALCO** (she died 1854; he was dead by 1883)
> → **PASQUALE FALCO** (b. c. 1839, d. 26 Sep 1883) **× MARIA AMALIA GUIDA**
> → **PASQUALINA FALCO** (b. 1 February 1877, via Camellara)

**A discrepancy, unresolved:** the birth act gives the house as «*al numero ventic[inque]*» and the
death act as «*al numero cinque*». Either the family moved along the street, or the machine has eaten
a syllable. **Not decided here.**

## And a second Arienzo Falco household

**Death, September 1909, act 70** (`3QS7-997N-55J`):

> «*… Comune di **ARIENZO**, sono comparsi **Giuseppe Crisci**, di anni cinquantasei, calzolaio … nella
> casa posta in **VIA ORTICELLI al numero VENTI**, **è morta CLEMENTINA [FALCO] di anni CINQUANTADUE**,
> nata in Arienzo, residente in Arienzo, **del fu MATT[EO]***.»

**CLEMENTINA FALCO, born about 1857, daughter of a MATTEO FALCO**, died at **via Orticelli 20**.

**Not the founder's Matteo**, who died in 1802. **A later Matteo Falco of Arienzo, dead by 1909** —
and **Via Orticelli is the street generation five lived on** («Via degli Orti», 1873). **No kinship is
claimed**; the household is recorded so the next Matteo Falco found at Arienzo can be checked against
it.
