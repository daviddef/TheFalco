# Andrea Falco, died 15 March 1901 (Morti 1901 act 19)

Found 15 November 2026 on the full Arienzo walk (MC55-RM9 image 105, right page). Francesco Crisci (28, calzolaio) and Gennaro Perrotta (30, sarto) declared on 16 March 1901 that at 7.40 in the evening of the 15th, at via Sant'Andrea 15, «è morto Andrea Falco, di anni settantanove, possidente, residente in Arienzo, nato in Arienzo, dal fu Vincenzo … e dalla fu Andreana Crisci … marito di Carmela Buono». Read at level 12 and the deceased's line, the parents and the wife at level 13 (z890), the declarants at level 13 (z891).

**Why he is this house's Andrea.** Four things, each independent: his name; both parents, the late Vincenzo Falco and the late Andreana Crisci, who head the household he is a child of; his wife's name, Carmela Buono; and his age, seventy-nine, which is exact for a man born on 1 December 1821. The act is the one the join of his two households had been waiting for: it said «no act yet says figlio di Vincenzo of the calessiere himself», and this one does.

**What is new.** His death, 79 years and three months after his birth; his trade as *possidente* (the births of 1847 to 1865 call him contadino and calessiere); and that his wife, «marito di», was alive on the day. Nothing is said about his children.

**Placed with it.** Gioacchino Falco, 55, contadino, is the first declarant in Morti 1901 act 13 (19 February 1901): the dead woman, Maria Rosa Morgillo, died at via Orticelli 8, the house where Gioacchino Falco's household recorded its births and deaths from 1878 to 1891, and where other Falco households recorded theirs to 1895. Placed in the household of Gioacchino Falco and Andreana Crisci as a declarant, on the name, the trade (contadino, as in 1891) and the house. A Gioacchino Falco of the same age and trade witnessed Nascite 1901 act 21 three weeks earlier with no street; that is a lead, not claimed.

Not claimed: how Maria Rosa Morgillo was related to him. The act does not say.
