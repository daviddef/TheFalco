# A SEVENTH CHILD FOR GENERATION SIX — Raffaele Falco marries at Forchia, 1937

**Forchia (BN), *Atti di Matrimonio — Parte II, Serie A*, 1937, atto 12** — `an_ua2008823`, image 19;
nominative `an_ud2008887`. **Read from the image, 13 September 2026.**

The civil register transcribing a church marriage celebrated in the parish of **SAN NICOLA DI MIRA**,
Forchia:

> «… L'anno millenovecentotrent**asette** – XV, addì **VENTOTTO di OTTOBRE** … a ore ant. sei e minuti
> quindici, in **Forchia**, sono stati uniti in matrimonio secondo il rito **Santa Romana Chiesa**:
>
> **1) FALCO RAFFAELE**, di anni **ventuno**, di professione **contadino**, **nato in ARPAIA**,

>
> **2) CRISCI FRANCESCA**, di anni ventuno, di professione **contadina**, nata in «Arienzo S. Felice»
> [?], residente in Forchia, **figlia di PIO e di DE LUCIA MARIA**.»
>
> Notice of the transcription sent to the parish priest **8 November 1937**. Officer: **Balzarano
> Domenico, Podestà**.

## What it adds

**«figlio di RAFFAELE e di MAIONE COSTANZA» is generation six's household** — Raffaele Falco
(b. Arienzo, 6 April 1873) and Costanza Maione (b. Arienzo, 2 May 1876), the parents of Carminantonio.

The children this archive could name were **Giovanna (1898), Carminantonio (1901), Anna del Giudice
(1904), Gerardo (1913)**, and **Giacoma** and **Aniello** undated. **This groom is a seventh:
RAFFAELE, born at ARPAIA about 1916**, a *contadino*, who married **Francesca Crisci** at Forchia on
28 October 1937 and was living at Forchia then — **as was his father, «residente in Forchia»**, aged
about sixty-four.

**And it strengthens the migration story with a second fixed point.** Gerardo was born at **Arpaia in
1913** — long treated here as «the first sign of the move north». A brother born at **Arpaia about
1916** puts the household there for at least three years, before it appears at **Forchia** in the
1930s. **Arienzo → Arpaia → Forchia** is now three places with records at each.

## AND A NAME THAT NEEDS SEPARATING

This archive says elsewhere that **«Carminantonio's son Raffaele reached Brisbane, married Caterina
Zampiello and is buried at Nudgee»**.

**This is not that man.** The groom of 1937 is **twenty-one**, born at Arpaia, and explicitly **the son
of Raffaele and Costanza Maione** — so he is **Carminantonio's BROTHER**, and he married a **Crisci**,
not a Zampiello.

**There are two Raffaele Falco in consecutive generations**, both in this family, and the archive had
been carrying only one. Whether the Brisbane Raffaele is Carminantonio's son — as the tree says — is
untouched by this act; **what is now certain is that the 1937 groom is not him.**

## Not read, and worth reading

The bride's birthplace is written **«Arienzo S. Felice»**, which also appears in a neighbouring act of
the same volume («nato in Arienzo San Felice, residente in Arienzo San Felice»). **Whether that is a
frazione of Arienzo or the clerk's way of writing San Felice a Cancello is not settled here.**
