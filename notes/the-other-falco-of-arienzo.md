# THE OTHER FALCO OF ARIENZO — a *Don*, a nun and a priest

**Read 13 September 2026** from the Arienzo death indexes of **1850** and **1857**.

Four generations of this archive's Falco are **bracciali**, **contadini** and **coloni**. Most of the
men who declared their deaths were marked *«non sapere scrivere»*. They live on **strada Camellara**.

**There is another Falco family in the same town, and it is nothing like them.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **DON FRANCESCO FALCO × DONNA MADDALENA STELLATO** | dead by 1857 |
| └── **DONNA ANGELA MARIA FALCO, *MONACA*** | died **14 November 1850** |
| └── **DON GIACOMO FALCO, *SACERDOTE*** | died **20 June 1857** *(Arienzo, Morti 1857, index atto 43: «Falco Sac. D. Giacomo di fu D. Francesco e D. Maddalena»)* |

**A household with a *Don* for a father, a *Donna* for a mother, a daughter in a convent and a son in
orders.** The index entries are all this archive has of them — **neither act has been read** — and
**no kinship to this line is claimed or suspected.**

> **Why it is worth a page of its own.** For four years this archive has read every Falco it found at
> Arienzo as a possible relation. It is now certain that **the surname carries at least two social
> worlds in one small town**, and probably two unrelated stocks. **The name identifies nobody.** What
> identifies people here is the street, the trade, the parish and the spouse — and this archive has
> the first three and uses them.

## And a separate coincidence that is not one

**DON GIOVANNI FALCO, DIACONO**, who died in 1849, is **not** of this house. His father is
**GIUSEPPE FALCO, *colono*** of strada Camellara — the founder's own son. A labourer's boy and a
gentleman's boy, both in orders, in the same parish, dying eight years apart. The archive nearly
filed them together on the strength of the *Don*.

## GIUSEPPE FALCO IS STILL ALIVE

**He is in none of the fifteen years above.** He was alive and named as a living father in **November
1849** and again in **April 1854**, when he was about **seventy-four**. **Fifteen consecutive years of
Arienzo deaths have now been read out, and he is not in them.**

Next: **1859–1865** (`an_ua14361`–`an_ua14367`), then the post-1865 Italian series — which, unlike the
Bourbon one, **is** full-text searchable on FamilySearch (collection `2043630`, 1,503 Falco pages for
Arienzo alone).

## And a third child for Crescenzo Falco and Maria Morgillo

They lost **Giuseppe (1)** and **Gennaro (2 days)** on consecutive days in June 1848. **ANTONIO** died
**30 October 1857**. Index only; the act has not been read.
