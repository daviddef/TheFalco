# WHY FILOMENA'S BIRTH ACT WAS NEVER FOUND — the volume is not online

**13 September 2026.** Enumerated Antenati's **complete** holdings for Forchia, comune by comune,
fondo by fondo, instead of guessing arks.

## Forchia is much larger on Antenati than this archive thought

**500 registers**, held by **TWO archives** and spanning **three fondi**:

- **Archivio di Stato di CASERTA** — *Stato civile napoleonico e della restaurazione*, **1809–1865**.
  (Forchia was in **Terra di Lavoro** until the province of Benevento was created in 1861.)
- **Archivio di Stato di BENEVENTO** — the same period in **duplicate** (the *Archivio Suppletorio*,
  often bundled: 1809-1810, 1813-1819, 1820-1824, 1825-1829 …), **and then**
- **Archivio di Stato di BENEVENTO — *STATO CIVILE ITALIANO*, 1866 onward.**

**The research queue recorded this last fondo as «not located».** It is located. It is on Antenati.

## The births, year by year, and the hole in them

Births in the *Stato civile italiano* run:

> 1866 · 1867 · 1868 … 1889 · 1890 · 1891 · 1892 · 1893 · 1894 · 1895 · 1896 · **_____** · 1898 ·
> 1899 · 1900 · 1901 · 1902 · 1903 · 1904 · 1910 · 1912 · 1913 · 1914 · 1915

**THERE IS NO 1897.** Not at Benevento, not at Caserta, not in any fondo.

**Filomena Annecchino was born on 10 April 1897.** Her act has been hunted for weeks across
FamilySearch and Antenati and the archive blamed *«FamilySearch's paging limit and fuzzy matching
across Annecchino, Annecchini and Anicchino»*. **That was the wrong explanation. The volume is not
digitised, and no amount of searching was ever going to find it.**

This had been *inferred* before, from ark arithmetic — the shelf-marks skip the year. It is now
**read off the holdings list**, which is a different and better kind of evidence.

## The routes that remain — and neither of them is a search

1. **The register itself**, at the **Archivio di Stato di Benevento**, unscanned. Needs a person in
   the building, or a paid order.
2. **THE PROCESSETTI — and this is the better bet, because they are already digitised.** Forchia has
   **73 processetti volumes**, beginning in 1809. **A marriage dossier copies out the bride's BIRTH
   EXTRACT in full**, so **Filomena's marriage to Carminantonio Falco would reproduce the very act
   that cannot be read directly.** What is not yet known is **how late the Forchia processetti run** —
   the enumeration was cut short when Antenati began throttling. **That is the next question, and it
   is one query.**

**Birth INDEXES are not a route**: Forchia has 30 of them and they stop at **1849**.

## Method

`https://antenati.cultura.gov.it/search-registry/?localita=Forchia&tipologia=Nati&s_page=N` —
**`s_page` pages, ten at a time**, and the result card gives the year, the fondo and the holding
archive. **Enumerate the holdings before concluding a record is missing**, and **do not reason from
ark arithmetic alone**: the arks for Forchia are split across two archives, so consecutive numbering
means much less than it appears to.

Antenati throttles the search endpoint after a few dozen pages while continuing to serve images.
Back off and resume.
