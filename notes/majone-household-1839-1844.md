# FOUR GENERATIONS OF MAJONE, JOINED BY TWO SOURCES THAT NEVER MET

Found **13 September 2026**, and found only because the 1844 marriage acts were checked against what
the archive already held in the same pass that published them.

## The death act, already in the archive since the 1839 sweep

> **FILIPPO MAJONE, 70, contadino, «marito di Maria Martini», strada Capodiconca, died 16 November
> 1839 at the eighth hour** — son of the late **BARTOLOMEO MAJONE, contadino**, and of **Francesca
> Diglio, defunta**. Declared by Nicola Abbatecola, 46, contadino, and Vincenzo Ruggiero, 54,
> vaticale, both of Capodiconca. *(Arienzo death register 1839, `an_ua14341`, image 59.)*

Born about **1769**.

## The marriage acts, read today

Three grooms of 1844, each act naming his parents:

| act | date | groom | age | «figlio di» |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 20 May 1844 | **BARTOLOMEO MAJONE** | c. 22 | Filippo Majone dec., contadino, and Maria Martini [?] |
| 17 | Sept 1844 | **CLEMENTE MAJONE** | 33 | Filippo Majone dec., contadino, and Maria Martini [?] |
| 26 | 24 Nov 1844 | **RAFFAELE MAJONE** | 37 | Filippo Majone dec., colono, and Maria Martino [?] |

**Same father, same mother, same year.** Three brothers married within six months of each other,
five years after their father's death — which is what an estate being settled looks like from the
outside.

## And the thing that makes it more than a coincidence of names

**The eldest generation names the youngest.** Filippo's father was **BARTOLOMEO**; Filippo's son is
**BARTOLOMEO**. That is the Neapolitan naming custom working exactly as it should, and it is
independent corroboration of a reading — «Maria Martini» — that was marked `[?]` when it went in.

Ages fit without strain: Filippo born c. 1769, his sons c. 1807, c. 1811 and c. 1822.

## What is NOT claimed

- **GIOVANNA MAJONE**, 27, of strada Stella, who married Antonio Bartolomeo Giordano [?] on
  12 June 1844 (act 10), is «figlia di … Majone **defunto**». Her father's forename was not read with
  confidence. **She may be a fourth child of this house and she is not entered as one.**
- Whether the mother is **Martini** or **Martino** is unsettled; both readings are in the acts.

## AND IT ANSWERS A STANDING QUESTION — partly

The research queue has carried: *«Test whether the **Maione** of Arienzo and the **Maione** of Arpaia
are one kindred.»*

**Two of Filippo's three sons are recorded as domiciled outside Arienzo, toward Arpaia** —
Bartolomeo (act 8) and Clemente (act 17) both «domiciliato in **Rocca d'Arpaia [?] strada della
Stella**», while their brother Raffaele stayed at strada Stella in Arienzo. **The place-name was not
read with confidence and is marked so.** But if it holds, the Majone of Arpaia are not a separate
kindred at all: **they are Arienzo sons who moved eight kilometres up the valley** — the same
short-range drift the Falco themselves made two generations later, when Raffaele Falco's last child
was born at Arpaia in 1913 instead of Arienzo.

**That is a lead, not a finding.** The test is the Arpaia registers.
