# GIUSEPPE FALCO AND ANTONIA DI GUIDA — seven children, 1805 to 1825

**The founder's son and his wife**, married at Arienzo on **4 August 1802**. Until today this archive
held **two of their children**. It now holds **seven**.

> **How much weight this carries.** The five baptisms below come from **FamilySearch's machine
> transcription** of the Arienzo parish registers. **The images have not been read.** Names are
> legible and internally consistent; **the days in particular are not verified**, and the machine has
> already been caught this week turning 1802 into 1902 and 1774 into 1777. The two civil records are
> read from the images.

**Twenty years of childbearing**, 1805 to 1825, from a marriage of 1802.

## Two things it settles

**1. The infant Raffaele's year was unknown; it is 1811.** The archive had a parish burial «Raphael
Falco filius Josephi e Antonia di Guida, infans, 26 February» with **no year**. The baptism ten days
earlier is on the same leaf, and the marginal note ties them: **born and buried in February 1811.**

**2. DON GIOVANNI FALCO WAS TWENTY-FOUR, NOT TWENTY-FIVE.** His death act of 26 November 1849 gives
his age as **venticinque**. A baptism of **April 1825** makes him **twenty-four**. *One more stated
age overturned by a baptism — the fifth this week.* **But the day of the baptism has not been read**,
and if it were read as March the arithmetic would still give twenty-four.

## And the archpriest changes

The 1805–1818 acts are signed **Hyacinthus Archipresbyter MORGILLO** — the same archpriest who
licensed Giuseppe's own marriage in 1802. By **1825** the signature is **Can. ANGELO M. SCARPATI**.
Useful for dating an undated leaf.
