# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1841 — read end to end by a delegated reader (`an_ua14438`)

> **25 October 2026.** The roster below is a finding aid: only the Falco acts the households file cites as READ FROM THE IMAGE were re-opened by the archive's own hand at ~2,800 px. Everything else is triage-grade.

**Ark:** an_ua14438. **Images:** 117.

## Year proof (from the manuscript)
- Image 1R, printed title page: «Registro degli atti dello stato civile. Degli atti di Nascita ... Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento quarantuno [the final word reads 'quarantuno']. Il presente volume contiene fogli duecento[quattordici]». Provincia di Terra di Lavoro, Distretto di Caserta, Circondario/Comune di Arienzo. Pencil '124'.
- Act 3 (image 4R): «L'anno mille ottocento quarantuno il dì tredici del mese di Gennajo» (first act, act 1, is of 5 Jan 1841).
- Act 99 (image 105R): «L'anno mille ottocento quarantuno il dì primo del mese di Dicembre».
- Closing certificate (image 116L): «L'anno mille ottocento quarantuno, il dì trentuno del mese di Dicembre, noi [Raffaele de' Musso Andrea?] ... Ufficiale dello Stato Civile del Comune di Arienzo ... abbiamo chiuso e firmato il presente registro di Nascite, di fogli scritti num. duecentoquattordici, contenente atti num. centosette.»

## Run
- First act: no. 1, 5 Jan 1841 (images 2-3). Last filled act: no. 107, 30 Dec 1841 (images 113-114).
- **107 acts, numbers 1..107 unbroken** (checked against the TSV: no gap, no repeat). Act 108 is a printed blank form («N. d'ord. cento otto», images 114R-115L), with a further blank form on 115L (page 216).
- The count 107 is confirmed by the officer's own closing certificate (116L, 'atti num. centosette').
- Officer's signature: Raffaele ... (de' Musso Andrea?) with the clerk's second signature 'Felice Morgillo, Cancelliere' under every act (the 'f. Morgillo fatto' flourish is the clerk, not a Falco).

## Layout
Act N = right page of image k (head, declarant, Indicazione of baptism) + left page of image k+1 (mother, father/'da lui dichiarante', date/hour of birth, child's name, witnesses, margin column). Layout is constant through the volume. Page numbers printed top outer corner (R page odd, L even), pencil folio numbers beside them.

## Duplicate frames (same spread filmed twice, not extra acts)
Images 31 (=30), 69 (=68), 73 (=72), 77 (=76), 78 (=76; so 76/77/78 are a triple filming). All seen. Rows 'dup' in the TSV.

## Tavola
At the BACK, and almost illegible: image 115R is a printed blank tavola form (page 233); image 116L is its verso, carrying the closing certificate; the handwritten tavola ink is on image 117L (page 234 verso) and shows through MIRRORED on 116R. Flipped and contrast-stretched, 116R reads 'Tavola alfabetica annuale de' nati', columns 'Cognomi e nomi de' nati' / 'de' genitori', and the legible lines are a page of C surnames (Crisci ...). It is ONE leaf only; no F page could be read or seen (the leaf is the C page, and the ink is too faint to read the rest). The tavola was therefore NOT used to cross-check Falco. Image 117R is a printed ministerial notice (Napoli 4 Luglio 1849) with a blank Procuratore del Re form (page 235).

## Damage / other
Right-hand pages of many images are shadowed by the binding and the paper-string tie; no loss of text at the act lines except where marked '?' in the TSV. Hours and ages are often only fair; copy as written.

## Near-misses cleared
- Act 66 (images 68R/70L, 30 Jul 1841), witness 'Carmine Fraja/Fraia' 4x contadino strada Capodicona: Fraia, not Falco (re-read at 3000 px).
- Act 101 (108L): declarant Clemente Crisci, witness Giuseppe Crisci (not Cioffi, not Falco).
- Act 107 (114L): witness 'Luca Crisci?' (C-shaped initial, 6 letters).
- Act 102-107: no F-surnames at all.
- Second signature 'Felice Morgillo Cancelliere' is on every act.

## Limitations
- Rows for acts 1-97 were written in the first pass; the second pass re-viewed them and the TSV marks '?' where letters are doubtful. Names, ages and hours of many non-Falco acts are fair, not certain (no verbatim transcription was required for them).
- Tavola unusable (above).
- Act 32 margin: the spouse's given name ('Benev[e]?to') is doubtful; surname Falco is clear.
- Act 98 witness 'Giuseppe Falco' is fair rather than clear: the initial F and the final -lco are in the surviving ink, but the middle letters are cramped.
