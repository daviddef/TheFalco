# Arienzo civil registers 1866–1910 — every Falco act (principal roles), read by a delegated reader and re-read by a second reader

> **26 October 2026.** The registers of Arienzo for 1866–1910 are on FamilySearch (collection 2043630, waypoints `MC55-FNT`, `MC55-VNL`, `MC55-RM9`; the archive had published them as lost). Twelve delegated readers looked at every leaf and listed every act naming a Falco; **ten second readers then re-read the 472 acts in which a Falco is the child, the deceased or a spouse**, from the leaf, without being shown the first reader's answer as an authority. This note is that second reading: **431 confirmed, 37 corrected, 4 not Falco** (the 4 are listed at the end). It is a finding aid — the archive's own hand has re-opened only the leaves named in `searched.json` and the household rows — and the births of Falco children have been entered in `households.json`; the deaths and marriages below have not been matched to households.

Columns: id · book · year · act · date of the act · the Falco person · father · mother · spouse/other · margin · what the second reader changed.
