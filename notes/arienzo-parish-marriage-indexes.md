# THE PARISH MARRIAGE INDEXES — and a sweep that was searching the wrong shelf

**13 September 2026.**

## 1. THE MISTAKE FIRST

Every FamilySearch sweep this archive has run on the Arienzo parish books used collection
**`M9J1-7MY`** — *Italia, Campania, Registri ecclesiastici 1551–1975*. Today's marriage act of
Giuseppe Falco and Antonia di Guida is **not in it**. It is in **`M9J1-9B4`**, the Marriages
collection — a different set entirely.

That means a sweep of 7MY for a marriage proves **nothing about marriages**. This morning a
word-sweep of 7MY returned **340 marriage-style leaves with no Falco on any of them**, which looked
like a clean negative and was not one: the leaf this archive had read three hours earlier, which
carries both the word and the name, was not among the 340.

> **Caught before it was written down as a rule.** That is the whole point of the standing note on
> [[wrong-negatives]]: three times this archive has turned a single failed look into a permanent
> "do not search again". This would have been the fourth.

**The collection ids, corrected:**

| id | what is actually in it |
|---|---|
| `M9J1-7MY` | Registri ecclesiastici — **baptisms**, in practice |
| `M9J1-9B4` | **Marriages** — where the 1802 Falco acts live |
| `M9J1-SMK` | Deaths |
| `1483010` | Archdiocese of Benevento church records — **returns nothing for Arienzo** |

## 2. WHAT THE RIGHT SHELF HOLDS

**1,260 distinct pages** of the marriage collection that name a Falco were read.

> **CORRECTED, an hour later.** `q.anyPlace=Arienzo` is a **weighting, not a filter**. A sample of the
> result set is **349 Arienzo pages to 51 from elsewhere** — San Felice a Cancello, Santa Maria a Vico,
> Parete, Dragoni, Aversa, Alvignano, Trentola, Castellammare. So the 1,260 are **about 87 per cent
> Arienzo**, and every count below should be read that way.

### NO MARRIAGE ACT FOR MATTEO FALCO — and what that is worth

Not one act in those 1,260 pages marries a Matteo Falco. **This is a real negative, and these are
its limits**, written down so nobody has to guess at them later:

- the search reaches pages by **extracted name**, and the handwriting recogniser renders *Falco* as
  *Falo*, *Falgo*, *Falto* and *Falcone*; a page where it did so is not in the 1,260;
- the search returned **2,358 results** for Falco + Arienzo, of which **1,260 were distinct pages** —
  the rest were repeats across offsets. Whether any page was never served at all is not known;
- **the indexes below have not been read.** They are the proper instrument for this question, and
  they are the next job.

**So: Matteo Falco's marriage has not been found, and has not been ruled out.**

### A NEW SIGHTING OF THE FOUNDER'S NAME, worth an image

Ark `3Q9M-CSMY-B7M2-N`, a marriage of Antonio Lettiero and Caterina Marcone, late 1786 (the act
after it is dated 13 January 1787): the witnesses are given as

> «praesentibus testibus **Fortunato Verdicchio**, **Paschale Falco filio Mat[the]i**…»

**If that reads *filio Matthei*, it is Pasquale Falco standing as a witness at nineteen, named as his
father's son.** The machine offers *Marilli* as an alternative, so this is a **lead, not a claim**,
and it needs the image.

## What to do next, in order

2. **Find an index covering the 1750s and 1760s.** That is where Matteo Falco's marriage would be

   else.

## POSTSCRIPT — the parish is an open question, and the archive got it wrong both ways

**13 September 2026, later the same day.** The index leaf naming Raffaele Falco was published first as
Arienzo's, then corrected to San Felice a Cancello's on the strength of FamilySearch's film label.
**Neither is established.**

- **FamilySearch's catalogue** calls film **007786833** *Registri ecclesiastici di San Felice a
  Cancello (Caserta)* — but a film label covers a **whole reel**, and a reel can hold several parishes.
- **The register bound immediately after the index** — images 1880 onward of that same film — says in
  its own acts: «Ego infrascriptus **Parochus Ecclesiae S. AGNETIS Virginis et Martyris TERRAE
  ARGENTII**». That is **Sant'Agnese in Arienzo**, and its first folios are **1843**.

What is certain: the entry «**Raphael Falco cum Angela Rosa Morgillo filia Horatii — fol. 2 a tergo**»,
read from the image; that **folio 2 of the volume that follows is 1843**, agreeing with the stray
«18 43» in the machine text beside the entry; and — from the civil registers, independently — that
**they were married in 1846 or earlier**, because their daughter Vincenza was born in November 1847.

**Reading folio 2 a tergo settles all of it at once.** The images either side of it are mapped
(1880–1890); the walk stopped at a FamilySearch **403 rate-limit**, not a wall.
