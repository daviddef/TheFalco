## Volume facts
- Portal label: Archivio di Stato di Caserta > Stato civile napoleonico e della restaurazione > Arienzo > 1857, 71 images.
- **Year proved from the manuscript, not the label**: every act opens «L'anno mille ottocento cinquantasette» (read in act 67 at 3400 px; the same wording in the other acts; margins carry 1876–1892 marriage notes). Act 1 is 1/2 January 1857; act 135 is 30 December 1857; dates in the register run monotonically.
- **Layout (verified, then used)**: image 1 cover, image 2 the printed title page, image 3L blank, acts begin at **3R**. Each image is one spread and **each PAGE carries one complete act** (Numero di ordine, declarant, mother AND father, residence, witnesses; the parish baptism notice and later marriage notes in the right-hand column). So images 3R–70R hold acts 1–135 in order (3R=1, 4L=2, 4R=3 … 70R=135); L = even act, R = odd act from act 2 on. One band (top 0.10–0.74 of the image, both halves) reads the whole act. Image 71 = blank printed forms. There is no need for the two-band method here.
- **N = 135 acts**, numbers 1..135 unbroken and unduplicated (checked by script on the TSV).
- **Tavola: none** in the volume (cover, title and back images inspected; last leaves 70R/71 are the end-of-register blank forms 136/137). The completeness argument therefore rests on the unbroken numbering plus an image-by-image sweep of every image 3–70, both halves; it cannot be cross-checked against an index count.
- Registrar: Pasquale d'Ambrosio, secondo eletto (act 67 signed «Pasquale d'Ambrosio»). Midwives: Rosa Balletta, Marianna Bernardo (fu Don Vincenzo), Rosa Morgillo, Rosa Crisci.
- Reading conditions: the leaves are legible; every act was read on a contact sheet at ~1,100 px per half page. 23 acts carry legibility «poor» in the TSV (a forename, age or trade doubtful): 3, 5, 8, 9, 18, 20, 25, 31, 32, 40, 41, 48, 60, 65, 66, 76, 78, 89, 90, 113, 125, 132, 133. In none was a SURNAME of father, mother, declarant or witness left blank or unread; but a few surnames are only approximate (marked '?' in the TSV) and are not Falco-shaped to the best of the contact-sheet reading. **No act is torn, stained or missing.** The TSV transcriptions are triage-grade (names to contact-sheet accuracy, ages ±), except the Falco act.

## Falco appearances: ONE

### Act 67 — CONFIRMED — image 36R, born 24 June 1857, registered 25 June 1857
Read at 3,400 px, cropped. «Numero di ordine 67. L'anno mille ottocento cinquantasette il dì venticinque di Giugno alle ore undici avanti di noi **Pasquale d'Ambrosio secondo eletto** ed ufiziale dello stato civile di Arienzo … è comparsa **Marianna Bernardo** figlia del fu D[on] Vincenzo, di anni trentasette, di professione levatrice, domiciliata alla Terra murata, quale ci ha presentata una femina … è nata da **Carmela Bruno** di anni trentasei domiciliata alla **Camellara** e da **Andrea Falco** marito della stessa, di anni trentasei, di professione **calessiere**, domiciliato colla moglie, nel giorno ventiquattro del suddetto mese alle ore otto nella casa de' suddetti conjugi. La stessa inoltre ha dichiarato di dare alla neonata il nome di **Giovanna Maria**.» Presence of **Giovanni Diglio**, contadino, domiciled alla Camellara, and **Angelo Mojone**, simile, ivi. Registrar's signature «Pasquale d'Ambrosio».
- Parish column (Sant'Andrea Apostolo): baptism administered on 25 June to «**Giovanna Maria Falco**» (the surname is written a second time here, F-a-l-c-o).
- Surname test: in the body the word is **F-a-l-c-o** — five letters, an F with its crossbar, a tall l, a round c, a closed o — written twice (father; parish note), against «Cioffi/Cossi/Calcagno/Silvio» which have different letter counts/shapes; the same hand writes «Camellara» two words earlier. Control: the two witnesses, Giovanni Diglio and Angelo Mojone, attest dozens of acts in this volume, always for strada Camellara families.
- No marginal marriage note on this act. Ages (36 for both) are the clerk's estimates and are not used to identify anybody.
- **Status: confirmed.**

## What is proven
- All 135 acts (1..135) were viewed on a contact sheet, both halves of images 3–70, and transcribed to `1857.tsv`; images 1, 2 and 71 were viewed.
- Exactly **one** act names a Falco in the register: act 67 (Giovanna Maria, 24 June 1857, to Andrea Falco, calessiere, and Carmela Bruno, strada Camellara). No Falco appears as declarant, midwife, witness or in any marginal note of the other 134 acts. Strength: surname-level reading of every parent, declarant and witness line on contact sheet resolution; the 23 «poor» acts are poor in forename/age/trade only. No tavola exists to cross-check.
- Not proven: ages and trades in the poor rows; and (as for every contact-sheet sweep) that a witness surname was never misread — but a witness named Falco would have to be misread as a different clear word in every one of 135 acts, which the repetition of the same two Camellara witnesses (Diglio, Mojone) makes unlikely.
