## Volume facts
- Ark an_ua14451, 73 images. Year proved from the manuscript: title page of register 1 "Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento cinquantaquattro"; acts all "L'anno mille ottocento cinquantaquattro"; tavola headed 1854.
- TWO REGISTERS in one volume: Register 1 = acts 1-120 (images 6-67); closing certificate at img68 "contenente atti num. centoventi" (acts 120, 63 leaves); back cover of reg.1 labelled "Registro 2° del 1854 Nati atti 20"? and images 70-73 = "Registro 2°": 6 acts (numbered 1-6 here, rows R2-1..R2-6), closing certificate img73 dated 1 Jan 1855 "contenente atti num. sei", then a blank form (R2-7) struck through.

- LAYOUT: one spread per image, one complete act per PAGE (left page = act n, right = act n+1); both parents on the same page as the act; baptism col on the facing side.
