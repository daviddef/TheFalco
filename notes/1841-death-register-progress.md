# Arienzo civil death register 1841 — sweep COMPLETE

**Volume:** `an_ua14343` (Arienzo, Morti 1841), 55 images.
**Read: images 2–55, acts 1–91. The volume is finished.**
**Officer:** the *sindaco*, whose forename this file does not claim — the signature reads
*Gaspare / Raffaele de' Nuzzo* and the hand does not settle it. **On 19 July the officer is not the
sindaco at all but AGOSTINO SEPISTELLA, «SECONDO ELETTO»**, the deputy, standing in (atto 35).
**Duplicate openings, each verified from the printed act number:** 13≡12, 19≡18, 23≡22, 28≡27,

Image 55 carries the volume's own **index** and the same printed **1849 ministerial certificate**

## Three more sightings of the line — and one new Falco

### GIUSEPPE FALCO, twice in three days — atti 72 and 74, October 1841

> …sono comparsi **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **cinquantasei**, di professione **COLONO**,
> regnicolo, **domiciliato in strada CAMELLARA**… *(atto 72, 16 October)*

> …sono comparsi **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **cinquantatré**, di professione **COLONO**,
> regnicolo, **domiciliato in strada CAMELLARA**… *(atto 74, 19 October)*

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-1841.jpg`

**Three days apart, the same clerk gave the same man two different ages.** With the 1842 and 1843
volumes the run now reads:

| 1817 | 1820s | 1828 | **Oct 1841** | **Oct 1841** | Jul 1842 | May 1843 | Aug 1843 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 37 | 40 · 46 · 46 · 40 | 56 | **56** | **53** | 52 | 56 | 55 |

Fifty-six and fifty-three in one week ends the argument. **These ages are not measurements**, and
this archive will not derive a birth year for Giuseppe Falco from any of them. What the acts *do*
establish, over twenty-six years, is steadier and more useful: **the same name, the same trade —
*colono* — and the same street.**

### BENEDETTO FALCO — a name the archive did not have — atto 1, 1 January 1841

> …sono comparsi **[Gasparo] Crisci**, di anni cinquantasette, contadino, domiciliato in strada
> **CAMELLARA**, **e BENEDETTO FALCO, di anni QUARANTA, di professione contadino, domiciliato in
> strada CAMELLARA**…

Plate: `site/public/plates/benedetto-falco-1841.jpg`

Read at 2800px. Born about **1801**, a *contadino* on the line's own street. **He is recorded, and
he is not placed.** No act names his parents; no act ties him to Giuseppe, to Michele Falco and
Antonia Migliore of the 1842 volume, or to the direct line. He appears once, in the first act of the
year, and then not again in these ninety-one acts.

**Two cautions, stated rather than buried.** The surname is a strong reading and not a certainty —
the initial letter is the looped capital this hand also uses for other letters, and it is the street
and the shape together that carry it. And the first declarant's surname could be **Crisci** or
**Cioffi**; the archive is not choosing between them, because *Crisci* on strada Camellara would
mean something and a coin-toss is not evidence.

## What else the volume gave

### The Lopa of Forchia

**Atto 17: «ANTONIA LOPA, d'anni ottantasette, NATA IN FORCHIA … figlia di ALESSANDRO LOPA defunto,
contadino, domiciliato a FORCHIA.»** Born about 1754.

The 1842 volume buries **Sabatino Lopa, 70**, in the Arienzo hospital, *«figlio di Alessandro Lopa
defunto»* — and puts **Maria Lopa, 45, a *conversa* of the Ave Gratia Plena, «nata in Forchia
d'Arpaia»**, in the same house. **A Lopa family of Forchia moved to Arienzo**, and three of them
are now in these registers. Forchia is one of the towns this family's other lines come from; that
is a reason to notice, not a connection.

### A ninety-nine-year-old nun, and a correction to what 1842 seemed to show

**Atto 31: «è morta nel monastero d'Ave Gratia Plena — SUOR ARCANGELA RIZZA(?), d'anni
NOVANTANOVE … NATA IN LAURO, di professione MONACA, figlia del fu Signor Domenico Rizza, di Lauro.»**
Born about 1742. Only the hundred-year-old basket maker of 1843 is older in these registers.

> **This qualifies a pattern the 1842 file drew.** That file observed that the *converse* — the lay
> sisters — came from outside Arienzo while the one *monaca* was local gentry, and suggested the map
> and the class line ran together. **They do not.** Suor Arcangela is a *monaca*, gentry, and from
> **Lauro**, well outside the valley. The pattern that survives is the simpler one: **rank inside
> the house tracked the father's class, not the birthplace.** *Converse* were labourers' and
> *villani*'s daughters; *monache* were the daughters of *proprietari* and *signori*, wherever they
> came from.

### The Morgillo, again, and one of them finally separated

Eleven Morgillo appear here too. Two matter:

- **Atto 21** puts **NICOLA MORGILLO, 55, *colono*, on strada Capodiconca** — very probably the
  Nicola Morgillo who dies there in October 1843 as *«figlio di Gaetano Morgillo e Maria Cioffi»*,
  where he is **fifty**. Probably, not certainly.
- **Atto 82** puts a **NICOLA MORGILLO, 50, *calzolajo*, on strada CAMELLARA** — a different street,
  a different trade. **A Morgillo on the Falco street**, and *not* merged with the other.

And **atto 71 settles one of the two Maria Morgillo**: the wife of Angelo Cioffi of strada Cioffi,
written simply «Maria Morgillo» in 1842, is **Maria Giuseppa Morgillo**. The Maria Morgillo who
married Luca Zimbardo of Capodiconca is somebody else.

### Households that run across the volumes

The three years now read together:

- **Aniello Grazia × Prudenzia Martone** — their infant daughter here (atto 29), Aniello declaring
  in 1842, Prudenzia dead at forty in 1843.
- **Andrea Mauro × Lazzara Verbone**, strada Camellara — a two-year-old here (atto 76), a
  ten-month-old in 1843.
- **Luca Zimbardo × Maria Morgillo** — **two sons, both named ANTONIO**: sixteen months old in
  August 1841, twenty-two days old in August 1842. The name was given again after the first died.
- **Gaetano Venafra × Maria Rosa Cioffi** — a three-year-old in September (atto 69) and a
  six-month-old in November (atto 86), ten weeks apart.
- **Gennaro Morgillo**, *vaticale* of strada Sant'Andrea — his son Simone here (atto 19), himself
  declaring in 1843 at **forty**, having been **forty-five** in 1841.

### And the ages, once more

**Luca Cioffi**, the *sartore* of strada Camellara who reports Chiara Rivetti's death in 1843, is
**33** in July 1841, **33** again nine days later, **30** in September 1841, **33** in September 1843
and **34** the very next day, when the clerk also demotes him from *sartore* to *contadino*.
**Angelo Martone** is 40 one day and 50 the next. **Domenico Cioffi** is 50 and then 60 five days
later. The register is a record of what was said at the desk, not of what was true.
