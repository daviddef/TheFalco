> **25 October 2026.** The roster below is a finding aid: only the Falco acts the households file cites as READ FROM THE IMAGE were re-opened by the archive's own hand at ~2,800 px. Everything else is triage-grade.

- Ark: an_ua14443; catalogue label 1846; 110 images.
- Year proof: title page 1R "Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento quarantasei"; every act opens "L'anno mille ottocento quarantasei" (act 1, 3 Jan 1846, 3R; act 106, 25 Dec 1846, 108R/107R); closing attestation 109L "L'anno mille ottocento quarantasei il dì trentuno del mese di Dicembre noi Domenico Finelli Sindaco ... fogli scritti num. centoquaranta [sic], contenente atti num. centosei".
- First act no. 1, 3 Jan 1846 (Tomaso Acquaviva secondo eletto; Domenico Finelli Sindaco signs from act 7, 2 Feb). Last act no. 106, 25 Dec 1846. 106 acts, run 1..106 unbroken (checked in 1846.tsv), no duplicates.
- Layout: continuing spread. Act N = right page of image N+1 + left page of image N+2 (act 106 = 107R+108L). Margin notes are in the outer column of the left page.
- Tavola: image 108R is the printed blank "Tavola alfabetica annuale" form (empty). A hand-written "Tavola de' nati nel 1846" is on 109R: a short alphabetical list with act numbers (read: 23 Arrigale Maria Carmela; C-names 44, 58, 70, 78 etc.), the rest of the page shows the verso through in mirror; no F section / no Falco entry visible; it is not a complete index. Stamp "THIS INDEX HAS BEEN MICROFILMED ALREADY". Not used as a substitute for reading the acts.
- Duplicate frames: none. Blank forms: 108R (tavola form), 110L blank, 110R printed ministerial notice (Napoli 4 Luglio 1849, unfilled).
- Damage: none material; some acts' ink is faint (witness ages/trades are doubtful, marked ?).

## HOW THIS WAS READ (honesty note)
All 106 act rows and the tail rows were written from images displayed in this session at 2200 px, spread by spread (act and its continuation together). Falco acts 6, 32, 44, 52 and 69 were additionally looked at as 3000 px crops. Rows written earlier in the session from results that had returned only a caption were deleted and re-done. Where two lines on a page disagreed I recorded the doubt in the TSV.

## FALCO ACTS (no Falco child is "hidden": each is a named role)
1. Act 6 (images 7R+8L), 20 Jan 1846, ore sedici, Tomaso Acquaviva secondo eletto. Child CLEMENTE FALCO, maschio, b. 19 Jan ore due, baptised 20 Jan. FATHER and DECLARANT Francesco Falco, 41 ("quarantuno"), contadino, strada Camellara; MOTHER "[first name unclear] Falco", 34 ("trentaquattro"), "sua moglie legittima". Witnesses Fabrizio Sette? (about 30) contadino strada Camellara and Clemente Giglio 36 falegname strada Sant'Andrea; none could write. No margin note. Falco clear. NOTE the mother is also written Falco, so either both spouses were Falco or the scribe wrote the child's surname for the mother; the surname after her first name is plain.
2. Act 32 (33R+34L), 23 Apr 1846, ore ventuno. Child Maria Antonia Crisci, femina, b. 23 Apr ore quattordici. Father and declarant Aniello Crisci, 38, contadino, strada Crisci. MOTHER GIOVANNA FALCO, "sua moglie legittima", age read "sessanta" (could be quaranta; ink faint). Witnesses Filippo Giglio sartore strada Croce and Arcangelo di Muzzo 34 contadino strada Camellara. MARGIN (34L): "Ad dì 9 Giugno 1875 n. 19 l'uff.le dello stato civile di Maria a Vico ha sposato Maria Antonia Crisci con Nicola de Lucia. Caserta 8 Aprile 1878" (not Falco). Falco clear.
3. Act 44 (45R+46L), 6 Jun 1846, ore undici. Child Bartolomeo Crisci (baptism line 'Bartolomeo' + a surname the scribe forms like Corzi/Crisci; the declarant signs "Raffaele Crisci"), maschio, b. 6 Jun ore nove. Father and declarant Raffaele Crisci, 34, contadino, strada Crisci. MOTHER FRANCESCA FALCO, 24 ("ventiquattro"), "sua moglie legittima". Witnesses Simone di Guida contadino strada Crisci and Salvatore Sposito 50 contadino strada Camellara; none could write. No margin note. Falco clear (read at 3000 px).
4. Act 52 (53R+54L), 4 Jul 1846, ore dodici. Child Girolamo Truglio, b. 3 Jul. Father and declarant Francesco Truglio, 36, vetturale, strada Capodrone; mother Angela Morazzo? 34. WITNESS FRANCESCO FALCO, 32 ("trentadue"), contadino, strada Camellara (second witness Crescenzo Corzi, contadino strada Sant'Andrea). Margin (54L): Girolamo Truglio married Rosa Chietto at Aversa 7 May 1893 (not Falco). Falco fair-to-clear (3000 px).
5. Act 69 (70R+71L), 13 Sep 1846, ore tredici. Child DOMENICA FALCO, femina, b. 13 Sep ore sette. FATHER and DECLARANT MATTEO FALCO, 38 ("trentotto"), colono, strada Camellara; MOTHER Alessandra Corzi, 36 ("trentasei"). Witnesses Pasquale Barletta? sartore strada Porta di Sopra and Francesco Corzi 48 sergente strada Croce; none could write. MARGINS (71L): printed column "A 25 Maggio 1881 l'uff.le dello stato civile d'Arienzo ha unito Domenica Falco con Pasquale Saccovino. Caserta 4 Giugno 1884"; and a handwritten note up the left edge, marked (1), which I could read only in part (it appears to give another date, 1874?, for the same marriage). Falco clear; margin note doubtful.

## Near-misses
None with a Falco-like surname; the surnames in this volume are Crisci, Corzi, Morgillo, Martone, Diglio, Lombardo, Landato, Porrino, Pezzella, Truglio etc. (Corzi and Crisci are close to each other in the scribe's hand, not to Falco.)

## Limitations
Witness ages, trades and several mothers' ages/surnames are doubtful (marked ?). Act 64 child's name is inconsistent between the baptism line (Giuseppe Altarelli) and the act (Crescenzo?). The hand-written left-edge note on act 69 and the age of Giovanna Falco in act 32 are doubtful. The 1846 tavola is a partial list and lists no Falco.
