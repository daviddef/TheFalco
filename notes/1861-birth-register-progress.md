# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1861 — read end to end (`an_ua14458`)

> **Delegated read, 18 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand.**
> A delegated reader read every act of this volume at contact-sheet resolution (about 1,100 px a half
> page) and wrote a roster of it; **every Falco act, in any role, was then re-opened at about 2,400 px and
> read from the image again**, and a random sample of the other acts (acts 120–121) was compared with
> the manuscript — **no name in the sample disagreed**. The roster is therefore a *finding aid*: ages,
> trades and many non-Falco surnames in it are approximate and are not to be cited. What the
> reader's text below calls "confirmed" is what was re-opened; where it says "survey" the reading was not.
> The households this produced are in `households.json` (row evidence names the act and the image);
> the rows for the volume are in `searched.json` and on `/searched`.

## Volume facts
- Ark `an_ua14458`, 82 images (Antenati label: Archivio di Stato di Caserta > Stato civile napoleonico e della restaurazione > Arienzo > 1861).
- Layout: each image is one SPREAD (left page + right page); EACH PAGE CARRIES ONE COMPLETE ACT (act number at top, declarant, both parents with ages/trades/domicile, witnesses, baptism notation in the printed right-hand column, mayor's signature). Both parents are on the same page as the act, so one band (about 10-85% of the page height, both halves) reads parent + declarant + witnesses + margin. Verified on images 3,4,5 and by reading the act numbers on both halves of every image (number-band sweep of images 3-82, L and R).
- Images: 1 = cover (inscribed "1861 ... Registro de Nati 1861"), 2 = title leaf ("Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento sessantuno"; "Il presente volume contiene fogli ..."), 3 = L blank leaf + R act 1, 4-80 acts (2 per image), 81 = repeat frame of image 80 (acts 150, 151 again), 82 = L act 152 pre-printed blank struck through with a large X, R blank form 153 ("BLANK PAGES" label). Images 9 and 53 are DUPLICATE frames of images 8 and 52 (same two acts photographed twice; confirmed by act numbers and by reading both).
- N = 151 acts, numbered 1..151, unbroken, no gaps, no repeats. Read: 151 of 151. Illegible/damaged acts: 0 (a few fields flagged `poor` in the TSV: acts 1 [first read], 8, 11, 45, 87, 127 have uncertain secondary names or ages; none affects the question "is there a Falco").
- First act: no. 1, 4 Jan 1861 (image 3R). Last act: no. 151, 31 Dec 1861 (image 80R; page marked "END"). Acts 152/153 are blank struck forms.
- Tavola (annual alphabetical index): NONE in this volume. Last leaves are the struck blank forms (images 81-82); no index leaf before or after. So the cross-check of tavola count against N is not possible; the check instead is the unbroken act-number run 1..151 plus the date run (dates ascend monotonically, the longest interval between consecutive acts is 14 days, 19 Jan (act 14) to 2 Feb (act 15)).
- YEAR PROOF (from the manuscript, not the label): act 1 reads "L'anno mille ottocento *sessantuno* il dì quattro di Gennajo"; act 86 "sessantuno" 3 Aug; act 151 "sessantuno ... 31 Dicembre"; the printed title leaf says "mille ottocento sessantuno"; the cover is inscribed 1861. Margin notes are later (1879-1894) and do not date the volume.
- Form change at unification: NONE visible. The whole volume stays on the same Bourbon pre-printed form ("Atto di nascita", "Regno delle Due Sicilie" codice title page, "Provincia di Terra di Lavoro / Distretto di Caserta / Circondario e Comune di Arienzo", parish notification column "Il parroco di Sant'Andrea Apostolo ..."). Only the officer changes: Nicola Martone signs as mayor/ufficiale dello stato civile from act 1 through act 85 (Aug 1861); from act 86 (3 Aug 1861) the officer is RAFFAELE RIVETTI, sindaco. The cancelliere throughout is Lorenzo Viggiano. No Kingdom-of-Italy wording appears in the printed text.
- Midwives (levatrici) declare most acts: Rosa Balletta (di fu Angelo, Porta di sopra), Rosa Morgillo (di fu Marco, Santa Lucia), Marianna Bernardo (di fu Don Vincenzo, Terra murata), Rosa Crisci (once). Where a father declares, the act says "da lui dichiarante" and his name is on the same page (so no father is hidden).

## FALCO APPEARANCES (every role) - 4 acts, all confirmed
Read at 1500-2400 px; in each, the F has the crossbar/loop and the word is F-a-l-c-o (control: the same hand writes "Falco" in the baptism column). No Silvio/Cosci/Calcagno/Salcito confusion: the Falco words were read at magnification on the Falco-bearing line AND repeated in the printed baptism column.

1. **Act 67, image 37R, 30 May 1861** (born 29 May 1861, ~5 pm, in the house of the spouses; declared by the levatrice Rosa Morgillo, 57?, fu Marco, Santa Lucia). Child: **NICOLA MORGILLO** (m). **Mother: MARIA FALCO, 36 ("trentasei"), domiciled in Arienzo.** Father: [forename not certain; looks like a Morgillo with a long first name] MORGILLO, 28, contadino, domiciled "ivi" (Arienzo). Witnesses Orazio Grosso and Pietro Mauro (contadini). Baptism line: Nicola Morgillo. No margin note. CONFIRMED (Falco is the MOTHER; child is under her husband's surname). Not placed on a named street ("in Arienzo").
2. **Act 69, image 38R, 2 Jun 1861** (born 1 Jun, ~4-5 pm; levatrice Rosa Morgillo). Child: **CIRO CLEMENTE FALCO** (m). Father **ANDREA FALCO, 40 ("quaranta"), trade word under his name not certain (reads like "calzolaro"), domiciled in Arienzo**; mother **Carmela Buono, 36 ("trentasei"), domiciled in Arienzo**. Born "nella casa di esso Falco". Witnesses Pasquale Zimbardo (contadino), Pasquale Monteforchio (muratore). Baptism: Ciro Clemente Falco. No margin note. CONFIRMED.
3. **Act 86, image 47L, 3 Aug 1861** (born 2 Aug, ~6 pm; levatrice Rosa Morgillo 59, fu Marco, Santa Lucia). Child: **ALFONSO ISIDORO FALCO** (m). Father **ANGELO FALCO, 35, "trojante"/trainante (carter), domiciled Santa Lucia**; mother **Maria Cimmino, 34, Santa Lucia**. Witnesses Giovanni Diglio (contadino, strada Camellara) and Angelo Majone. Baptism: Alfonso Isidoro Falco. No margin note. This is the first act signed by Raffaele Rivetti as mayor. CONFIRMED. Trade word read "trojante/trainante"; uncertain spelling, meaning a carter/hauler.
4. **Act 102, image 56L, 14 Sep 1861** (born 13 Sep, 5 pm; levatrice Rosa Morgillo, fu Marco). Child: **FILOMENA ALESSANDRA FALCO** (f). Father **GIUSEPPE FALCO, 36, contadino, domiciled Arienzo ("casa di esso Falco")**; mother **Rosa Zimbardo, 26, Arienzo**. Witnesses Pasquale Zimbardo and Pietro Mauro (contadini). Baptism: Filomena Alessandra Falco. **MARGIN (later, read at 1300 px): "Addì 19 Maggio 1892 Filomena Alessandra Falco sposò Vincenzo Lucia in Arienzo 9-6-92"**, signed (Rivetti? / illegible). CONFIRMED. (Margin gives a marriage in 1892, i.e. a person born in 1861, not a living-person concern for this report beyond name; fine to record the 1892 fact, the child was born 1861.)

No Falco as declarant, midwife, or witness anywhere in the volume. No Falcone / Falcon / Calco / Salco observed.

## Uncertain candidates
None left open. Items that looked at first sight like possible Falco and were cleared: none arose in the 1861 pass beyond the four above (surnames in the volume are dominated by Crisci, Diglio, Morgillo, Zimbardo, Porrino, Arricale, Mauro, Laudato, Liparulo, Martone, Cimmino, Guida, Pesitelli, Altavelli).

## Margin notes (valuable)
Marginal annotations of later marriages (1879-1894, mostly sent from Caserta 29 May 1889 and 6 Jun 1884 and 27 Aug 1881 as certified copies) appear on about 45 acts; they are transcribed in the `notes` column of the TSV (not every date certain). The only Falco-related margin is act 102. Other surnames that carry a marriage note and may be useful later: Laudato (act 5, to Agostino Bardacci of Cesena), Guida (13, 77), Marletta (14), Migliore (32), Romano (33), Mauro (38, 76?), etc.

## What is PROVEN
- All 151 acts of the 1861 Arienzo birth register (numbers 1-151, unbroken; images 3-80 each read on both halves; images 9, 53, 81 are repeat frames; 82 blank) were seen and read at parent/declarant/witness level.
- Falco occurs in exactly four acts: 67 (Maria Falco as mother of Nicola Morgillo), 69 (Andrea Falco/Carmela Buono, Ciro Clemente), 86 (Angelo Falco/Maria Cimmino, Alfonso Isidoro), 102 (Giuseppe Falco/Rosa Zimbardo, Filomena Alessandra).
- No Falco family at strada Camellara or Capodiconca appears in 1861; none of the four is described as living on a named street.
## What is NOT proven / limits
- Transcriptions are best readings from images at ~2200 px (the Antenati ceiling for most leaves is ~3000-3200 px). Non-Falco surnames, ages and trades in the TSV are triage-grade ('?' marks uncertain letters). The roster is good for finding candidates, not for citing as evidence.
- Foot-of-page signature blocks were not transcribed; marginal notes were read for the right-hand notification column only.
- No tavola exists, so there is no independent count to compare with N.
