> **Delegated read, 25 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand at ~2,800 px.** The reader's roster is a finding aid; its non-Falco rows are triage-grade and are not cited. Households: `households.json`; row: `searched.json`.

- Ark: an_ua14437. Images: 128.
- YEAR PROOF (manuscript): title page (image 1R): «Registro degli atti dello stato civile. Degli atti di Nascita ... Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento quaranta. Il presente volume contiene fogli 151» (Provincia di Terra di Lavoro, Distretto di Caserta, Comune di Arienzo). Act 1 (image 2R): «L'anno mille ottocento quaranta il dì tre del mese di Gennajo ad ore quindici avanti di noi Filippo Troisi Sindaco». Closing certificate (image 125L): «L'anno mille ottocento quaranta il dì trentuno del mese di Decembre noi Filippo Troisi ... abbiamo chiuso e firmato il presente registro di Nascite ... contenente atti num. Cento diciotto». Hand-written tavola (126): «Tavola annuale alfabetica di nati per l'anno Milleottocentoquaranta 1840». The label 1840 is CORRECT.
- First act: no. 1, 3 Jan 1840 (Angela Rosa Rivetti). Last act: no. 118, 29 Dec 1840 (Raffaele Orologio?, mother Mariangela Diglio). N = 118 acts, matching the number written in the closing certificate. Run unbroken 1..118: YES (checked mechanically on the TSV: no gap, no duplicate).
- Layout: the continuing-spread form of the brief. Act N = right page of image N+1 + left page of image N+2 up to the first duplicate frame (act 50 on 51R+53L because image 52 re-films 51), thereafter act N = image N+3 / N+4 until image 119 (act 81 on 83R + 86L; act 114 on 118R + 120L). Right page: «ATTO DI NASCITA» with «INDICAZIONE» (baptism) column; left page of next frame: mother, father, child's name, witnesses; the printed right-hand column of that left page holds the margin notes (later marriages, all dated 1869-1875 and certified «Caserta 1884»). The number printed top-left of a left page is the PAGE number, not the act number.
- Front matter: image 1 = title page (right) with a blank left page; act 1 begins at 2R.
- Duplicate frames (same spread filmed twice, not extra acts): image 52 (of 51), image 84 (of 82), image 85 (of 83), image 119 (of 118), image 127 (of 126, tavola, with the target «THIS INDEX HAS BEEN MICROFILMED ALREADY»). Rows for them are in the TSV.
- Blank forms at the end: image 124 (printed «Num. d'ordine cento diciannove», unfilled, pencil 120).
- Tavola: image 126 (hand-written, «Tavola annuale alfabetica di nati ... 1840»), INCOMPLETE: 21 entries in the A–C stretch (Ambrosio, Angiovino, Crisci, Cimmino, Carfora, Casa, Cillo) ending at act 106. The F page does not exist; the tavola was not used as a substitute for the acts. Its entry for act 99 reads «Crisci Luigi / Crisci Aniello, e Falco Giovanna / 30 Ottobre 1840», independently confirming the mother Falco. 128 = 1849 ministerial/Procuratore del Re notice (4 Luglio 1849), left page blank; 125R blank.
- Damage: none material; one leaf (act 83) is faint. Legibility: handwriting fair; trades, ages, mothers' surnames and witnesses' residences marked `?` where doubtful.
