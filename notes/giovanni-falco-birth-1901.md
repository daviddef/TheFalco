# Giovanni Falco, born 10 December 1901 (Nascite 1901 act 123)

Found 15 November 2026 on the full Arienzo walk (MC55-RM9 image 87, right page). Pasquale Falco, trainante, aged 32 as the act has it, declared on 12 December 1901 that at 4.15 in the afternoon of the 10th, at corso Caudino 85, «da Angelamaria Attanasio sua moglie legittima, donna di casa» a boy was born, named Giovanni. Witnesses Alfonso Iannone (42, sarto) and Vincenzo Maione (46, contadino); nobody signed. Read at level 12 and the declarant's line, street, mother, child's name, margin and witnesses at level 13.

**Why it is in the house of Pasquale Falco and Angela Maria Attanasio.** Three things the act says, each independent of the others: the father's name and trade (Pasquale Falco, trainante, as in the same house's acts of 1896, 1898, 1899, 1904, 1906 and 1907); the mother's full name written out (Angelamaria Attanasio, in every one of them); and the street (corso Caudino, numbers 80 to 85 across the acts). The house had no child between Pasquale (b. 1899) and Giuseppe (b. 1904), and the act fills that gap.

**The margin.** A later hand wrote «Deceduto in Arienzo S. Felice in data 15-2-45», entered at Santa Maria Capua Vetere on 10 May 1957. The place after Arienzo is read as it stands and not interpreted. The margin names no marriage.

**Not claimed.** The Pasquale Falco, 37, trainante, who witnessed act 30 of the same register on 1 March 1901 is probably the same man (name and trade), but that rests on two things only and stays a lead.
