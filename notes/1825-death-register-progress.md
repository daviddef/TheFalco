# Arienzo civil death register 1825 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14327` (Arienzo, Morti 1825), 39 images. **Read in full.**
**Acts:** 59, running 20 January – 28 December 1825.
**Officer:** **GIOVANBATTISTA DE' NAPOLI, secondo eletto**, throughout — Vincenzo Morgillo, who had
signed since 1820, died in November 1824.
**At the back:** the officer's closing certificate, and bound in after it a printed **ministerial
circular of 4 July 1849** from the Real Segreteria di Stato di Grazia e Giustizia at Naples about
the keeping of these registers — twenty-four years younger than the volume it sits in.
**Duplicate bindings:** 19≡18, 25≡24, 35≡34.

## Giuseppe Falco buries two children in nineteen days

### Maddalena Falco, seven — atto 30, 31 May 1825

> …**è morta nella sua propria casa MADDALENA FALCO, d'anni SETTE**, nata in Arienzo, di professione
> Contadina, domiciliata ivi, **figlia di GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di professione Contadino, domiciliato
> ivi, **e di ANTONIA DI GUIDA**, domiciliata ivi.

Plate: `site/public/plates/maddalena-falco-death-1825.jpg`

**This closes a life the archive had only opened.** Her civil **birth** act of **7 June 1818**
records her born on strada Camellara to Giuseppe, thirty-six, and Antonia di Guida. She died **a
week short of her seventh birthday**. A documented grandchild of Matteo Falco and Francesca Crisci,
and a first cousin of Vincenzo Falco's children.

### Clemente Falco, fifteen months — 19 June 1825

> …**è morto nella sua propria casa CLEMENTE FALCO, di mesi QUINDICI** … **figlio di GIUSEPPE
> FALCO**, di professione contadino, **e di ANTONIA DI GUIDA**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/clemente-falco-death-1825.jpg`

Born about **March 1824** — a child of this house the archive did not have. He died **nineteen days
after his sister**.

## The two houses that stand for each other

**GIUSEPPE FALCO has now declared three times for the household of FRANCESCO COSSI and MARIA
FALCO** — Antonia in June 1823, Giuseppe in March 1824, and **Giuseppa, eight months old, on 2 April
1825**. Three dead children in three years, and he reported every one.

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-1825.jpg`

**And the reciprocal is now on record.** The men who declared **Maddalena Falco's** death are
**CESARE and FRANCESCO COSSI**, both of strada Camellara — and Francesco Cossi, thirty-four, is very
likely Maria Falco's husband. When Clemente died three weeks later it was **MARCELLO COSSI** who
came.

The register calls these men *«vicino»* every time and **never *«parente»***, so no relationship is
stated anywhere. But two households standing for each other through five child deaths in three years
is the strongest hint yet that **this third Maria Falco is Giuseppe's kin**. It is written down as a
lead, and not as a finding.

## What the same man's four sightings do to his age and trade

| | age | trade |
|---|---|---|
| 20 Jun 1823 | 43, then 42 the same day | contadino |
| 25 Mar 1824 | 42 | **colono** |
| 2 Apr 1825 | **40** | **massaro** |
| 19 Jun 1825 | — | contadino |

**His stated age goes backwards** and his trade is given three different ways. This is the clearest
single illustration of the cautions now set out on the [method page](../method/): a stated age is a
range, and a trade is what the neighbour standing at the desk happened to say.

## Also in this volume

- **ANNA ARRIGALE**, thirty-four, *«moglie di CARLANTONIO MORGILLO»*, died 3 February — the wife of
  the **third** Carlantonio Morgillo, of strada Capodiconca. In August her husband buries an
  eight-month-old son, Clemente.
- **Another death in the Monastero de' Cappuccini** — Giuseppe Saccavino, seventy-eight, born at
  Durazzano, a man of unknown parents.
- **Two Cossi brothers**, Carmine, eight, and Raffaele, six, of strada Cossi, **died on the same
  day**, 15 June.
- **Domenico Balletta**, still a *sartore* on strada Camellara at forty-seven — Giuseppe Falco's
  neighbour since at least 1817.
- Trades new to the archive: a ***sonatore*** (musician), a ***domestico***, and a *proprietario*
  from **Cajazzo**.
