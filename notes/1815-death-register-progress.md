# Arienzo civil deaths, 1815 — READ IN FULL

**Volume**: `an_ua14317`, 49 images, 77 acts, 5 January – 22 December 1815
**Read**: 13 September 2026, by name-band sweep across every act
**Why**: the last unread year in the window that could close the Arcangelo Crisci knot

---

## THE QUESTION THIS VOLUME WAS OPENED TO ANSWER — and it is now closed

The 1814 marriage dossier left one thing open: **which of the two Arcangelo Crisci married
Anna Falco?** The archive's standing reading is the younger (30 in 1817, b. c.1787). The elder
(52 in 1817, b. c.1765) is proved to be Adriana's father — she was baptised in 1796, when the
younger man was nine.

The elder could only *also* be Anna's husband if his wife **Sara Ruggiero** had died between
December 1814, when she consented before a notary, and about 1818, when Anna married.

**She did not.** Two findings close it:

### 1. She bore a child in January 1815 — act 4, 9 January

> …i quali hanno dichiarato che a' detto dì **nove** del mese corrente di **Gennajo** ad ore
> **una di notte** **CARMELA CRISCI** figlia d'**ARCANGELO CRISCI**, e **SARA RUGGIERO**, di
> giorni **sette**, di professione contadina, domiciliata **strada Camellara**, è morta nella
> sua propria casa.

**A new child of the family** — Adriana Crisci's baby sister, born about 2 January 1815, three
weeks after Adriana's wedding, dead at seven days. And **Sara Ruggiero was delivered of a child
in the first week of 1815**, which is not the condition of a woman about to be replaced.

**The declarants are the two families side by side**: «**ANDREA CRISCI**, di anni quarantad[ue],
contadino, strada Camellara, e **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni **trenta**, massaro di campagna,
strada Camellara, **vicini** della defunta». *Vicini* — neighbours, not kin; the archive does not
upgrade that word. Andrea Crisci at ~42 in 1815 is the same man as the «Andrea Crisci, 58,
contadino, strada Camellara» who declared Anna Falco's death in 1831.

### 2. She is in no death register at Arienzo, 1815–1843

- **1815 — read in full for this question.** 77 acts. No Sara Ruggiero.
- **1816–1843 — already read, continuously.** The only Sara Ruggiero in twenty-nine years of
  death registers is **act 126 of 13 December 1817**, and she is **a different woman**:

| | The 1817 decedent | Adriana's mother |
|---|---|---|
| Father | **Raffaele** Ruggiero, deceased | **Pasquale** Ruggiero, living in 1814 |
| Mother | **Fortunata Villano** | not recorded |
| Husband | Arcangelo **SOSSI** | Arcangelo **CRISCI** |

Two women of one name in one town, **both married to an Arcangelo**. This is the most dangerous
near-collision the archive has met, and it would have closed the question the wrong way.

> **And the two families are connected.** Adriana Crisci's godmother at her 1796 baptism was
> **FORTUNATA VILLANO** — who is the *mother* of the other Sara Ruggiero. The Ruggiero of Arienzo
> stood godparent to each other's children. That link is noted, not built on.

### The verdict

**Sara Ruggiero did not die in the window.** The elder Arcangelo Crisci's wife was alive
throughout the years in which he would have had to remarry. **There were two Arcangelo Crisci,
and Anna Falco married the younger.** The knot the 1814 dossier re-opened is shut.

**Honest limits.** She could have died outside Arienzo, and the sweep read the name band of each
act at survey resolution rather than each act in full. A missed act is possible. The positive
evidence — a birth in January 1815 — does not depend on the sweep.

## The other Falco find: a third Raffaele

**Act ~68, image 41 left, 9 October 1815**, read at native resolution:

> …domiciliato in questo Comune, strada **[Camel]lara**, **vicini** del Defunto, i quali hanno
> dichiarato che a' detto dì **nove** del mese corrente di **Ottobre** ad ore **sette**,
> **RAFFAELE FA[LCO]** figlio di **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, ed **ANTONIA DI GUIDA**, di anni **due**…

**A child not previously recorded.** Born about 1813, died at two, on strada Camellara. Giuseppe
Falco and Antonia di Guida were already known as a couple from the 1816 and 1818 birth registers;
this is their earliest known child and their earliest joint sighting.

It also puts **a third Raffaele Falco** into the 1810s, alongside Vincenzo's two. The three are
cleanly distinguishable by their parents, but anyone working from a name index would collide them.

**Giuseppe Falco's age is wrong again.** Thirty in January 1815 implies 1785; thirty-seven in
October 1817 implies 1780; the age series for this man has never held still. See
`notes/giuseppe-falco-1842.md` equivalents in findings.

## Notes on the volume itself

- **1815 closes at act 77**, certified by the sindaco on 31 December 1815; the last death is
  Caterina Madonna, 73, *filatrice*, **strada Camellara**, 22 December.
- **There is a year-end alphabetical index** — act number, name, *patria*, trade, father's name —
  but **only the G–M page was photographed**. A–F and N–Z are not in the scan. It cannot be used
  to test for a surname.
- **Images 48 and 49 are duplicate shots of the same opening**, as are several others in this
  volume. Antenati duplicates openings; do not read a duplicate as a second document.
