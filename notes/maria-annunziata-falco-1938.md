> «… Ufficiale dello Stato Civile del Comune di **NAPOLI, MONTECALVARIO**, avendo ricevuto dal
> Direttore **Ospedale Pellegrini** un avviso in data di ieri … dò atto che a ore **pom. dieci** del
> giorno **TRENTUNO LUGLIO** ultimo scorso, in detto Ospedale **è morta FALCO MARIA ANNUNZIATA di
> ANTONIO e [di] ANNECCHINO FILOMENA, di anni CINQUE, da FORCHIA**. F.to Giorgio Vissardi. — Per copia
> conforme all'originale in carta libera per uso trascrizione **a richiesta del Comune di Forchia**.
> **Napoli 3-4-1939** — XVII»

**A five-year-old girl of Forchia died at ten o'clock at night on 31 July 1938 in the Ospedale
Pellegrini in Naples**, and the act was copied back to her own comune eight months later.

## Is she Carminantonio and Filomena's daughter?

**Probably, and it is not proved.**

**For:** the mother is **ANNECCHINO FILOMENA** — the exact and unusual pairing already documented in
this family, and the only Filomena Annecchino the Forchia records show in a Falco household. The child
is **of Forchia**, **aged five** in 1938, so **born about 1933** — which falls neatly among the known
children. And the household is already documented at Forchia in **August 1937**, when
**Carminantonio declared the death of their nine-month-old son ANIELLO**.

**Against:** the act names the father **«ANTONIO»**, not **Carminantonio**. It is a Naples act,
drawn from a hospital notice, so a shortening is easy — but it is a different word, and this archive
has been caught before treating a name plus a date as an identification.

**So it is entered as PROBABLE.** If it holds, **this household buried two children in eleven months**:
Aniello at nine months on 2 August 1937, and Maria Annunziata at five on 31 July 1938 — and the second
died in a hospital in Naples, forty kilometres from home.

## AND A MISTAKE OF MINE, WHICH IS THE OLD ONE

Reading the contact sheet, I said out loud that the index had the parents wrong — that the act gave
«*Angelo … Annecchino Anna … d'anni trentadue … moglie di Annecchino Gabriele*».

**None of that is in this act.** I was reading a **neighbouring entry on the facing page** and
attributing it to this one. Rendered properly, the act says exactly what the index said: **father
Antonio, mother Filomena Annecchino.**

**This archive has published this failure before**, in almost the same words: *«reading a horizontal
band of a register page is not reading an entry — anchor on the margin label and the entry's own
first and last words.»* It happened again today, on the first pass, and the only thing that caught it
was rendering the entry on its own before writing anything down.
