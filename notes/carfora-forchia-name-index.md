# THE CARFORA OF FORCHIA — 260 records, and a door that turns out to be shut

**13 September 2026.** Harvested Antenati's name index for **CARFORA at FORCHIA**: **260 records**,
**190 births, 59 deaths, 11 marriages**, in `data/antenati-names-carfora-forchia.tsv`.

**The target was MARIA CARFORA** — wife of **Carmine Annecchino**, and, if the family tree is right,
**Filomena Annecchino's mother**. The archive calls that descent *inferred, not documented*, and a
single act naming Maria Carfora's own parents would change that.

## She is in the index — and only ever as somebody's mother

The index shows her exactly where it should:

> **Maria Carfora** — *Figlio=* **Giacinta Annecchino** · *Figlio=* **Agata Annecchino** ·
> *Figlio=* **Alfonso Carmine Annecchino**

**and nowhere else.** Every one of those rows carries **no birth data of her own** — no date, no place,
no parents. The index links her to her children's acts and to nothing that would identify her.

## Three routes, all shut

1. **Her own birth act** — not linked. Forchia has plenty of candidates in the right window
   (Maria Antonia 1861, Maria Filomena 1863, Maria Sabata 1863, Maria Carmina 1863, Maria Grazia 1865,
   Maria Francesca 1866, Maria Giovanna 1867, 1869…) and **no way to choose among them**. Her first
   child was born in 1883, so she was born about 1860–65; that narrows nothing.
2. **Her marriage to Carmine Annecchino**, about 1880–82 — **inside the Forchia gap**. Antenati's
   Forchia marriages run **1809–1865 and then 1931–1941**. Nothing between.
3. **Her death** — **not in the indexed window**. All 59 Carfora death acts were read: **not one has an
   ANNECCHINO spouse.**

**So Maria Carfora's parents are not reachable through Antenati at all**, and this is recorded so the
attempt is not repeated.

## What the harvest is worth anyway

**The Carfora of Forchia are now mapped**, with parents, as households rather than names:
**Carmine × Raffaela Crisci · Antonio × Maria Papa · Gabriele × Lucia Crisci · Michele × Maria Rosa
Fusco · Agostino × Porzia D'Ambrosio · Carmine × Nicoletta Cimmino · Costantino × Maria Crisci ·
Agostino × Carmina Goffredo · Michele × Stella Maietta · Carmine × Alessandra D'Onofrio.**

**And two of them came from Arienzo.** **ROSA CARFORA**, born **Arienzo**, died Forchia; **MICHELE
CARFORA**, born **Arienzo**, died Forchia. The Carfora were walking the **same corridor as the Falco** —
Arienzo up the valley to Forchia — a generation or two earlier. **No kinship is claimed**; it is the
road that is the point, not the blood.
