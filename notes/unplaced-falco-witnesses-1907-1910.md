# Falco witnesses and declarants held unplaced, 1907-1910 (checked 15 Nov 2026)

Rule: a witness or declarant given only by name, age and trade is not placed in a household. A name identifies nobody; the ages are guessed. Each entry below was re-checked against the walk's notes for any independent tie (his own street, his wife or father named, «parente», a margin) and none was found, so all stay unplaced. This is the ledger a later second tie would settle.

## Morti 1907 and 1908 (declarants)
- Giovanni Falco, contadino, declarant in Morti 1907 act 1 (55) and act 13 (62): named by forename, age and trade; the street is the deceased's. Two Giovanni Falco are alive in the archive at once, so even the two acts are only «very likely» one man.
- Pasquale Falco, 37, trainante, declarant in Morti 1907 act 44 (level 13): not tied to the Pasquale Falco and Angelamaria Attanasio house of Corso Caudino on a name and a trade; ages 37 here, 40 in 1906 and 1907 do not settle it.
- Giovanni Falco, 58, and Giuseppe Falco, 60, contadini, declarants in Morti 1908 act 44 (the death of Crescenzo Falco's infant son in via Orticelli 2): the dead child is placed on his parents, street and interval; the two declarants are not placed on a name.

## Nascite 1908-1910 (witnesses)
- Pasquale Falco, 41, trainante, witness in Nascite 1908 act 48 (level 13): name, trade and age agree with the Corso Caudino Pasquale Falco, but the act does not say where the witness lives and the house number is the declarant's. Also witness in act 110 of 1908, and Pasquale Falco, 42, trainante, in Nascite 1910 act 41.
- Carmine Falco, 53, trainante, witness in Nascite 1909 acts 5 and 71 (level 13); Carmine Falco, 55, trainante, in Nascite 1910 act 54: probably one man, and a name, a trade and two ages are the whole of that.
- Antonio Falco, 50, contadino, witness in Nascite 1909 act 23: the Antonio Falco and Carmela Guida house holds an Antonio Falco, contadino, written 60 in 1905 and 63 in 1907, which does not settle a 50.
- Giuseppe Falco, 64, contadino, in Nascite 1910 act 28 and Giuseppe Falco, 57, contadino, in act 30 (level 13); Angelo Falco, 43, contadino, in Nascite 1910 act 108 (level 13). No ties.
- Pasquale Falco, 44, in Nascite 1883 act 66 (the act whose margin names the Ruotolo and Falco Annunciata marriage): witness only.

## What would settle one
A second document naming the same man with a wife, father, street or occupation that only one house can match: a marriage act of his children, a death act of his own, a conscription roll, a will. None of these is in the registers read.
