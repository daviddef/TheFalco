# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1859 — read end to end (`an_ua14456`)

> **Delegated read, 18 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand.**
> A delegated reader read every act of this volume at contact-sheet resolution (about 1,100 px a half
> page) and wrote a roster of it; **every Falco act, in any role, was then re-opened at about 2,400 px and
> read from the image again**, and a random sample of the other acts (acts 18–19 and 24–25) was compared with
> the manuscript — **no name in the sample disagreed**. The roster is therefore a *finding aid*: ages,
> trades and many non-Falco surnames in it are approximate and are not to be cited. What the
> reader's text below calls "confirmed" is what was re-opened; where it says "survey" the reading was not.
> The households this produced are in `households.json` (row evidence names the act and the image);
> the rows for the volume are in `searched.json` and on `/searched`.

## Volume facts
- Portal label: Arienzo > 1859, 80 images.
- **Year proved from the manuscript**: image 2 is the printed title «Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento cinquantanove» (Distretto di Caserta, Comune di Arienzo); image 3R is headed «Tavola alfabetica de' nati per l'anno 1859»; every act reads «L'anno mille ottocento cinquantanove» (acts 141 and 143 read at 3,400 px). Act 1 is 1/2 January 1859; the last act (144) is 27 December 1859 (registered 27/28 Dec).
- **TAVOLA PRESENT, AT THE FRONT** — images 3R, 4L, 4R, 5L, 5R, 6L, 6R, 7L (pages I–V; alphabetical by the CHILD's surname, with number, both parents, birth date). The **F page is complete on image 4R**: three entries only — **89 Ferraro Antonio** (D. Francesco × Cioffi D. Carm.), **111 Finelli Elisabetta Carmela Francesca Paola** (D. Domenico × Vottetto? Anna), and **141 FALCO Costanza** (Luigi × Cimmino Carolina, 22 Dec). The F group is closed by the «G» heading below it on the same page, so the page-break rule does not apply. I did not count the whole tavola against N = 144.
- **Layout**: image 1 cover, image 2 title page, images 3R–7L tavola, **acts begin at 7R** (act 1). Each page carries one complete act; L = even act, R = odd act from act 2 on: 7R=1, 8L=2 … 79L=144; 79R is blank printed form 145, struck through, marginal «END». Image 80 = blank. One band (0.10–0.74, both halves) reads the whole act.
- **N = 144 acts**, numbers 1..144 unbroken and unduplicated (script check).
- Legibility: all 144 acts read. Nine acts are «poor» in the TSV (a forename/trade/surname only approximate): 18, 40, 41, 47, 51, 58, 59, 61, 63. No tear or stain hides a name.
- Midwives: Rosa Balletta (most), Rosa Morgillo (fu Marco), Marianna Bernardo, Rosa Crisci.

## Falco appearances: TWO acts (both read at ~3,400 px and cropped)

### Act 141 (image 77R) — CONFIRMED — Falco FATHER
«Numero di ordine 141. L'anno mille ottocento cinquantanove il dì ventitré di Dicembre alle ore sedici avanti di noi Stefano Scarpati sindaco … è comparsa **Rosa Morgillo** figlia del fu Marco, di anni cinquantotto, levatrice, domiciliata a Santa Lucia, quale ci ha presentato una femina … è nata da **Carolina Cimmino** di anni trenta, domiciliata a **Capodiconca**, e da **Luigi Falco** marito della stessa di anni trentasei, contadino, domiciliato colla moglie, nel giorno ventidue del suddetto mese alle ore ventitré, nella casa de' suddetti conjugi.» Name **Costanza** (parish column: baptised «Costanza Falco»). No margin note. The tavola row 141 agrees (Costanza; Luigi e Cimmino Carolina; 22 Dec). Same couple as 1858 act 48 (Pasquale, Capodiconca).

### Act 143 (image 78R) — CONFIRMED — Falco MOTHER
«Numero di ordine 143 … il dì ventisette(?) di Dicembre alle ore sedici … Rosa Morgillo levatrice … una femina … è nata da **Maria Falco** di anni quaranta, domiciliata a **Strada Nuova**, e da **Raffaele Morgillo** marito della stessa di anni ventotto, colono, domiciliato colla moglie, nel giorno ventisei del suddetto mese alle ore ventitré, nella casa de' suddetti conjugi.» Name **Antonia** (parish: baptised «Antonia Morgillo»). Witnesses Pietro Grazia (fruttajuolo, Porta di Sopra) and Pellegrino Saccovino. No margin note. This child is NOT in the F page of the tavola (indexed under Morgillo, as expected for a Falco MOTHER). The act date line reads the 27th/28th: the registration day is uncertain at survey scale; the birth was the evening of the 26th.
Note: a Maria Falco aged 40 married to a man 12 years her junior, strada Nuova — compare the archive's «Maria Falco» households before attaching her.

## What is proven
- All 144 acts viewed (both halves of images 7–79) and transcribed to `1859.tsv`; tavola images 3–7 viewed; the F page read.
- Two Falco acts: 141 (father Luigi, 22 Dec 1859, Costanza) and 143 (mother Maria, 26 Dec 1859, Antonia Morgillo). The tavola confirms no other Falco CHILD (an independent check for the father-side); the survey of the acts' parent, declarant and witness lines found no other Falco as mother, declarant, midwife, witness or in a margin.
- Limits: the tavola was not counted entry-for-entry against 144; the nine «poor» acts have approximate surnames; marginal marriage notes were read at survey resolution.
