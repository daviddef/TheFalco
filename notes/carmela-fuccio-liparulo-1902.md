# Carmela Fuccio and Pietro Liparulo, 1902

**What was found (15 November 2026).** The banns of 14 June 1902 (Pubblicazioni 1902 Parte I act 8, MC55-RM9 image 135) and the marriage act of 15 September 1902 (Matrimoni 1902 Parte I act 11, image 151) of Pietro Liparulo, 28, calzolaio, and Carmela Fuccio, 20, cucitrice, daughter of Raffaele, sarto, 48, and of «Falco Andreana».

**Why she is placed.** Her birth act (Nascite 1882 act 3, 30 December 1881, twin of Marianna) was already in the house of Andrea Falco and Maria Carmela Buono, and its margin, a later hand, says «Con atto 11 Sett. 1902 N. 11 in Arienzo la Fuccio sposò Liparulo Pietro». The banns and the marriage name the same groom, the number is the act's number, her age is 20 and her father is the same trade and name. The margin was a lead until the act was opened.

**The disagreement.** The margin gives 11 September; the marriage act is dated «quindici di settembre» (level 13, z902). The number agrees, the day does not; the act's own date is used. The register offers no explanation and none is claimed.

**What the acts add.** Andreana Falco, the bride's mother, is alive and resident at Arienzo on 14 June 1902: her husband declares that she does not present herself to consent. The last word of that clause (banns) and of the marriage act's matching clause is not settled at level 13, so no reason is claimed.

**What is still inferred.** That this Raffaele Fuccio's wife is the house's Andreana, b. 1854, rests on her name, the sarto Raffaele and the 1875 marriage to a Faccia (Faccia/Fuccio/Fuccia); no act of these registers names Andrea Falco as her father.

**Also corrected.** The row of Angela Rosa Falco (daughter of Carminantonio and Giovanna Arricale) labelled «marriage» with the date of the banns is now the banns (20 August 1902, Pubblicazioni 1902 act 9), and the marriage itself (Matrimoni 1902 act 12, 24 September 1902) has its own row.

**Not a Falco:** the officer of Pubblicazioni 1902 Parte I act 11 is written «Giacomo De Falco», assessore anziano funzionante da Sindaco: an office-holder, not a party.
