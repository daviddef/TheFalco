Acts run 5 January – 29 December 1833. Images 56–58 are the closing formalities
(cancelliere Felice Morgillo's signature and the Procuratore del Re's printed certificate).

## The blank index — why these years must be read linearly
Image 56 carries a printed form headed **"TAVOLA alfabetica annuale de' Morti per l'anno
mille ottocento…"** — an annual alphabetical index of deaths, with ruled columns for surname,
forename, *patria*, profession, parents, and date of death. **It was bound in and left entirely
blank.** The clerk signed the closing certificate and never filled the table.

This is the concrete reason the 1829–43 search could not be shortcut. It is not that the volumes
lack an index; it is that Arienzo's index pages were never completed.

## The one Falco of 1833

### Pasquale Falco, aged 3 — image 9 (left), act no. 16

> Num. d'ordine **sedici**. L'anno milleottocento **trentatre**, il dì **ventidue** del mese di
> **Febrajo**, alle ore quindici, avanti di noi **Francesco d'Ambrosio, Sindaco** ed uffiziale
> dello Stato Civile del Comune di **Arienzo**, Distretto di **Caserta**, Provincia di
> **Terra di Lavoro**, sono comparsi **Grazio Torrino**, di anni quaranta, di professione
> contadino, domiciliato **strada Camellara**, vicino del defunto, ed **Angelo Lombardo**, di
> anni trenta, di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi, strada Camellara, vicino del defunto,
> i quali han dichiarato, che nel giorno **ventidue** del mese di **Febrajo**, anno corrente,
> **alle ore una di notte**, è morto nella sua propria casa **PASQUALE FALCO, di anni tre**,
> nato in Arienzo, domiciliato ivi, **figlio di VINCENZO FALCO**, di professione contadino,
> domiciliato ivi, **e di ANDREANA CRISCI**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/pasquale-falco-child-death-1833.jpg`

**What it adds**
1. **Vincenzo Falco and Andreana Crisci appear together as a household in a civil record**, at
   their address. Until now the couple was attested only by their marriage act of 15 December
   1814 and by their son Raffaele's baptism of 1818.
2. **A new child for that household**: Pasquale, born c. 1830, died aged three at one in the
   morning on 22 February 1833.
3. **Vincenzo used his father's name twice and lost the child both times.** This Pasquale died in
   February 1833, while the grandfather Pasquale was still living. The grandfather died 1 October
   1834; ten weeks later Vincenzo's next son was born and given the name again, and died at ten
   days old on 25 December 1834. Two children, one name, twenty-two months apart.
4. **Strada Camellara again** — and the declarants are again the neighbours: Grazio Torrino and
   Angelo Lombardo, both *contadini* of that street.

Corsi (Maria, 10; Grazia, 9 months; Alessandra, widow of Vincenzo Corsi; Maria Torrino w. Simone
