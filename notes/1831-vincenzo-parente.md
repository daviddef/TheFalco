# The first documentary link between the direct line and a Camellara household

**Arienzo, Morti 1831 — `an_ua14333`, image 19 (right), page 67, atto no. 33.**

> L'anno mille ottocento **trentuno**, il dì **quattro** del mese di **Maggio**, alle ore quindici,
> avanti di noi **Andrea Sorice, secondo eletto** ed uffiziale dello Stato Civile del Comune di
> Arienzo, **Distretto di Nola**, Provincia di Terra di Lavoro, sono comparsi **Andrea Crisci**, di
> anni cinquantotto, di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi, **strada Camellara**, *vicino della
> defunta*, e **VINCENZO FALCO**, di anni **trentotto**, di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi,
> **strada Camellara**, ***parente della defunta***, i quali han dichiarato, che nel giorno quattro
> del mese di Maggio, anno corrente, alle ore tredici, è morta nella sua propria casa
> **ANNA FALCO, di mesi tredici**, nata in Arienzo, domiciliata ivi, **figlia di MICHELANGELO FALCO**,
> di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi, **e di MARIANTONIA VIGLIOTTA**.

## Why this matters

The clerk distinguished the two declarants deliberately: Andrea Crisci is *vicino* — a **neighbour**.
**Vincenzo Falco is *parente* — a kinsman.**

**Vincenzo Falco, aged 38 in 1831, of strada Camellara, is generation 3 of this line.** Born 1793;
thirty-eight in May 1831 fits exactly. He is the son of Pasquale Falco and Chiara Rivetti, the husband
of Andreana Crisci, and the father of Raffaele.

So a civil act, sworn before the officer of the Stato Civile, records that **the direct line and the
Michelangelo Falco household of strada Camellara are related.** Until now the archive could only say
that several Falco households shared one street and decline to join them. This joins one of them.

**What it does not say** is *how*. *Parente* is the general word for kinsman — brother, cousin, uncle,
nephew. Michelangelo could be Vincenzo's brother (Pasquale and Chiara's known children do not include
a Michelangelo, but the baptism register has gaps), or a first cousin through Matteo's brothers, or
further out. The act establishes the fact of kinship, not its degree.

## Also new
- **Mariantonia Vigliotta** — Michelangelo Falco's wife, a name the archive did not have.
- Michelangelo and Mariantonia named a daughter **Anna** twice: this one, thirteen months old, dead
  4 May 1831, and another Anna, eight days old, dead 7 March 1834.
- The act gives the district as **Nola**, where the 1832–34 acts give **Caserta**. Arienzo's district
  appears to have been re-drawn between 1831 and 1832. Recorded as observed; not yet verified.

## The consequence for the archive
The Camellara cluster is no longer simply "unjoined". One of its four households is documented as
kin. The others — Michele, Francesco & Raffaela — remain unjoined, and the honest position is now:
**one joined, three not.**
