# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1847 — read end to end (`an_ua14444`)

> **Delegated read, 25 October 2026; every Falco act re-opened by the archive's own hand at ~2,800 px.** The reader's roster is a finding aid; its non-Falco rows are triage-grade and are not cited. Households: `households.json`; row: `searched.json`.

**Ark:** an_ua14444. Catalogue label "1847". **113 images.** Archivio di Stato di Caserta > Stato civile napoleonico e della restaurazione > Arienzo > 1847.

## Year proof (manuscript)
Image 1 right page, printed title: «Registro degli atti dello stato civile ... Degli atti di Nascita a norma del libro 1.° tit.° 2.° della parte prima del Codice per lo Regno delle Due Sicilie. Dal primo Gennajo a tutto il dì trentuno Dicembre mille ottocento quarantasette.» Image 1 left page is the 1849 ministerial notice (verso of the back cover form). Every act opens «L'anno mille ottocento quarantasette». Act 1 is dated 9 Gennajo 1847 (born 8 Gennajo); last act 107 is dated 28 Dicembre 1847; closing certificate (L img 111) dated 31 Dicembre 1847. The label is right.

## Counts
- First act: no. 1, 9 Gennajo 1847 (R img 2 + L img 3). Last act: no. 107, 28 Dicembre 1847 (R img 110 + L img 111).
- **107 acts**, numbered 1..107 with no gap and no repeat (checked against the TSV). Closing certificate on L img 111, signed by Domenico Finelli, sindaco, and Lorenzo Viggiano, cancelliere: «registro di fogli scritti ... contenenti atti numero centosette». The printed pencil folio numbers run 111 (title) ... 218 (act 107), 219 (blank form 108), 220 «END» (ministerial note).
- Blank printed forms: act 108 (R img 111 + L img 112; struck through with diagonals) and 109 (R img 112); **none filled**. Image 112 carries a film target «BLANK PAGES».
- Tavola: **none found.** The only index-like object is a narrow lined strip on the left page of image 113 (the spread ends with the 1849 note on the right): a column of act numbers (18, 45, 35, 60, 62, 31, 31, 103, 7, 24, 28, 39, 46, 3?, 29?, 94, 99, 106) beside a column of names which is hidden under the folded page edge; a film target says «THIS INDEX HAS BEEN MICROFILMED ALREADY». **Not readable; the F page was not seen.** Cross-check against a tavola is therefore not possible for this volume; the acts were read one by one.

## Layout
As in the brief: act N = right page of image k + left page of image k+1; the image k for each act is in the TSV (img_open/img_close). Because of the two duplicate frames (images 7 and 48) and the title leaf, the offset between act number and image number changes from 1 (acts 1-4) to 2 (from image 8) to 3 (from image 49). Margin notes (marriages, 1870-1892) are written in the right-hand column of the LEFT page. Officer is Vincenzo Martone, secondo eletto (most acts) or Domenico Finelli, sindaco (a minority, e.g. acts 66, 70, 82, 86, 102, 103-107); cancelliere Lorenzo Viggiano.

## Damage / legibility
Whole volume legible at 3000 px; the clerk's hand is cursive and many ages, first names of mothers and street names are doubtful; these are marked `?` in the TSV. Surnames of declarants/fathers were read from the signature or declarant line and usually confirmed by the INDICAZIONE (baptism) line.

Note on the brief's lead: the lead called the Andrea Falco act "act 17"; the printed number is **15** (pencil folio 126). The lead's "act 48 witness «Michele Fa?io»" is confirmed as a Michele Falco witness; I read the surname as Falco (identity with the clearly written Michele Falco of acts 69, 94, 98: same age 42-43, same trade, same street, same clerk).
No Falco mother, no midwife, no Falco margin note anywhere in 1847.

## Limitations
- All 107 acts were read at about 3000 px, both halves; but many rows (names of mothers, ages, trades, witnesses) are transcribed with `?` and are **triage-grade for non-Falco persons**; the Falco acts (15, 92) and the Falco witness lines (48, 69, 94, 98) were re-rendered at 3000-3400 px and are solid.
- Act 48's surname letter (the fifth) is not independently legible; the identification rests on the clerk's repeated witness.
- Tavola strip (img 113) not readable.
- Dates of acts with `?` were read from the R page header and may be off by a day where the numeral is faint.
