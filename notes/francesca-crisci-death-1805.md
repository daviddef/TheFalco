# FRANCESCA CRISCI, WIDOW OF MATTEO FALCO — 31 December 1805

**Arienzo parish death register, ark `3Q9M-CSMY-B772-V`. READ FROM THE IMAGE.** Margin: «**Francisca
Crisci**».

> «**Anno D[omi]ni 1805, die vero 31 Decembris, FRANCISCA CRISCI VIDUA MAT-THEI FALCO, aetatis suae
> annorum 60 circiter**, omnibus Sacramentis mu[ni]ta, in Comm[unione] S. M. E. **obiit**, et **in
> Sodalitate S. Mariae Gratiarum** ejus cadaver humatum est. **Hyacinthus Archip[resbyte]r Morgillo**»

The surname breaks across the line — «Vidua **Mat**‑ / **thei** Falco» — which is why the machine
rendered it «Manel» and why this took three attempts.

---

## THE FOUNDING COUPLE IS NOW CLOSED AT BOTH ENDS

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **MATTEO FALCO** | **18 July 1802**, aged about **55** | buried **in the archpriest's cemetery** |
| **FRANCESCA CRISCI** | **31 December 1805**, aged about **60** | buried **in the confraternity of Santa Maria delle Grazie** |

**Born about 1747 and about 1745.** She was three and a half years a widow.

### And every number now fits

She bore a child in **January 1786**, at about **forty-one** — ordinary. She was about **two years
older** than her husband — ordinary. Her children run from **1767 to 1786**, from about twenty-two to
about forty-one — ordinary. **Nothing about this couple is strange any more**, and that is itself the
confirmation: the readings that made them strange were the wrong readings.

## HOW THE AGE WAS GOT WRONG THREE TIMES IN ONE DAY

| reading | source | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **«ottanta», about eighty** | my paraphrase of a garbled machine word, «annovembocircite» | **an over-read of my own source.** Withdrawn the same hour, and the objection built on it withdrawn with it |
| **«70»** | read off the image — **from the entry BELOW** | **the age of FRANCESCA CILLO, uxor Vincentii Laudato**, who died on 1 January 1806. Not hers at all |
| **«60»** | **the act itself**, anchored on its own margin label | **correct** |

> **The lesson is not «the machine is unreliable» — the archive knew that.** It is that **reading a
> horizontal band of a register page is not reading an entry.** Twice in one afternoon a number was
> taken from the line above or below the one it belonged to. **Anchor on the margin label and on the
> entry's own first and last words**, every time, or the page will hand you a neighbour's facts.
