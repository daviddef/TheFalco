# Arienzo civil BIRTH register 1816 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14413` (Arienzo, Nati 1816), 66 images. Read in full, images 2–62.
**Acts:** 1 – **123** (the closing certification on image 63 states the register closed at
*numero d'ordine centoventitre*). Images 63–65 are that certification, the annual index, and a
printed ministerial circular of 4 July 1849 on removing blank leaves from civil registers.

## The index — and why it does not help
Image 63 carries a **filled "Tavola Annuale Alfabetica"**, unlike the death registers whose tavole
Arienzo left blank. But it is indexed by the **child's forename**, not by surname, and the father's
surname column is lost in the gutter behind a loose inserted leaf. It cannot be used to search for
Falco; the year still has to be read. A modern archival label on the page reads
*"THIS INDEX HAS BEEN MICROFILMED ALREADY."*

**Act 56 unsettles generation four** — the same couple named another son Raffaele on 10 May 1818.
See `notes/1818-birth-register-progress.md` and the open questions page.

**Act 116 is a different Giuseppe Falco** from the *contadino* of strada Camellara who married
Antonia di Guida. Two men of one name, both thirty-six within two years, different streets, different
trades, different wives. They must not be merged.

## Strada Camellara in 1816
**Falco** (Vincenzo), **Saccavino** (Virgilio, 38 — the man who reported Pasquale Falco's death from
that street eighteen years later), Martone (Giuseppe, Matteo), Laudato (Domenico), Loffreda (Pietro),
Grazia (Francesco), di Giglio (Carmine), and **Sara Ruggiero the midwife**.

## Maria Rosa Falco: not here either
She is absent from **1816, 1817 and 1818**. Sixteen at her death in May 1834 should put her in one of
them. Either the age was loose by more than two years, or she was born outside Arienzo, or she was
baptised without a civil act. **Michele Falco of strada Camellara remains undated.**
