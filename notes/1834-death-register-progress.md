## Falco found in 1834
### 1. Pasquale Falco — THE TARGET, FOUND (image 43 of 57, page 236, act no. 69)

> L'anno mille ottocento **trentaquattro**, il dì **primo** del mese di **Ottobre**, alle ore **dodici**,
> avanti di noi **Francesco d'Ambrosio, Sindaco** ed uffiziale dello Stato Civile del Comune di
> **Arienzo**, Distretto di **Caserta**, Provincia di **Terra di Lavoro**, sono comparsi
> **Virgilio Saccavino**, di anni quarantacinque, di professione contadino, regnicolo, domiciliato
> ivi, **strada Camellara**, vicino del defunto, e **Nicola Corsi**, di anni trenta, di professione
> contadino, regnicolo, domiciliato ivi, strada Camellara, vicino del defunto, i quali han dichiarato,
> che nel giorno **primo** del mese di **Ottobre**, anno corrente, alle ore **cinque**, è morto nella
> sua propria casa **PASQUALE FALCO**, d'anni **settantaquattro**, **marito di CHIARA RIVETTI**,
> di professione **contadino**, nato in Arienzo, domiciliato ivi, **figlio di MATTEO FALCO, defunto**,
> di professione contadino, domiciliato ivi, **e di FRANCESCA CRISCI, defunta**, domiciliata ivi.

Everything checks: the wife, both parents, the town, the trade, the street.

**What it settles**
- **Pasquale Falco died 1 October 1834** at five in the morning, at home on strada Camellara.
  The 1829–1843 bracket closes.
- **Chiara Rivetti was still living** — he is *marito di*, not the record of a widower.
  She outlived him by nine years (d. 16 Sep 1843).
- **Matteo Falco and Francesca Crisci were both already dead** by October 1834.
- The act **independently confirms the parentage** taken from the 1767 baptism, from a different
  record series and a different institution — civil, not parish.

**The one discrepancy:** the stated age of 74 implies birth c. 1760; his baptism is 24 October 1767,
which would make him 66. Stated ages in these registers are routinely loose, and the act was given by
two neighbours, not by family. The parentage and the wife are decisive; the age is not.

### 2. Pasquale Falco the younger — his grandson and namesake (image 53, right)

> …nel giorno **venticinque** del mese di **Dicembre**, anno corrente, alle ore dieci, è morto nella
> sua propria casa **Pasquale Falco, di giorni dieci**, nato in Arienzo, domiciliato ivi,
> **figlio di Vincenzo Falco**… [strada Camellara]

**Vincenzo Falco is Pasquale's eldest son** — documented at his own marriage, 15 December 1814,
Arienzo act no. 26: *"Vincenzo Falco, 21, contadino, figlio maggiore di Pasquale Falco"*, m. Andreana
Crisci. This ten-day-old was born about 15 December 1834, eleven weeks after his grandfather died,
and given his name — the ordinary Neapolitan custom. He lived ten days.

It also places **Vincenzo's household on strada Camellara** in 1834, the same street as his father's.

### 3. Maria Rosa Falco, 16 (image 23, right) — 30 May 1834
*Contadina*, born Arienzo, **daughter of Michele Falco, contadino, strada Camellara**. Declarant
Francesco Mascariello, 22, contadino, of the same street. Michele's household also buried
Salvatore Falco, 14 months, in August 1836.

### 4. Anna Falco, 8 days (image 10, left) — 7 March 1834
**Daughter of Michelangelo Falco, contadino, strada Camellara.** Declarant Tomaso Esposito, 70,
contadino, same street. Michelangelo is a name new to the archive.

## Strada Camellara
1834 turns the street into the Falco address. Living on it in this one year:
**Pasquale** (d. 1 Oct), **Vincenzo** his son, **Michele**, **Michelangelo**, and — from the 1836
register — **Francesco**. Every one of them a *contadino*. The gentry Falco of Arienzo
(Don Francesco, *proprietario*; his daughter Donna Raffaela, *monaca*) live elsewhere in the town
and remain a separate, unjoined branch.
