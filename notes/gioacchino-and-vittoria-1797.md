# A DAUGHTER IN 1797 — and a problem it makes for Gioacchino's age

**Arienzo parish baptisms, ark `3Q9M-CSD3-13K1-N`** — FamilySearch machine transcription, **the image
has not been read**:

> «Anno D[omi]ni Millesimo Septingentesimo **nonagesimo septimo 1797**, die vero [sesta?] **6 Maii**,
> R[everen]dus Canonicus D. **Vincentius Scarpato** Substitutus baptizavit infantem natam ex
> **JOACHIMO FALCO e VICTORIA CEMMINO** conjugibus hujus Parochiae, cui dedi nomen **JOANNA**.
> Matrina fuit **Fortunata Villano**. Hyacinthus Archipresbyter Morgillo.»

**GIOACCHINO FALCO and VITTORIA CIMMINO are the couple this archive proved in February**: the 1851
death act names Gioacchino as «figlio di **MATTEO FALCO** defunto … e di **FRANCESCA CRISCI** defunta»
and his wife as **Vittoria Cimmino**. A daughter **GIOVANNA, baptised 6 May 1797**, is a child of
theirs this archive did not have.

## AND HERE IS THE PROBLEM

| source | what it says | Gioacchino born |
|---|---|---|
| **Death act, 14 Feb 1851** | «di anni **SETTANTASEI**» | **c. 1775** |
| **Parish baptism, 25 Nov 1774** *(image read)* | «Joachim Falco» — **and the margin carries a cross and «obiit die 11 Decembris 1777»** | that boy **died at three** |
| **Parish baptism, 2 Feb 1779** *(transcription)* | «qui nominatus est JOACHIM», ex Mattheo Falco et Francisca Crisci | **1779** |
| **This baptism, 6 May 1797** | Joachim Falco is **a married father** | **on or before c. 1778** if he was twenty at the birth |

**If our Gioacchino is the boy of February 1779, he married at about seventeen and was a father at
eighteen.** Possible — and not what this archive would have guessed.

### Three readings, none of them established

1. **He is the 1779 boy, and he married very young.** The correction published this morning stands:
   he was **seventy-two** at death, and the 1851 clerk's «seventy-six» was four years out.
2. **The 1797 father is a different Gioacchino Falco.** The town holds **at least two and probably
   three Falco stocks** — see `notes/the-other-falco-of-arienzo.md` — and a Gioacchino Falco appears
   as a **marriage witness** in the same period without any tie to this family.
3. **The marginal *obiit* of 1777 belongs to a neighbouring entry**, the 1774 boy lived, and the death
   act's «seventy-six» was right all along. *Least likely: the cross and the name are drawn beside
   each other and were read at magnification.*

**The archive does not know which, and nothing is moved on the strength of it.** What is published is
the daughter, the tension, and the three readings.

## And a late-19th-century couple, found in the same sweep

Ark `3Q9M-CSD3-13JJ-W` carries «…baptizavi infantem nat[am] ex legittimis coniugibus **JOACHIM FALCO
et ANDREANA CRISCI**, cui dedi nomen **CARMELA**», priest **Raphael Can. Cimmino**. The years on that
leaf are badly garbled by the machine (1591, 159, 1897), but the hand and the formula are late.

**This is not the 1797 couple.** The archive already holds a **Gioacchino/Guglielmo Falco × Andreana**
with children born at Arienzo in **1880, 1883 and 1891** — Raffaela, Giovanni and Carmina. **CARMELA
is a fourth**, and the act gives her mother's surname as **CRISCI**, which the archive did not have.
Transcription only; **the image has not been read and the year is not legible.**
