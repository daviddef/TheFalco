# The unplaced Falco acts of 1866–1906 (item 24), triaged 16 November 2026

The V-table ([arienzo-1866-1910-falco-acts.md](arienzo-1866-1910-falco-acts.md)) lists every act in which a Falco is a
principal. A text match of each V-line against `data/households.tsv` left **55** lines with no household row of the same
act. This note says what each one is. **Nothing was written to a household in this triage**: the FamilySearch pane was
signed out when the three candidates below needed their level-13 read, and a placement is not made from the delegated
table alone. (The rule for 1907–1910 declarants and witnesses stays in
[unplaced-falco-witnesses-1907-1910.md](unplaced-falco-witnesses-1907-1910.md).)

How the triage was done: the V-line's act date was compared with the household rows dated within six days and the first
names compared by eye. **That is a pointer, not a check**; where a row below says «already in a house», it means a house
row with the same date and a matching first name exists, and the act was not re-opened to confirm it.

written** (V-table cells are the delegated reader's, and some are cut short).

## What would move any of C

A second document: a marriage or death of the same woman, or a conscription or notarial paper naming her husband or father.
None is in the registers read.
