# THE MARRIAGE DOSSIER OF 1897 — generation six, with the documents it was built from

**14 September 2026.** FamilySearch collection **2043630** — identified yesterday as **the
processetti**, the *allegati* to the Caserta marriage registers, **1866–1929**. Generations five and
six both married inside that window at Arienzo. **Generation six's dossier is now open.**

> **«(1) 1897 — Fascicolo N. … Registro dei Matrimoni — FALCO RAFFAELE · MAIONE COSTANZA —
> (5) Numero 41, Parte 1ª — (6) DOCUMENTI INCLUSI N. SEI»** — `3QS7-L97N-1D75`

## 1 · The banns

**First publication 12 September 1897**, second **19 September**, both at the Casa Comunale of
Arienzo before **Raffaele de Marzo**, assessore delegate (`3QS7-997N-1DB4`, `3QS7-897N-18LC`).

## 2 · The groom's birth extract — and it corroborates two generations at once

> «*Estratto dal Registro degli atti di Nascita per l'anno **1873**, N. **39** — **RAFFAELE FALCO**.
> … nel giorno **otto** del mese **Aprile** … Dinanzi a me **Nicola Finelli, Sindaco** … Comune di
> **ARIENZO** … è comparso **CARMINANTONIO FALCO di Raffaele, contadino, di anni VENTIDUE**,
> domiciliato in Arienzo, **VIA DEGLI ORTI**, il quale mi ha presentato un bambino di sesso maschile
> che dichiara essergli nato **il giorno SEI corrente mese di Aprile ad ore ventidue** dalla di lui
> moglie **GIOVANNA ARRICALE fu FRANCESCO**, seco lui domiciliata … al quale figlio dichiara di dare
> il nome di **RAFFAELE**.*» — `3QS7-L97N-1D3Q`

- **Born 6 April 1873, registered the 8th.** The archive's date was already 6 April — **now confirmed
  from the act.**
- **His father is «CARMINANTONIO FALCO DI RAFFAELE, aged TWENTY-TWO»** → born about 1850–51. The
  archive has generation five as **Carmine Antonio Falco, born 10 July 1850**. **Corroborated
  independently, and the gen-5-to-gen-6 link is now documented rather than assumed.**
- **His mother is «GIOVANNA ARRICALE FU FRANCESCO».** The archive spells her *Arricole*; the act says
  **ARRICALE** — and it gives her father for the first time: **FRANCESCO ARRICALE, already dead by
  1873.**
- **The household lived in VIA DEGLI ORTI** — not Camellara. **Generation five had moved off the
  street**, and this is the first address the archive has for them.
- Witnesses: **Domenico Ponotolo fu Pietro**, 46, and **Bartolomeo Diglio di Antonio**, 23.

## 3 · The certificate of poverty — and a father for the bride

> «*IL SINDACO **Certifica, che FALCO RAFFAELE di CARMINANTONIO e MAIONE COSTANZA di ANIELLO**, nati e
> domiciliati in questo Comune, **SONO POVERI**.* — Arienzo, **29 Agosto 1897**» — `3QS7-L97N-1D7R`

- **COSTANZA MAIONE'S FATHER WAS ANIELLO MAIONE.** New.
- **They were certified paupers** so that the dossier could be drawn free of charge. Generation six
  married on a poverty certificate.

## AND, FROM THE SAME VOLUME OF ALLEGATI — NOT from this dossier

Bound a few leaves away, behind a document certified **31 October** and **23 November 1896** — so
belonging to **another couple's fascicolo** — is a death extract this archive has wanted:

> «*Estratto dal Registro degli atti di morte per l'anno **1883** = Num. **60** — **FALCO PASQUALE**.
> … addì **ventisette di Settembre** … sono comparsi **Pasquale Saccavino**, 30, contadino, e **Luigi
> Maione**, 41, contadino … che a ore pomeridiane tre e trenta **di ieri**, nella casa posta in **VIA
> CAMELLARA AL NUMERO CINQUE**, **è morto PASQUALE FALCO, di anni QUARANTAQUATTRO, agricoltore**,
> residente e nato in Arienzo, **del fu FRANCESCO, contadino**, … **e della fu RAFFAELA FALCO**,
> contadina, domiciliata in vita col marito, **marito di MARIA AMALIA GUIDA**.*» —
> `3QS7-897N-1DLW` / `3QS7-897N-1D23`

**That is the FRANCESCO FALCO × RAFFAELA FALCO household** — the one three separate index entries once
made look like three different Falco–Falco couples, until the acts dissolved them into one.

- **A son the archive did not have: PASQUALE FALCO, born about 1839, died 26 September 1883.**
- **His wife: MARIA AMALIA GUIDA.**
- **VIA CAMELLARA, NUMBER FIVE** — the **first house number** this archive has ever had for the street.
- **Francesco Falco was dead by 1883** («del fu Francesco»), and Raffaela too, which fits her death in
  1854.

**The attribution is stated carefully: this extract is in the same bound volume, not in Raffaele and
Costanza's fascicolo.** It was found because the volume is searchable, not because it belongs to them.

# THE PUBLICATION ACTS — two more households, from the same collection

## The banns of 26 August 1897 (`3QS7-997F-M4CW`) — the fullest statement of generation six

> «*è comparso **FALCO RAFFAELE, di anni VENTIQUATTRO**, contadino, residente in Arienzo, **figlio di
> CARMINANTONIO, di anni QUARANTASEI**, contadino, residente in Arienzo, e figlio di **GIOVANNA
> ARRICALE**, contadina, residente in Arienzo. E pure comparsa: **MAIONE COSTANZA, di anni VENTUNO**,
> contadina, residente in Arienzo, **figlia di ANIELLO, di anni CINQUANTASEI**, contadino, residente
> in Arienzo, **e figlia di MARTONE ANNA**, contadina, residente in Arienzo*»

- **COSTANZA MAIONE'S MOTHER WAS ANNA MARTONE.** New — the archive had neither parent until today,
  and now has both, with the father's age.
- **ANIELLO MAIONE was fifty-six in August 1897** → born about **1841**, a *contadino*, **still
  living**, as was his wife.
- **Costanza was twenty-one** → born about 1876, against a recorded **2 May 1876**. Confirmed.
- **Carminantonio was forty-six** → born about **1851**, against a recorded **10 July 1850**.

## The banns of August 1902 (`3QS7-8976-3K95`) — A DAUGHTER FOR GENERATION FIVE

> «*Esposito **FRANCESCO**, di anni **ventisei**, colono, residente in **San Felice a Cancello**,
> figlio di **PAOLO**, di anni **sessantatre**, colono, … e figlio **della fu MORGILLO MARTA** …
> E pure comparsa: **FALCO ANGELA ROSA, di anni VENTIDUE**, contadina, **figlia di CARMINANTONIO**,
> residente in Arienzo, di anni **cinquantacinque**, contadino, **e figlia di ARRICALE GIOVANNA**,
> contadina*»

**ANGELA ROSA FALCO, born about 1880, daughter of Carminantonio Falco and Giovanna Arricale** — **a
child of generation five this archive did not have**, and Raffaele's sister. She married **FRANCESCO
ESPOSITO** of **San Felice a Cancello**, son of **Paolo Esposito** and the late **MARTA MORGILLO**;
the banns ran 24 August, 31 August and 4 September 1902.

**She is named ANGELA ROSA after her own grandmother** — **Angela Rosa Morgillo**, generation four's
wife, who had died in April 1880, about when this child was born. **The naming-after-the-dead runs
right through this family**, and here it is again in living memory rather than in Latin.

**And the groom's mother was a MORGILLO of San Felice a Cancello** — which is exactly what generation
four's wife was. **No kinship is claimed**; the two families were clearly still moving in the same
small orbit two generations on.

## A discrepancy, published rather than smoothed

**Carminantonio's age is given as FORTY-SIX in August 1897 and FIFTY-FIVE in August 1902** — five
years apart, nine years of ageing. The archive has already documented that the Arienzo clerks guessed
ages; **this is the same phenomenon inside one man's own lifetime, in two documents five years apart.**
The recorded birth of **10 July 1850** sits between the two.
