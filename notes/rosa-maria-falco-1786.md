# «PROJA» WAS ROSA MARIA — and the founders had one child fewer than we thought

**13 September 2026, read from the image** at `3Q9M-CSD3-13XZ-1`.

## The act

> «… **Sabbatinus Can[oni]cus CRISCI Substituens baptizavit infantem NATAM ex MATTHEO FALCO, et
> FRANCISCA CRISCI Con[jugibus] hujus Parochiae, que nominata est ROSA MARIA**; … Caecilia Martone …»

Margin: **«Rosa Falco»**. The act immediately below it opens «*Anno D[omi]ni Millesimo septingentesimo
octogesimo sexto **1786**, die vero **sexta 6 Januarii***», which brackets hers at on or before
6 January 1786 and is consistent with the machine's **5 January**.

**The midwife is Cecilia Martone** — the same *obstetrix* who attended **Gioacchino's birth in
February 1779**. One woman delivered at least two of this family's children seven years apart.

## What it corrects

The archive was carrying this child **twice**:

- as **«a daughter, name not legible (machine gives «proja»), 5 Jan 1786»** — an unidentified infant,
  counted as a child of her own;
- and as **ROSA FALCO**, a woman with a life: married **Carlo Antonio Morgillo, 12 July 1802**, died
  **strada Camellara, 16 October 1830**, «*d'anni quarantadue*».

**They are one person.** The founders had **one child fewer** than the list said.

**The age does not quite fit, and it is published anyway.** Baptised January 1786 she is **forty-four**
in October 1830, not forty-two. The Arienzo clerks guessed ages — this archive has documented that
elsewhere with a whole series — and two years is inside the noise. **Choosing the reading that tidies
it away would be the error.**

## AND A CITATION MOVED

Her baptism was cited to **`3Q9M-CSD3-13XB-R`**, an ark taken from machine-parsed text. That page's own
extract reads «*Angelis Porrino … die 2 Septembris **1793** … Rosa Falco … **1808***» — **three dates
that cannot all belong to one 1786 act.** The act is on **`13XZ-1`**, now read.

**What is actually on `13XB-R` has not been checked and nothing is claimed about it.** A Rosa Falco
does appear in its text, in acts of 1793 and 1808; if she is this Rosa Maria — married in 1802, alive
until 1830 — she could be standing as a godmother. **That is a guess and is recorded as one.**
