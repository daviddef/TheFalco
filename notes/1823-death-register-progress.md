## «GIUSEPPE FALCO FU MATTEO» — image 20 (right), 20 June 1823

> …sono comparsi **CESARE GIGLIO**, di anni **cinquanta**, di professione **Contadino**, regnicolo,
> domiciliato in questo comune, **strada Camellara**, *vicino della defunta*, e **GIUSEPPE FALCO
> FU MATTEO**, di anni **quarantatre**, di professione **Contadino**, regnicolo, domiciliato
> **strada Camellara** vicino della defunta … è morta nella sua propria casa **MARIA CRISCI, di
> anni tre**, … **figlia di MARCELLO CRISCI**, di professione contadino, **e di MARIA ROSA MAURO**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-fu-matteo-1823.jpg`

**This is the first record in the CIVIL series to state Giuseppe Falco's parentage outright.** The
clerk writes *«fu Matteo»* simply to tell him from the town's other Giuseppe Falco — and in doing so
confirms, from an entirely separate series, what the archive previously had only from the parish
books: **Giuseppe Falco of strada Camellara is a son of the late Matteo Falco**, and so a brother of
Pasquale, Rosa and Angela.

**And it steadies his age.** He is forty-three here, so born about **1780** — which agrees with the
thirty-seven he gives in October 1817. The full run:

**The dead child's father is Marcello Crisci** — the same Marcello Crisci, then twenty-five, who
with Andrea Crisci declared the death of Fortunato Crisci, the two-day-old son of Arcangelo Crisci
and **Anna Falco**, on strada Camellara in January 1820. His own daughter Maria was three.

## Also so far

- **Vincenzo Rivetti**, thirty-two, of strada Monticello de' Cappuccini, «figlio di **Paolo Rivetti**
  e **Francesca delli Paoli**», died 30 January — declared by **Don Giuseppe Rivetti, *sacerdote***,
  and **Antonio Rivetti**, both of that street and both *«parente del defunto»*. **This complicates
  the tidy split** this archive drew after 1821 between a gentry Rivetti house and a labouring one:
  the priest is kin to the *contadini* of Monticello. Worth saying plainly.
- **A second Vincenzo Rivetti**, whose widow Enrichetta Lupo died in February — a different man from
  the one who died in January, who was married to Vittoria Altavilli.
- **A third living Nicola Rivetti**: a *fruttajolo* of strada Terra Murata, married to Maria Rosa
  Giglio.
- **Suor Elena Anna Racca**, eighty, a ***conversa*** — a lay sister — born at Santa Maria a Vico.
