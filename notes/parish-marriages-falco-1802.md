## 1. GIUSEPPE FALCO × ANTONIA DI GUIDA — «Anno Domini 18[0]2, die vero … Augusti»

> …praemissis tribus denunciationibus de more … nec non explorato mutuo consensu per verba de
> praesenti … in Matrimonium consensit … **JOSEPHUM FALCO filium MATTHEI, e FRANCISCAE CRISCI**
> e[t] **ANTONIAM DI GUIDA filiam JOSEPHI e MAGDALENAE PIVERA(?)** … servata forma Ritus Romani,
> praesentibus testibus **Clemente d'Iglio**, **Franc[esc]o Crisci**, **Ioseph…**

### What it settles

**GIUSEPPE FALCO IS DOCUMENTED AS A SON OF MATTEO FALCO AND FRANCESCA CRISCI, IN THE SAME ACT THAT
MARRIES HIM TO ANTONIA DI GUIDA.**

Earlier the same day this archive published, as **probable**, that the Giuseppe Falco of strada
Camellara married to Antonia di Guida was the founder's son — resting on street, trade and period.
**It is now proved**, and with it:

> **The MATTEO FALCO who died at Arienzo on 28 August 1809, aged fifteen months, is the founder's
> own GRANDSON** — the child of Giuseppe and Antonia, given his grandfather's name and buried while
> the grandfather still lived. Not «a child of another household», which is how this archive first
> published him.

### And a new generation on the di Guida side

**ANTONIA DI GUIDA is the daughter of GIUSEPPE DI GUIDA and MADDALENA PIVERA(?)** — two people this
archive has never held. The mother's surname is the least certain word on the page.

## 2. CARLO ANTONIO MORGILLO × ROSA FALCO — the same leaf

> …de nostri licentia in Matrimonium conjunxi … **CAROLUM ANTONIUM MORGILLO filium q[uondam]
> CAROLI ANTONII, et EMILIAE …** e **ROSA FALCO filiam MATTHEI, e FRANCISCAE CRISCI** … praesentibus
> testibus **Andrea(?) Crisci** di Nicola, **Andrea Sacchetto**, **Filippo Carmelato** … —
> **Hyacinthus Archip[resbyter] Morgillo**

**Rosa Falco's marriage, which this archive did not have.** Her death of 16 October 1830 named her
parents; this names them again and adds her husband's parents: **Carlo Antonio Morgillo was the son
of a Carlo Antonio already dead, and of Emilia**.

It also disentangles a small confusion: the archive carried «Carlantonio Morgillo» as Rosa's
husband, and there were **two men of that name, father and son**.

---

## Why this matters beyond two marriages

Both of Matteo Falco's own vital events are **proved to lie outside the civil registers**. The
parish books are the only front left on which he can be reached — and this leaf shows they carry
exactly what is needed: **his children named as his children, by name, with their spouses' parents
attached.**

**The founding couple's documented children now stand at five**, and two of them —Giuseppe and Rosa—
are now documented twice over, by marriage as well as by death.
