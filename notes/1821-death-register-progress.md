## No Falco

Seventy-seven acts and **not one Falco** — not deceased, not parent, not spouse, not declarant.
That is the fourth such year of the eight now read (1829, 1835, 1818 and 1821), and it is worth
setting down so nobody reads the volume again.

## But the best lead yet on Chiara Rivetti

### Carmine Rivetti — image 23 (right), 15 September 1821

> …**è morto nella sua propria casa CARMINE RIVETTI di anni cinquantatre, marito di GIUSTINA
> MORGILLO**, nato in Arienzo, di professione **Contadino**, domiciliato ivi **strada Monticello
> de' Cappuccini**, **figlio di NICOLA RIVETTI Defunto**, di professione **Contadino**, domiciliato
> ivi, **e di ANNA DI RUGGIO Defunta**, domiciliata ivi.

Plate: `site/public/plates/carmine-rivetti-death-1821.jpg`

**Chiara Rivetti**, who married Pasquale Falco, is *«filia quondam **NICOLAI** et **ANNA**»* in her
1843 Latin burial entry — the only record that names her parents — and was born about 1763. **Carmine
Rivetti was born about 1768.** Same father's forename, same mother's forename, the same generation,
and — this is the part that matters — **the same station**. Nicola Rivetti here is a *contadino*
and Anna di Ruggio a labourer's wife.

**This competes with, and fits better than, the 1816 lead.** That one had **Donna Francesca
Rivetti**, *«figlia del fu **Don Nicola**, e **Donn'Anna Quotolo**»* — gentry. Chiara married a
*contadino* and lived on strada Camellara among day-labourers. A house that married one daughter to
a *proprietario* is unlikely to have married another to Pasquale Falco.

**Neither is established**, and the archive now says so on the open-questions page, with both
candidates set out. If the *contadino* couple is the right one, **Chiara Rivetti's mother was ANNA
DI RUGGIO**. What would settle it is the Rivetti baptisms, or Chiara's own marriage record.

## A trap: a third Carlantonio Morgillo

Image 9 (right), 13 April: **Maria Giuseppa Morgillo**, ten, *«figlia di **CARLANTONIO MORGILLO**,
di professione Contadino… **e di ANNA ANIGALE**»*, of **strada Capodiuomo**.

Checked at full resolution because a daughter of that name and age could have been Rosa Falco's.
**She is not.** The mother is Anna Anigale. So Arienzo held **three** Carlantonio Morgillo in these
years:

| | street | wife | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| the elder | — | **Manilia Rivetti** | dead by August 1818 |
| **Rosa Falco's husband** | **strada Maddalena** | **ROSA FALCO** | 33 in 1817 and 1818, 35 in 1819 |
| a third | **strada Capodiuomo** | **Anna Anigale** | daughter Maria Giuseppa d. 1821 |

## Also worth having

- **A death in the royal prison.** Image 15 (left), 20 June: **Michele Amoriello**, twenty-two, a
  *calzolajo* born at Moiano, died **«nelle Regie Prigioni»** of Arienzo — declared by **Luigi
  Alfonso, forty-eight, *custode delle Regie Prigioni***. In October the keeper's own daughter dies
  and he is named again, as **Luigi Zeppariello, custode delle Regie Prigioni**, of strada
  Sant'Agostino. Arienzo had a gaol and this archive did not know it.
- **The Ospedale di Ave Grazia Plena** takes six of the year's dead, always declared by the same
  pair — Matteo Migliore and Domenico Migliore, *calzolai* of San Felice, who evidently did this
  duty for the hospital.
- **Another di Guida line.** Image 6 (left): **Carmina de Guida**, sixty, daughter of **Agostino di
  Guida**, *contadino* — a branch distinct from the Giuseppe di Guida of strada Maddalena.
- **Arcangelo Merenna and Teresa Rotondo** appear again (image 13 right), the couple behind the
  1818 «Merenna, not Annecchino» near-miss. The name keeps its distance from this family.
- **Two more Morgillo–Rivetti ties**: Carmine Rivetti married **Giustina Morgillo**, and Teresa
  Saccone was the widow of a **Fedele Morgillo**.

## A limitation, stated

**Images 34 (right) and 35 (left) are badly overexposed** in the scan — the ink is washed out and
neither act could be read in full. Both are of strada Camellara and strada Porta di Sopra
respectively. Neither shows a Falco in what is legible, but they are the only two acts of this
volume this archive cannot vouch for. They would need re-photographing, or the second original.
