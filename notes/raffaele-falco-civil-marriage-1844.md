# FOUND — generation four's CIVIL marriage act, at San Felice a Cancello

**13 September 2026.** Read from the image: **`an_ua50256`, image 13, act 12** — San Felice a Cancello,
*Matrimoni 1844*.

## The act

> «L'anno mille ottocento **quarantaquattro**, il dì **SETTE del mese di MARZO**, … avanti di Noi
> **Domenico Bergamo secondo eletto** ed uffiziale dello Stato Civile del comune di **SAN FELICE**,
> distretto di Caserta, Provincia di Terra di Lavoro, sono comparsi nella Casa Comunale
>
> **RAFFAELE FALCO** di anni **ventisei**, nato in **ARIENZO CORPO**, di professione **contadino**,
> domiciliato **strada CAMMELLARA**, **figlio di VINCENZO FALCO** di professione **simile**,
> domiciliato **ivi**, **e di ANDREANA CRISCI** di professione **filatrice**, domiciliata **ivi**;
>
> e **ANGELA ROSA MORGILLO** di anni **ventisei**, di professione **contadina**, **nata in questo
> Comune**, domiciliata in detto comune, **figlia di ORAZIO MORGILLO** di professione **colono**,
> domiciliato in detto comune, **e di VINCENZA PESCE** di professione **contadina**…»

And in the facing **INDICAZIONE**: «*il dì **NOVE** del mese di **Marzo**. Il Parroco di … ci ha
rimessa una delle copie della controscritta promessa; in piè della quale ha certificato, che la
celebrazione del matrimonio **è seguita nel giorno NOVE del mese di MARZO anno corrente**…*»

## What it gives that nothing else did

- **VINCENZO FALCO WAS ALIVE ON 7 MARCH 1844** — no *defunto*, «domiciliato **ivi**», on strada
  Cammellara with his son. **A new latest-date for generation three**, who had been traced only to his
  1792 baptism and his October 1814 marriage.
- **ANDREANA CRISCI was alive too**, a *filatrice* — a spinner — on the same street.
- **ORAZIO MORGILLO and VINCENZA PESCE were both alive**, at San Felice, he a *colono*.
- **Both spouses are twenty-six**, which fits: he was baptised 10 May 1818, she born 24 February 1818.
- **«ARIENZO CORPO»** — written by a **San Felice** clerk who had no reason to copy an Arienzo habit.
  See the correction of the same day: *Corpo d'Arienzo* is Arienzo.

## IT ALSO CORRECTS WHAT THIS ARCHIVE PUBLISHED THIS MORNING

Sixty-four Arienzo marriage acts of 1843 and 1844 were read out today and the conclusion published:
**«generation four's civil marriage act is not where it must be»**, with San Felice a Cancello listed
as untested possibility number one.

**It was there.** The rule turns out to be the ordinary one — **the civil promise is made in the
BRIDE'S comune**, and the bride was born and still living at San Felice. The Arienzo sweep was not
wasted (it is what proved the act was not at Arienzo, and it produced the Majone household and six
Camellara households), but **the reasoning that made Arienzo the obvious place was wrong**, and it was
wrong because this archive assumed the groom's town.

## AND A FOUR-DAY DISCREPANCY, PUBLISHED RATHER THAN TIDIED

- The **parish act** gives the marriage as **5 March 1844**.
- The **civil indicazione** certifies the celebration as **9 March 1844**.

**Both are read from images and they do not agree.** This is the second such gap the archive has found
— the first Gioacchino's death is «11 Decembris» in a baptismal margin and 14 December in the burial
register. **No attempt is made here to choose.** Either the parish day was misread, or the two books
recorded different moments (the celebration and its registration), or one clerk wrote from memory.

## AND A CLAIM NOW IN DOUBT

`/which-falco` states that **Sant'Agnese is one of Arienzo's three parishes**, and that generation four
married there because the bride was the priest's own «*mea Parochiana*».

**The civil act says the bride was born at San Felice and still domiciled there in March 1844.** If she
was the priest's parishioner, **his parish is more likely to be at San Felice than at Arienzo.** The
parish name in the *indicazione* runs over two lines and **was not read with confidence** — so nothing
is asserted, and the Arienzo claim is flagged rather than corrected. **The test is the parish act
itself, re-read for the name of the church.**
