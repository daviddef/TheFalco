# Carminantonio Falco's birth act, 1901 (generation seven)

**Found 13 November 2026**, in the full walk of the Arienzo civil registers: MC55-RM9, image 47, *Nascite 1901 Parte I*, **act 8**.

- Declared 13 January 1901, 11.40 a.m., by **Raffaele Falco**, 27, contadino, of via Camellara (number blank): at 6.30 in the evening of **11 January 1901**, «da **Costanza Maione** sua moglie legittima» a son, **Carminantonio**. Witnesses Francesco Maione (57) and Gennaro Carfora (38), contadini; nobody signed. Read at level 13 (z877, z878, z879).
- It is the birth act of the Carminantonio Falco whose Italian passport (Questura di Benevento, 5 August 1976) gives «Arienzo (CE), 11/1/1901», the husband of Filomena Annecchino of Forchia. Day and town agree; the name is the paternal grandfather's (Carminantonio, husband of Giovanna Arricale); the parents are the couple married at Arienzo on 26 September 1897 (Matrimoni 1897 act 21), whose house already held his sister Giovanna (1898) and his sister Anna (1904).
- Recorded as a birth row in `raffaele-falco-costanza-maione` and joined to his own house by `person-joins.json` (child-head), with the passport as the second tie. This is the first document that names both his parents. It is the generation-six to generation-seven link that the tree alone carried.
- The act names no later event; no margin note was seen. His marriage (1920s, Forchia or Arpaia) is still to be read.
- Read, not placed: the other Falco cell in the first 12 acts of the register is none. Near-names not counted: Maione (the register's spelling; the Atlas catalogues Majone and Maione), Carfora, Arricale.
