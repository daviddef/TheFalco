# Arienzo civil death register 1818 — sweep complete

**Volume:** `an_ua14320` (Arienzo, Morti 1818), 55 images. **Read in full.**
**Acts:** roughly 80, running 1 January – 19 December 1818. Images 54R–55 are blank forms.
**Officer:** **Andrea Sorice, Sindaco**, with **Salvatore Scarpati, secondo eletto** deputising in May.
**Forms:** written on **1817-printed forms** — as 1817 was written on 1816 forms. The clerk strikes
the printed year and writes the current one above.
**Crop that works:** left `8,19,46,27`, right `50,19,46,27`, width ~950. **Taller than the 1817
band** — this clerk sets the act lower on the leaf, and the shorter band clips the line carrying the
parents and the spouse.
**Duplicate bindings:** 22≡21, 34≡33, 36≡35, 51≡50.

## The headline: no Falco died in Arienzo in 1818

Eighty acts, and not one Falco among the dead. But **Giuseppe Falco of strada Camellara appears
again**, and the volume turns out to carry a good deal else that this family needs.

## Giuseppe Falco, and the sighting that proves he is one man

### Image 24 (left), 8 May 1818

> …sono comparsi **DOMENICO BALLETTA**, di anni **quaranta**, di professione **sartore**,
> domiciliato in questo comune, **strada Camellara**, e di **GIUSEPPE FALCO**, di anni
> **trentasei**, di professione **contadino**, domiciliato in questo comune, **strada Camellara**,
> *vicini*…

Plate: `site/public/plates/giuseppe-falco-declares-1818.jpg`

**Four weeks later**, on 7 June 1818, the birth act of his daughter Maddalena gives him as
**thirty-six, contadino, of strada Camellara**, husband of Antonia di Guida. Same age, same trade,
same street, one month apart. That settles what four earlier sightings left open:

| Sighting | Age | Trade | Street | Implied birth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deaths 1816, act 76 (1 Dec) | 32 | massaro di campagna | Camellara | c. 1784 |
| Deaths 1817, img 60R (24 Oct) | 37 | massaro di campagna | Camellara | c. 1780 |
| Deaths 1817, img 61L (28 Oct) | 37 | massaro di campagna | Camellara | c. 1780 |
| **Deaths 1818, img 24L (8 May)** | **36** | **contadino** | **Camellara** | **c. 1782** |
| Births 1818, act 57 (7 Jun) | 36 | contadino | Camellara | c. 1782 |

**Domenico Balletta is his neighbour in October 1817 and again in May 1818.** The trade changes from
*massaro di campagna* to *contadino* between the two — a real drop in standing, or a change of
clerk. The archive treats these as one man and says so.

## What else the volume gave

### A second Carlantonio Morgillo — image 38 (right), 3 August 1818

> …sono comparsi **CARLANTONIO MORGILLO**, di anni **trentatre**, *contadino*, domiciliato in
> questo comune, **strada Maddalena**, e di **MATTEO MIGLIORE**, di anni quaranta, *calzolajo*,
> domiciliato nel **comune di San Felice**… *vicino, parente* … **GIUDITTA MORGILLO** *figlia delli
> fu* **CARLANTONIO MORGILLO**, *e* **MANILIA RIVETTI**, ***vedova di Giuseppe Gimbardo***.

Plate: `site/public/plates/carlantonio-morgillo-1818.jpg`

The declarant is **Rosa Falco's husband** — Pasquale Falco's brother-in-law. The dead woman's father
is **a different, older Carlantonio Morgillo**, already dead. Two men of the name, one generation
apart, and this archive nearly had only one.

**A lead:** the declarant is recorded as *parente* of Giuditta. If he is her brother, this act names
**his own parents — Carlantonio Morgillo the elder and Manilia Rivetti.** The register does not say
how they were related, so it is not asserted.

**And it tightens his age**, which this archive has flagged as unreliable: thirty in February 1816,
thirty-six in October 1816, **thirty-three in September 1817, thirty-three in August 1818**. The two
later readings agree on a birth about **1784–85**.

### Orazio Morgillo — image 31 (right), 6 July 1818

> …sono comparsi **ANGELO MORGILLO fu SIMONE**, di anni **cinquanta**, *contadino*, **strada
> Travo**, e di **ORAZIO MORGILLO**, di anni **ventisei**, *contadino*, strada Travo, *vicino* …
> **PASQUALE MORGILLO** *figlio di* **GIUSEPPE MORGILLO**, *e* **GELSOMINA RIVETTI**.

Plate: `site/public/plates/orazio-morgillo-1818.jpg`

Generation four of this line married **Angela Rosa Morgillo, daughter of an Orazio**. This Orazio
Morgillo, twenty-six in 1818, was born about 1792 — the right age to have fathered a daughter in
February 1818, and **the first Orazio Morgillo this archive has found in any record**. It is a
**lead and not a link**: Angela Rosa was born at **San Felice**, not Arienzo.

### Don Giuseppe Rivetti, priest — image 47 (right), 6 October 1818

> …sono comparsi **D. GIUSEPPE RIVETTI**, di anni **quarantuno**, di professione **SACERDOTE**,
> domiciliato in questo comune, **strada Sant'Andrea**…

With **Donna Francesca Rivetti** of January 1816 — daughter of *Don* Nicola Rivetti and *Donn'*Anna
Quotolo — this is the second sign of a **Rivetti branch of standing** at Arienzo.

**But it cuts both ways, and the archive should say so.** These three volumes now show Rivetti as
*bracciali* on strada Camellara, *vaticali* on strada Sant'Andrea, a *Don*, and a priest. The
surname spans the whole social range of the town. That makes the inference behind the standing
question *«Was Chiara Rivetti gentry?»* — which rests on nothing but the forenames Nicola and Anna —
**weaker, not stronger**.

### Three generations of Rivetti, and a fourth Morgillo–Rivetti marriage

- **Paolo Rivetti**, d. 25 March 1818, *figlio del fu* **Giuseppe Rivetti** *e* **Andreana Sossi** —
  and the 1817 volume names **Maria Rivetti, daughter of Paolo Rivetti and Caterina Madonna**.
  So: Giuseppe & Andreana → Paolo → Maria, with the declarants «vicini e parenti» on **Camellara**.
- **Marcello Rivetti**, d. 7 September, son of **Giuseppe Rivetti** and **Lucia Silvestro**.
- **Antonio Rivetti** (48) and **Carlo Rivetti** (57), *contadini* of strada Sajanella.
- **Nunzio Rivetti** (35, *bracciale*, **strada Camellara**), **Raffaele Rivetti** (22, then 28),
  **Girolama Rivetti**, **Manilia Rivetti**, **Gelsomina Rivetti**, **Antonia Crisci** married to a
  Balletta.
- **Nicola Morgillo, 30, *cavajolo*, of strada Camellara** (image 19 right, January) — direct proof
  that the Morgillo were on the family's street before they were in-laws.

**A caution on Rivetti ages.** Salvatore Rivetti is forty-four in September 1817 and thirty in
September 1818. Francesco Rivetti is thirty-five in April 1817, thirty in September 1817 and
forty-seven in September 1818. These are not usable without a second record.

## Traps checked and cleared

- **«Felice Angela MERENNA»** (image 37 right) is **not** an Annecchino. Enlarged: a clear capital
  M and «erenna». Logged so nobody counts her.
- **«vedova di Francesco Fat—»** (image 18 left) is **not** a Falco: the double-t rules it out.
  Almost certainly Fattorusso, a name already in these registers.

## A parallel that supports the Raffaele argument

**Colomba Laudato**, daughter of Aniello Laudato and Maria Martone, died 20 July 1818. A **Colomba
Laudato, daughter of Aniello Laudato and Marta Martone**, died 1 March 1817. Same parents, same
name, seventeen months apart — an independent Arienzo example of the naming custom that settled
generation four of this line: a family reused a name only after the first child had died.

## A human thread

**Sabato Vassallo**, the *sportellaro* who stood with Giuseppe Falco to declare the death of the
infant Raffaele Falco on 28 October 1817, was himself buried on **20 March 1818**, aged about
twenty-seven, son of Francesco Vassallo and Gelsomina Ruggiero. His own son **Marco Vassallo**
followed him on 17 July.

## Two originals — read both

Images **3–13** of this volume are a **second original** of the twenty-two acts the 1817 volume
carries at its images 78–88. Two clerks, and they disagree in detail. That caught a mistake in this
archive's own reading: the declarant at Maria Rivetti's death on 1 January is **Nunzio Rivetti, 35,
of strada Camellara**, not «Luigi Rivetti, 75, of strada Annunziata». Where an act exists in both
originals, read both and prefer the clearer hand.

## What this closes

**Arienzo deaths 1816, 1817 and 1818 are now read in full, act by act.** Across the three years the
Falco dead are: none in 1816, **Maria Falco** and the infant **Raffaele Falco** in 1817, none in
1818. The living Falco who appear as declarants or spouses are **Gioacchino**, **Giuseppe** (five
times), **Angela**, and the two Maria Falco named as mothers.

Still to read: the Arienzo death registers for **1819–1828** and **1837–1843**, the birth registers
before 1816 and after 1818, and the *processetti*.
