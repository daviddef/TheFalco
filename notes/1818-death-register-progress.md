They carry the same twenty-two acts, 1 January – 27 February 1818, that `an_ua14319` carries at its
images 78–88. Two clerks, two copies, and they **do not agree in detail** — see the section «Two

the declarant at Maria Rivetti's death on 1 January is **Nunzio Rivetti, 35, bracciale, of strada

## What images 14–19 have already given

**Three generations of Rivetti, on strada Camellara.** Image 19 left names **Paolo Rivetti**, son of
the late **Giuseppe Rivetti** and **Andreana Sossi** — and the 1817 volume names **Maria Rivetti,
daughter of Paolo Rivetti and Caterina Madonna**. So: Giuseppe & Andreana → Paolo → Maria, with the
declarants of Paolo's death «vicini e parenti» and living on Camellara. Chiara Rivetti, who married
Pasquale Falco, is not yet joined to any of them, and nothing here joins her.

**Nicola Morgillo and Giuseppe Rivetti declare together, both of strada Camellara** (image 19
right). The Morgillo and the Rivetti — the two families the Falco married — are neighbours on the
Falco street in 1818.

**A trap checked and cleared.** Image 18 left ends «vedova di Francesco Fat—», the surname running
into the gutter. Enlarged, the double-t rules out Falco; it is almost certainly Fattorusso, a name
already in these registers.
