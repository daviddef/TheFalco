# Giovanna Falco (b. 1891) and Angelo Morgillo, 16 January 1913

Status 15 Nov 2026: **the marriage act itself is not reachable online; what we hold is one margin.**

## What is held
- Nascite 1891 act 60 (MC55-VNL leaf 1647 right, level 13, z540): Giovanna, born 11 May 1891 at via Orticelli 1, daughter of Antonio Falco and Carmela Guida. Later hand in the right margin: «Con atto 16-1-913 N° [number not settled] in Arienzo la Falco sposò Angelo Morgillo». A stamped line below gives her death at Arienzo on 12-11-61 (noted at Santa Maria Capua Vetere 6-2-62). Both are the margin's statements; neither act has been opened. Already in the household `antonio-falco-carmela-guida`.
- The other Giovanna Falco in the registers (Nascite 1898 act 79, daughter of Raffaele Falco and Costanza Maione) was 14 in 1913 and is not the bride.

## Where the 1913 act is NOT (holdings checked 15 Nov 2026)

- FamilySearch collection 2043630 (Arienzo civil registration, Tribunale): ends 1910 (MC55-FNT, MC55-VNL, MC55-RM9, plus the Allegati volumes MC55-BM9 to 1904 and MC5R-9WL 1905-1910). Record Atlas lists no Arienzo civil volume after 1910.
- So Pubblicazioni 1912, Matrimoni 1913 and any 1913 allegati are not online in either place. This is a holdings finding, not a search nil: no register of 1913 was opened.

## Who Angelo Morgillo is: not settled
Angelo Morgillo appears several times in the 1866-1910 registers and none is tied to Giovanna: a colono of San Felice a Cancello, son of Giovanni and Rosa Cerbagno, who married a Guida in May 1907 (Pubblicazioni 1907 act 12); a commerciante of Santa Maria a Vico, son of Vincenzo, who married a Rivetti in November 1902 (Matrimoni 1902 act 21); a father of a Morgillo child of 1903 (Nascite act 38) and of 1900 (act 90); a boy born 17 June 1897 (Nascite 1897 act 52), who was fifteen in 1913. No margin noted in the walk names a Giovanna Falco beside any Morgillo, so the groom's own birth is probably outside the Arienzo registers read, or his margin was not recorded. Do not place any of them on the name.
