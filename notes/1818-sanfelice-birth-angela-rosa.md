# ANGELA ROSA MORGILLO'S BIRTH ACT — found at San Felice a Cancello

**Volume**: San Felice a Cancello, Nati 1818 — `an_ua50395`, **image 55 (left)**, atto 32, foglio 32
**Read**: 13 September 2026, at native resolution (3469 px)
**Why it matters**: generation four's wife, and the first primary record of her parents

> L'anno mille ottocento **diciotto** a' dì **ventiquattro** del mese di **Febrajo** ad ore tredici,
> avanti di noi **Vito Ferrari Sindaco** ed ufiziale dello Stato Civile del Comune di **sei Casali
> d'Arienzo**, Provincia di Terra di Lavoro — è comparso **ORAZIO MORGILLO** di anni **ventisei**,
> di professione **bracciale**, domiciliato in detto Comune **Massaria delle Fosse**, ed ha
> dichiarato che nel detto dì ad ora **sette** è nato **una bambina** nella sua propria casa di esso
> dichiarante, e da **VINCENZA PESCE** d'anni **ventisette**, di professione **filatrice**, sua
> legittima moglie, che ci ha presentato … **a cui si è dato il nome di ANGIOLA ROSA**.

## What it establishes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Angela Rosa Morgillo** | born **24 February 1818 at seven in the morning**, at the **Massaria delle Fosse**, San Felice a Cancello. Named **ANGIOLA ROSA** at the font. |
| **Orazio Morgillo** | **26** in Feb 1818, so born about **1792**. A ***bracciale*** — a day-labourer. |
| **Vincenza Pesce** | **27**, a ***filatrice***. Born about **1791** — **the first date this archive has ever had for her.** |

**Orazio Morgillo and Vincenza Pesce were carried on the family tree and nowhere else.** They are
now in a register, with ages, trades and an address. A *massaria* is a farmstead, not a street:
Angela Rosa was born on a farm, in her father's own house.

## How it was found, and the lesson

The search began with **San Felice's marriages for 1817** (`an_ua50229`), read act by act — and
**failed**: no Orazio Morgillo, no Vincenza Pesce. The birth act reached the same couple anyway.

> **A birth act names both parents outright. A marriage act has to be found first.**
> When the target is a couple and a child's date is known, go to the birth register.

## Two things that nearly stopped this

1. **The town.** San Felice a Cancello is catalogued on Antenati as «San Felice (oggi San Felice a
   Cancello)», and this archive had recorded its registers as *"none of which this archive has
   read"* — which was heard as *cannot*. See `notes/antenati-holdings-discovery.md`.
2. **The volume.** `an_ua50395` is catalogued as **Nati 1818** but is a COMPOSITE: images 1–39 are
   the **marriages** of 1818, images ~40–63 the **births**. Worse, the birth forms are **pre-printed
   for 1816** — every act reads «L'anno mille ottocento *sedici*» with «**diciotto**» written above
   in the clerk's hand. Read at survey resolution the year looks like 1816 and the volume looks like
   the wrong one. **It is not. Check for the interlinear correction.**

## The caution

An **Orazio Morgillo, aged twenty-six, of strada Travo at ARIENZO** declares a death there on
**6 July 1818** — same name, same age, different town, five months later
(`notes/1818-death-register-progress.md`). One man holding land in both places, or two men of an
age. **Not decided.** A death act for either would settle it by naming his parents.

## Still open

- **Orazio Morgillo × Vincenza Pesce's marriage**, c.1816–17. Not in San Felice 1817
  (`an_ua50229`, read in full). **Next: San Felice Matrimoni 1816 = `an_ua50228`.**
- **Vincenza Pesce's parents.** The 1817 sweep found a **GIOVANNA PESCA, 48, «figlia delli furono
  FELICE PESCE ed ANTONIA CERRONE»** and a **VENERANDA PESCE**, ~50. If Vincenza (b. c.1791) is
  Giovanna's sister, her parents are Felice Pesce and Antonia Cerrone. **A lead, not a claim.**
- **The Massaria delle Fosse** — a named farm at San Felice. Findable on the ground.
