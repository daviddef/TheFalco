## FALCO section

Four acts with a Falco child and father; three acts with a Falco witness; one margin.

**Act 19, 26 Feb 1850 (image 27R + 28L), role CHILD and FATHER/DECLARANT.** Vincenzo Falco, b. 26 Feb ore dieci, son of Angelo Falco, 26, contadino, strada Croce (declarant, signs «Angelo») and Maria Cimmino, 26, contadina. Witnesses Antonio Sanzone 50 and Fabrizio Crisci 53 (cantiniere). Verbatim in falco_notes.txt (3000 px). Margin: «A 30 Dicembre 1874 l'uff. dello stato civile d'Arienzo ... Vincenzo Falco con Angela Pettone? ... Caserta 31 Luglio 1884».

**Act 22, 5 March 1850 (30R + 31L), CHILD and FATHER/DECLARANT.** Giovanni Falco, b. 5 Mar ore dieci, son of Crescenzo Falco, 30, contadino, strada Camellara (declarant) and Maria Morgillo, 28. Witnesses Angelo Anzovino 41 and Gaetano Sanzone 50. Margin: «A 23 Settembre 1877 ... Giovanni Falco con Giuseppa Martone — Caserta 17 Giugno 1882». Verbatim in falco_notes.txt.

**Act 34, 22 April 1850 (42R + 43L), WITNESS.** Crescenzo Falco, 30, contadino regnicolo, strada Camellara, second witness to Angelo Crisci (father Matteo Crisci, declarant, strada Capodiconca; mother Giuseppa Pipitelli); first witness Bartolomeo Gimbardo 32. Read at 3000 and 3400 px.

**Act 52, 11 July 1850 (60R + 61L), CHILD and FATHER.** Carmine Antonio Falco, b. 10 July, son of Raffaele Falco, 28, contadino, strada Camellara, and Angela Vo.. Morgillo, 25 (tavola: Morgillo Maria Rosa); declarant the midwife Felicia Martone; witnesses Angelo Anzovino 41 and Pasquale Montefuschio 52. Margin: «A 18 Novembre 1872 ... Carmine Falco con Libera Arricale (Arrigale?) — Caserta 5 Giugno 1884». Verbatim in falco_notes.txt.

**Act 63, 31 July 1850 (71R + 72L), MARGIN.** Giuseppa Martone, b. 30 July, father Antonio Martone 32 (strada Camellara), mother Crescenza Verdicchio 40; margin (read at 3400 px): «A 23 Settembre 1877 l'uff. dello stato civile d'Arienzo ... Giuseppa Martone con Giovanni Falco — Caserta 17 Giugno 1882», the same day and Caserta date as the margin of act 22: Giovanni Falco (act 22) married Giuseppa Martone on 23 Sept 1877.

**Act 68, 19 Aug 1850 (76R + 77L), WITNESS.** Raffaele Falco, 28, contadino regnicolo, strada Camellara (read at 3400 px), second witness to Emanuela Arrigale (father Clemente Arrigale 24, declarant; mother Crescenza Pettone? 22); first witness Lorenzo Crisci 21. Same man as the father in act 52.

**Act 90, 19 Nov 1850 (101R + 102L), CHILD and FATHER.** Antonia Falco, b. 19 Nov ore dodici, daughter of Michele Falco, 46, contadino, strada Camellara, and Giovanna d'Ambrosio, 34; declarant the midwife Felicia Martone 76; witnesses Matteo Crisci 50 and Clemente Crisci 40 (both «ministeriale»). Margin: «A 26 Aprile 1875 ... Antonia Falco con Antonio Zimbardo — Caserta 23 Luglio 1884». Verbatim in falco_notes.txt.

**Act 92, 21 Nov 1850 (103R + 104L), WITNESS.** Crescenzo Falco (written «Cre?enzo Falco», same hand and wording as act 34), 30, contadino regnicolo, strada Camellara, second witness to Carmine Porrino (father Luca Porrino 36, declarant, mother Maddalena Crisci 25); first witness Pasquale Crisci 43? Read at 3400 px against act 34.

Falco men of strada Camellara in 1850 as the volume gives them: Angelo (strada Croce, 26), Crescenzo (30), Raffaele (28), Michele (46). No Falco midwife, mother or declarant.

### Limitations
- Whole-spread reads at 2200 px; only the acts above and the doubtful lines were rendered at 3000-3400 px. Many witness ages and street names carry `?`; surnames of a few poor lines (acts 30, 55, 77, 85 witnesses) are uncertain but none looks like Falco.
- Margin notes were read for every act; none other names a Falco.
- The volume has no closing certificate in the images (117 ends in blank forms).
