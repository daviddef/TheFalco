# RAFFAELA FALCO — a daughter of the founder's son, and a Falco who married a Falco

**Arienzo, Morti 1854 (`an_ua14356`), atto 34**, image 24, read from the image, 13 September 2026.

> «…che nel giorno **venti** del mese di **Aprile** anno corrente, alle ore **undici**, è morta nella
> sua propria casa **RAFFAELA FALCO, MOGLIE DI FRANCESCO FALCO**, nata in **Arienzo**, di anni
> **TRENTAQUATTRO**, di professione **contadina**, domiciliata **STRADA CAMELLARA**, **figlia di
> GIUSEPPE FALCO** di professione **contadino**, domiciliato ivi, **e di ANTONIA GUIDA**, domiciliata
> ivi.»

## Four things at once

**1. A DAUGHTER OF GIUSEPPE FALCO AND ANTONIA DI GUIDA that this archive did not have.** Born about
**1820**, died **20 April 1854** at thirty-four. Their known children are now four: the infant
**Matteo** (d. 1809), the infant **Raffaele** (a parish burial of 26 February, year unread),
**DON GIOVANNI the deacon** (d. 1849), and **RAFFAELA**.

**2. GIUSEPPE FALCO WAS STILL ALIVE IN APRIL 1854** — «figlia **di** Giuseppe Falco», no *fu*, no
*defunto*, and a trade written out. He was born about 1780 and married in 1802, so he was about
**seventy-four**. That is now the latest sighting of the founder's son, five years later than the
one in Don Giovanni's act.

**3. STRADA CAMELLARA.** She died on it. Four generations of this family are now documented there.

**4. A FALCO MARRIED A FALCO, INSIDE THE FOUNDER'S OWN FAMILY.** Raffaela's husband is **FRANCESCO
FALCO**. This is not an isolated curiosity: the 1850 index carries **Falco Angelantonio «di Falco
Domenico e Falco Mariantonia»** and **Falco Antonio «di Falco Francesco e Falco Giuseppa»**, and the
1853 index carries **Falco Clemente «di Falco Emmanuele e Falco Giuseppa»**. **Four Falco–Falco
marriages at Arienzo in four years.** Either the surname is common enough there to be two or more
unrelated stocks — which the *Don* and *Donna* Falco of 1850 already suggest — or the family married
cousins. **The archive does not know which, and does not guess.**

## AND THE PARISH REGISTER JOINS ON

From the Arienzo parish death entries, found in the same sweep that opened the marriage collection:

> «Anno Domini **1855**, die vero **14 Augusti**, obiit **CLEMENS FALCO filius FRANCISCI, et
> RAPHAELAE FALCO**, aetatis suae **annorum QUINQUE**, cujus cadaver in coemeterio hujus
> Archipresbyteralis Ecclesiae humatum est.»

**That is their son.** Clemente Falco, born about **1850**, **died 14 August 1855 aged five** — fifteen
months after his mother. A civil act and a parish entry, found by different methods on different
days, reconstructing one household:

> **FRANCESCO FALCO × RAFFAELA FALCO** *(d. 20 Apr 1854, strada Camellara)*
> └── **CLEMENTE FALCO**, b. c.1850, d. 14 Aug 1855

*The 1853 index's «Falco Clemente, di Falco Emmanuele e Falco Giuseppa» is a **different** child of a
**different** couple. Checked before it was joined to this one.*

## Method note — the index format changes from year to year

- **1844–1850**: alphabetical *tavola*, by surname then forename, in a **separate volume** to 1849 and
  **bound into the register** from 1850.
- **1851–1853**: bound in, still alphabetical.
- **1854**: **NOT alphabetical.** A numbered list **by act number**, each line giving the deceased and
  **both parents** — «34 = Raffaele[a] Falco di Giuseppe e Antonia Guida». Faster to read than an
  alphabetical table and more informative, but it must be read end to end.
- **1855**: the *tavola* is not at images 2–3; the layout changed again.

**Never assume last year's layout.** Each volume's front matter has to be looked at before it is read.

## Also in these years

- **1851**: one Falco only — **Gioacchino, atto 7** (the known act). **No Giuseppe Falco.**
- **1852**: **no Falco at all** — the index runs Diglio → Ferro → Guida.
- **1853**: **Falco Clemente, atto 46**, «di Falco Emmanuele e Falco Giuseppa».
