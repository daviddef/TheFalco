# Carmine Falco's Italian military service — where the papers would be (17 Oct 2026)

Status: **the register is FOUND (17 Oct 2026, see the top section); no copy has been requested.** Compiled from a research pass whose reading of the Archivio di
Stato di Benevento (ASBN) and several Defence sites was **through web-archive snapshots only** — those hosts refused live
connections from this machine — so «reported online» below means exactly that, not tested.

The man: born Forchia (BN) 8 June 1930 (Arpaia marriage extract), class (leva) **1930**, so called up about 1950–51 *if* he served in
the Italian army. The only evidence that he served at all is the family's undated photograph.

## The best lead — ASBN's own online index of the ruoli matricolari
- The ASBN holds *Esiti di leva* (classes 1870–1934) and *Ruoli matricolari* (classes 1874–1931) per the Antenati page for that
  archive; two other snippets gave different ranges (1874–1928; «1867–1943») and **were not read at their sources**.
- Its online «Indice onomastico» is searchable by cognome, nome, classe, luogo di nascita; the dropdowns contain FALCO and
  FORCHIA. **Not run** (the site was unreachable from here). **Someone should run it: FALCO, Carmine, classe 1930, Forchia.**
- Ruoli pass to the Archivio di Stato 70 years after enrolment; a class of 1930 would have reached it about 2020.

## The fuller file — the foglio matricolare, at the Army
For a man in the ranks the request goes to the Army's document office for the place of birth: **Reparto Attività Territoriali,
Caserta** (covers Caserta and Benevento) or **Ufficio Documentale, Naples** (all Campania since 2020); one shared address,
comtersud@esercito.difesa.it / comtersud@postacert.difesa.it. Needs: the applicant's identity, proof of kinship, and for a
deceased man a death certificate or self-declaration. **Privacy rule as the agent read it (Persomil circular of 2021, varied
7 Aug 2026): subject dead 50 years or less — the heirs' consent plus an atto di notorietà; the family should confirm this with the
office before sending anything.** Carmine died in 2026, so it is that route, and the heirs are living people who must be the ones
to ask: **this archive does not write to them or on their behalf.**
